AI voice bots in collections and sales calls

Announced November 2026

Axis Bank's AI voice bots now handle most of its collection calls, and millions of sales calls. Routine calls are resolved by bots, leaving human agents for the harder cases.

What it is

The figures come from the bank's investor presentation for the September 2026 quarter, filed on 17 October 2026. They cover AI bots working in debt collections and in outbound sales calling.

How it works

The bots speak with customers on collection calls and work to resolve each call without a human agent. The bank also runs AI-led sales calls at scale.

Technology used

AI voice bots (the bank's Voice AI capability); the vendor is not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The bank reported that AI bots handled nearly 60% of collection calls in the September 2026 quarter, with a call-resolution rate of about 50%, and that it had made more than 2.5 million AI-led sales calls (as reported in the source).

Source (Independent reporting): Banks report 10-50% gains in productivity, turnaround time and costs from AI-led initiatives" (The Economic Times, 1 November 2026)

ADI — Axis Deep Intelligence employee assistant

Announced June 2026

ADI is Axis Bank's generative AI assistant for employees, trained on the bank's policies and public website. Branch and head-office staff can get answers on products and processes without passing queries up the line.

What it is

ADI (Axis Deep Intelligence) is described in the bank's Integrated Annual Report 2025-26. It supports more than 84,000 employees.

How it works

Staff ask ADI questions in plain language and receive answers drawn from the bank's internal policy documents and its public website. It is used most heavily for branch-level queries.

Technology used

Generative AI (the bank's ADI assistant); the underlying model is not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The annual report said ADI resolves more than 81% of branch-level queries, with about 35,000 monthly users handling around 2.8 lakh (280,000) queries a month.

Source (Bank announcement): Integrated Annual Report 2025-26" (Axis Bank, June 2026)

AXIOM AI operating model and "Zero Ops" document processing

Announced April 2026

Axis Bank put its AI work under one operating model, AXIOM, with shared platforms for documents, voice and knowledge. The same AI platforms are reused across the bank instead of each team building its own.

What it is

AXIOM was introduced with the bank's FY26 results in April 2026. Its reusable platforms include VisionXtract for document processing, Voice AI, Knowledge AI and RM Copilot for relationship managers; the bank also reported ISO/IEC 42001 certification for its AI management system.

How it works

Under the bank's "Zero Ops" approach, documents flow through VisionXtract and are processed automatically unless they need a human check. The FY26 annual report recorded more than 115 million AI-led document services and more than 50 credit models.

Technology used

Axis Bank's VisionXtract document AI, Voice AI, Knowledge AI and RM Copilot platforms, under the AXIOM operating model.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The bank said 80–90% of documents under Zero Ops need no human touch (bank comments reported by Financial Express).

Source (Independent reporting): How Axis Bank is using AI to improve productivity while keeping AI spend within its technology budget" (Financial Express) · Second source
