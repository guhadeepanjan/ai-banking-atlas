Private Banking

Wealth Management FX Intelligent Expert — AI video column with LSEG

Announced February 2025 · Went live December 2024

An AI video column that turns the bank's daily foreign exchange research into one-minute videos for wealth clients. Clients get market views as a short video in their own language, not a long written note.

What it is

The Standard Chartered Wealth Management FX Intelligent Expert was announced on 6 February 2025, built in collaboration with LSEG. It debuted for retail clients in mainland China in December 2024, with Singapore, Hong Kong and Taiwan to follow.

How it works

AI summarises the Daily Market Navigator, the market publication of the bank's Global Chief Investment Office. It translates the material where necessary, and automatically converts it into a one-minute video with voiceover and relevant visuals.

Technology used

Artificial intelligence technology from LSEG (London Stock Exchange Group), applied to the bank's Daily Market Navigator publication.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. The bank said the service aims to provide faster time to market, scalability and cost efficiency.

Source (Bank announcement): Standard Chartered Launches Industry-First AI-Powered FX Insights" (Standard Chartered press release, 6 February 2025)

Integrate Falcon TST forecasts into the SCALE liquidity engine

Announced August 2025

Standard Chartered plugged Ant International's Falcon forecasting model into its SCALE engine. The system predicts a client's cash flow and FX exposure in real time, so hedges can be priced and placed around the clock.

What it is

The joint solution links Ant International's Falcon Time-Series Transformer model with SCALE, the bank's Aggregated Liquidity Engine. It serves Ant International and its clients first, as part of the bank's wider FX automation programme.

How it works

Falcon learns patterns from large historical data sets and forecasts cash flow and FX exposure hourly, daily and weekly. SCALE uses those forecasts to price and settle multi-currency bookings with guaranteed FX rates, 24/7.

Technology used

Ant International's Falcon Time-Series Transformer (TST), a transformer model with close to 2 billion parameters, integrated with Standard Chartered's Aggregated Liquidity Engine (SCALE).

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Forecasts run at more than 90% accuracy and now cover over 60% of Ant International's FX conversion transactions. The release reports FX costs cut by up to 60% and liquidity management costs by up to 50%.

Source (Independent reporting): Standard Chartered and Ant International launch AI-powered treasury and FX solution" (The Asian Banker, 25 August 2025)

Alibaba Cloud's AI technology and solutions are applied inside the bank's operations in the named areas, under a strategic technology and growth partnership between the two groups. The release describes a programme of deployment rather than a single product launch.

SC GPT — the bank's own generative AI tool for employees

Announced March 2025

SC GPT is Standard Chartered's own generative AI tool, rolled out to staff in 41 markets. It gives more than 70,000 employees one shared, bank-controlled assistant instead of public AI tools.

What it is

SC GPT is the bank's internal generative AI application, launched on 27 March 2025. It is part of the bank's wider programme to spread AI across its business.

How it works

Employees use SC GPT inside the bank's own systems to help with everyday work. The Asian Banker reported it had more than 15,300 daily users. It also reported that the bank's AI use cases grew from about 200 at the end of the first quarter of 2025 to more than 300 a year later.

Technology used

Standard Chartered's in-house SC GPT generative AI application; the underlying model is not named in the bank's release.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The Asian Banker reported an average productivity uplift of about 6% among SC GPT users.

Source (Bank announcement): Standard Chartered rolls out SC GPT, advancing AI-driven innovation in banking" (Standard Chartered press release, 27 March 2025) · Second source
