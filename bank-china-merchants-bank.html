"AI Xiao Zhao" corporate intelligent assistant

Announced September 2026 (Euromoney profile published 10 September 2026, reporting CMB's 2025 deployment)

CMB's AI-powered assistant Xiao Zhao served corporate customers at scale during 2025. It shows CMB's AI reaching wholesale clients, not only its large retail base.

What it is

An AI-powered assistant for CMB's corporate customers, sitting inside the bank's corporate digital channels, which Euromoney describes as the strongest area of CMB's digital adoption.

How it works

The source gives limited operating detail: it reports the assistant in service alongside corporate online channels that handled 97.72% of financing and 86.62% of foreign-exchange transactions. No further workflow detail is given in the source.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Xiao Zhao "served 267,100 corporate customers," per the source. No separate efficiency or revenue benefit for the assistant is stated.

Source (Independent reporting): "Best digital bank in the Greater Bay Area 2026: China Merchants Bank" — Euromoney, 10 September 2026

AI cuts SME credit processing from 36 hours to under 3 hours

Announced August 2026

CMB uses AI across SME lending, from drafting due-diligence reports to monitoring loans after payout. Average credit-service time fell from 36 hours to 2.72 hours.

What it is

AI applied across CMB's SME and corporate lending workflow, covering due diligence, credit processing and post-loan monitoring, as reported in the bank's 2026 interim results.

How it works

The AI generates around 90% of the content in some due-diligence reports. Employees adopted 68% of the AI-generated findings in post-loan monitoring. Its warnings arrive 45 days earlier than under conventional monitoring processes.

Technology used

China Merchants Bank's own large-model technology system, with 256 domain-specific models deployed across 1,386 AI-enabled scenarios.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Average SME credit-service time fell from 36 hours to 2.72 hours.

Source (Bank announcement): Deutsche Bank plans 15-to-two core overhaul as banks quantify AI gains" (The Asian Banker, November 2026)

Unified AI compute scheduling platform on Kubernetes with HAMi

Announced September 2026 (CNCF announcement dated 8 September 2026)

CMB built one control plane so AI training, fine-tuning and live inference share nearly 10,000 accelerator cards from different makers. This is the infrastructure that lets CMB run its large-model applications more cheaply at scale.

What it is

A unified, bank-built AI infrastructure platform, recognised as the winner of the CNCF End User Case Study Contest for KubeCon + CloudNativeCon + OpenInfra Summit + PyTorch Conference China 2026.

How it works

Kubernetes is the foundation; Kueue manages training queues, quotas and admission. KEDA and Prometheus scale inference from live demand, HAMi pools and slices shared accelerator capacity, and Fluid speeds access to data, model weights and checkpoints. The bank's in-house Twinkle framework lets five LoRA tenants share one base-model instance by default.

Technology used

Kubernetes, HAMi, Kueue, KEDA, Prometheus, Fluid, and CMB's in-house Twinkle training framework; heterogeneous accelerators including NVIDIA, Kunlunxin, Ascend and Moore Threads

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Bringing 99% of accelerator compute under the framework "lifted average utilization from 35% to more than 60%" and cut "the cost of processing 1 million tokens (input and output combined) by more than 60%" under comparable conditions; the Twinkle sharing setup cut accelerator usage by 80% and increased training density fivefold.

Source (Independent reporting): "China Merchants Bank Wins CNCF End User Case Study Contest for Unifying AI Training and Inference on Kubernetes" — CNCF (bank's own end-user case study), September 2026

856 large-model application scenarios deployed bank-wide

Announced April 2026 (reporting results for the year ended December 2025; CMB's 2025 annual results announcement is dated 27 March 2026)

By the end of 2025, CMB had 856 large-model applications in use across the bank, not just pilots. This is one of the largest disclosed production AI footprints at a Chinese bank in the window.

What it is

A bank-wide programme of large-model applications embedded in front-line business processes. It sits under CMB's push to become an "intelligent bank" and move AI "from a supporting tool to the core of services."

How it works

The applications run across the bank's businesses and replace manual work in day-to-day processes. CMB's CIO said the bank is also adding multiple measures to control large-model hallucination risk, because the models are used in real financial work.

Technology used

Large models: vendor/model not specified in the source. (A separate Euromoney report on the same 2025 programme refers to 183 specialist models deployed and daily token processing up 10.1 times in 2025.)

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The applications "replaced more than 15.56 million hours of manual work last year — equivalent to the workload of over 8,000 full-time employees," per CIO Zhou Tianhong as reported in the source. Separately in the same report, an AI assistant for account managers "has driven a 20 percent increase in the average transaction volume per customer."

Source (Bank announcement): "Financial majors step up AI-driven strategic overhaul" — China Daily, 16 April 2026
