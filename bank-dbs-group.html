DBS Joy — Gen AI-powered virtual assistant for corporate clients

Announced November 2025

DBS Joy is a 24/7 virtual agent inside DBS IDEAL that answers corporate and SME banking questions in plain conversation. It replaced fixed, pre-programmed answers with personalised Gen AI replies at scale.

What it is

DBS Joy was first launched in 2018 as Asia's first corporate banking virtual assistant, and was relaunched with new Gen AI capabilities for all corporate clients. It gives instant answers to frequent queries and common requests, with more detailed and personalised responses than the old version.

How it works

Clients use it inside DBS IDEAL, the bank's digital platform for businesses. For complex needs it automatically hands the user to a human service specialist, who is supported by a Gen AI co-pilot for faster data retrieval and recommendations. Responses pass through several layers of internal checks and safeguards, and trained "DBS Joy evaluators" review chat quality afterwards.

Technology used

Developed in-house by DBS; large language models integrated with the bank's proprietary knowledge base (no named external model or vendor in the source).

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Customer satisfaction scores improved by over 23% during the trial period; since trials began in February 2025, DBS Joy had handled over 120,000 unique chats, with about 4,000 corporate clients, mostly SMEs, using it every month.

Agentic AI credit-memo chain for large corporate lending

Announced July 2026

Some 70 to 80 linked AI agents draft credit memos for large corporate loans. It is DBS's most advanced agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own) deployment, replacing days of manual work.

What it is

Credit memos traditionally required a relationship manager to work through a ~120-page annual report, a similar-sized sustainability report, client financials and industry analysis. The agent chain now assembles that material into a pre-formatted draft memo. The output is deliberately not approval-ready: the human still makes the lending decision.

How it works

The agents pull together external news, internal client notes and competitive analysis, then deliver a structured draft. The relationship manager questions the system through a chat interface, asking it to justify conclusions and produce supporting evidence. DBS caps the number of steps any agent chain can run, applies checks between stages, and does not let agents act autonomously until governance tooling matures.

Technology used

In-house enterprise agents built by DBS on its three-layer AI infrastructure (system of knowledge; agent "factory/foundry"; consumption layer), wrapped in a control plane for identity, observability, traceability, policy enforcement and kill switches; model-agnostic — no single named model or vendor in the source.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (The source states only that the manual task previously consumed days of a relationship manager's time; no quantified saving is given.)

CSO Assistant — generative AI co-pilot for customer service officers

Announced October 2025

CSO Assistant is a generative AI co-pilot that helps DBS's customer service officers while they handle customer calls. More than 1,000 officers across four markets use it on over 250,000 customer queries a month.

What it is

The assistant sits in the customer service officer's workbench in the contact centre. DBS describes it on its own responsible-AI page (6 October 2025); rollout began at the Singapore customer centre in 2024 and extended to Hong Kong, Taiwan and India.

How it works

It transcribes and summarises live calls, tags the reason for every call automatically, and gives the officer an integrated knowledge base while they talk. It also triages customer email and drafts replies through a companion tool, "WriteLah!".

Technology used

Built in-house by DBS; powered by Google Cloud's Customer Engagement Suite with Google AI, using generative AI models including Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude, with voice telephony and speech recognition (per Computer Weekly).

Benefit reported (Measured result)

DBS reports full-rollout transcription accuracy of 98–99%, and that the assistant reduces the time officers need to provide solutions by about 20%. It supports more than 250,000 customer queries a month.

Source (Bank announcement): Ethical & Responsible AI in Banking" (DBS, 6 October 2025) · Second source

DBS-GPT — internal Gen AI chatbot for all employees

Announced November 2025

DBS-GPT is DBS's own secure Gen AI chatbot, open to every employee in its core markets. It is the firm-wide foundation on which DBS builds its other Gen AI use cases.

What it is

An internal Gen AI assistant that helps staff with routine work such as summarising transcripts, generating content and coding. DBS describes it as a foundational "horizontal" layer, industrialising Gen AI across the bank alongside specific "vertical" use cases. Per DBS Annual Report 2025, it provides role-based access to over four million DBS policies and content items.

How it works

Staff use it inside a controlled, secure environment designed to meet financial-industry requirements, so they can search and synthesise information across the bank without exposing data to public tools.

Technology used

DBS's in-house Gen AI chatbot equipped with DBS knowledge; the source does not name an underlying model or external vendor.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (DBS separately reported approx. SGD 1 billion of economic value in 2025 from its overall data analytics and AI/ML programme, over 2,000 models across more than 430 use cases, but that figure is programme-wide and is not attributed to DBS-GPT, so it is not claimed as this deployment's benefit.)
