Agentic AI for mortgage and HELOC pre-adjudication (Real Estate Secured Lending)

Announced May 2026

TD launched its first agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own) for mortgage and HELOC pre-adjudication. It prepares the underwriter's summary memo, leaving the lending decision with the human underwriter.

What it is

Autonomous AI agents that use generative AI language models to complete tasks with minimal human intervention. It is the first step in an end-to-end agentic transformation of TD's Real Estate Secured Lending operations. It is overseen by TD's Trustworthy AI team (checks on privacy, security, fairness, accountability and explainability).

How it works

The agents classify client documents, extract key information, calculate client income and validate it against selected policy requirements, perform consent checks and search for discrepancies. They then generate a concise application summary memo for the underwriter.

Technology used

Agentic AI / generative AI language models developed by Layer 6, TD's AI research and development centre, with TD's technology, data, RESL and risk teams (specific LLM not named in the release)

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Early results stated in the source: the pre-adjudication process fell from an average of 15 hours to an average of less than three minutes, with more accurate results, reducing risk and the unit cost of adjudication (no dollar figure given).

Source (Bank announcement): "TD Launches Agentic AI to Transform Real Estate Secured Lending from End to End" — TD Bank Group newsroom, 21 May 2026

Launches Credit Assist to draft commercial credit memos in minutes

Announced October 2026

TD Bank U.S. has launched Credit Assist, a multi-agent AI tool for its commercial credit teams. It drafts six sections of a credit memo from deal documents, with citations, for underwriters to review.

What it is

Credit Assist serves more than 300 TD credit professionals who prepare over 10,000 commercial credit memos a year. TD unveiled it at the 2026 Evident AI Symposium in New York on 22 October 2026.

How it works

An orchestration layer coordinates specialised agents that process, extract, analyse and organise information from complex deal documents. A single review can involve 20 to 30 documents spanning more than 50 document types. The agents draft six memo sections in parallel, each with a cited narrative and its own review pass.

Technology used

Multi-agent AI with an orchestration layer, developed by TD's Analytics, Intelligence & AI team with Layer 6; the release does not name the underlying model.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

TD says preparing a first-draft credit memo fell from up to three working days to less than 30 minutes. In testing on 220 curated deals, 95 percent of evaluated outputs passed subject-matter expert review.

Source (Bank announcement): TD Launches Credit Assist, Turning Days of Commercial Credit Work into Minutes" (Business Wire, 22 October 2026)

TD Securities AI Virtual Assistant

Announced July 2025

TD Securities launched a proprietary generative AI chatbot for its front-office sales, trading and research staff. It turns large volumes of internal research into short, cited summaries so staff can answer client questions faster.

What it is

An internal Knowledge Management System (KMS) chatbot for TD Securities' Institutional Sales, Trading and Research professionals, launched initially as a pilot. It was built with TD's technology groups, Layer 6 and Enterprise Innovation teams, and TD said similar KMS platforms were planned across seven businesses by end-2025.

How it works

Using Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), the assistant searches internal TD Securities research documents, analyses and summarises key points, and returns insights with direct citations to the source material. Its Text-to-SQL feature converts plain-language questions into SQL queries against the data repository and presents results as summary tables and visual plots.

Technology used

Proprietary TD generative AI chatbot; Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG); Text-to-SQL; built with Layer 6 (underlying LLM not named in the release)

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (The release says it is designed to save colleagues time; no measured figure is given.)

Source (Bank announcement): "Virtual AI Assistant To Help Power TD Securities" — TD Bank Group newsroom, 8 July 2025

Generative AI Knowledge Management System for contact centre agents

Announced February 2026 · Went live November 2025

TD added a generative AI assistant, built on a Knowledge Management System, in its US contact centers. Agents get consistent answers during live calls, cutting client hold time and resolving issues faster.

What it is

TD Bank told CIO Dive that it added a generative AI virtual assistant in its US contact center. The Knowledge Management System handles questions from agents and provides consistent responses, in order to reduce client hold time on calls and resolve issues faster. It also lowers how often problems are escalated to supervisors.

How it works

During a call, the agent asks the assistant, which retrieves and synthesises policy and product information from the bank's knowledge base. TD later extended the same tool from its contact centers to its Canadian branch network of over 1,000 branches.

Technology used

TD's generative AI Knowledge Management System; TD's virtual assistants are built by its Layer 6 AI team on the bank's own content. The CIO Dive report does not name an outside model or vendor for this deployment.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

TD reported that the assistant reduced call hold times by 12%, and that 80% of US contact-centre agents used it in its first month (November 2025).

Source (Independent reporting): TD Bank US head of AI weighs in on agents" (CIO Dive, 18 February 2026)

TD AI Prism — predictive foundation model

Announced June 2025

TD launched TD AI Prism, a single predictive foundation model built entirely in-house. It predicts many customer needs, so TD can match customers with the right product or action and personalise communications.

What it is

A large-scale AI model combined with TD's bank datasets to understand customers holistically. It replaces many separate single-purpose models with one architecture, and was to be used first to personalise marketing communications during 2025.

How it works

The model processes 100 times more data variables than before to improve predictive performance. It was built on TD's secure cloud platform, so customer data does not leave the bank, and grew out of Layer 6 research on tabular foundation models.

Technology used

TD AI Prism: proprietary model built in-house by Layer 6, TD's AI research and development centre, on TD's secure cloud platform

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (The release states improved predictive performance and 100× more data variables, but gives no quantified business benefit.)

Source (Bank announcement): "TD Announces Launch of Groundbreaking Predictive Foundation Model" — TD Bank Group newsroom, 11 June 2025
