Deploy Gong Xiao Cai agent for personal customer managers

Announced August 2026

Gong Xiao Cai is a dedicated AI agent for ICBC's personal customer managers. It helps them search data, run analyses and develop client plans. It provided support over 22 million times in early 2026.

What it is

Gong Xiao Cai is an intelligent agent built exclusively for ICBC's team of personal customer managers. It supports the full marketing process from analysis to customer management.

How it works

Customer managers use the agent to search for data and conduct analyses. It also helps them develop service plans for clients.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

The bank says Gong Xiao Cai effectively improved the quality and efficiency of frontline marketing services. It supported precise allocation and steady growth of customers' assets.

Support credit review with ICBC eXaminer (Gong Xiao Shen)

Announced August 2026

ICBC eXaminer is a digital credit review and approval assistant. It handles credit risk analysis, compliance assessment, financing plan design and report drafting. It supported approvals totalling over RMB1 trillion in early 2026.

What it is

ICBC eXaminer (Gong Xiao Shen) is a digital assistant for credit review and approval staff. It supports core credit approval tasks across the bank.

How it works

The assistant uses large AI model capabilities to analyse customer credit risk. It also assesses compliance, designs financing plans and drafts reports for reviewers.

Technology used

Large AI models.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

The bank says the assistant helps credit reviewers substantially improve the quality and efficiency of credit review and approval.

ICBC Zhiyong — enterprise-level financial large model system

Announced March 2025

ICBC built its own very large financial model, called ICBC Zhiyong, and uses it across the bank. One in-house model now supports work in more than 20 major business areas and 200 scenarios.

What it is

ICBC Zhiyong is an enterprise-level financial large model technology system with hundreds of billions of parameters. ICBC reported it in its 2024 annual results, announced on 28 March 2025.

How it works

The model is applied inside bank business areas and scenarios, rather than sold as a customer product. ICBC said it led the industry in core elements such as computing power, algorithms and data.

Technology used

ICBC Zhiyong, ICBC's in-house financial large model system with hundreds of billions of parameters.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The source states it empowered more than 20 major business areas and 200 scenarios; no money or time saving is given.

Source (Bank announcement): ICBC Achieves Steady Business Development While Maintaining Stability in 2024" (ICBC, own newsroom, 28 March 2025)

Enterprise-level intelligent risk control platform

Announced March 2025

ICBC sped up building a bank-wide smart risk control platform that watches, measures and warns about risks. Risks can now be spotted earlier and dealt with faster across the whole group.

What it is

The platform covers an enterprise-level risk view, measurement, monitoring and early warning, and decision-making. It is based on internal and external financial and non-financial data.

How it works

It is built to promote early perception, targeted identification, timely warning and efficient resolution of various risks. ICBC also strengthened its independently developed ICBC e Prevention product alongside it.

Technology used

Machine learning, knowledge graphs and big models, as stated in the source.
