ServiceNow AI Platform adoption with ejada Systems

Announced November 2025

Al Rajhi Bank signed a multi-year deal to run its internal operations on ServiceNow's AI platform. One platform will now handle workflows across the bank's HR, IT and financial services teams.

What it is

Under a multi-year agreement announced on 9 November 2025, Al Rajhi Bank will use the ServiceNow AI Platform. It will transform its operations and automate workflows across HR, IT and financial services. ServiceNow described the project as among the biggest digital transformation agreements in the region.

How it works

The platform brings IT service management, AIOps, business operations and operational resilience into a single intelligent layer, replacing legacy systems. ejada Systems is ServiceNow's consulting and implementation partner and will deploy and manage the platform across the bank's operations.

Technology used

ServiceNow AI Platform; ejada Systems as implementation partner.

Announced October 2025
