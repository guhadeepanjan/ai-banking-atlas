Digital Email Manager and Voice BOT 2.0 in the voice and email service channels

Announced July 2025

ICICI Bank's service channels use a machine-learning email manager that reads and pre-answers customer emails, and a voice bot for phone calls. Its voice channels handle about 5 million customer engagements a month.

What it is

The bank's Annual Report 2024-25 describes its Voice Channels operation: the "Digital Email Manager" for customer email and "Voice BOT 2.0" for calls. The report also records voice biometric authentication, which verified 3.36 million calls in fiscal 2025.

How it works

The Digital Email Manager uses machine learning and contextual analysis to interpret the content of customer emails and provides pre-processed templates and auto-responses. It is integrated with back-end systems and robotic process automation. Voice BOT 2.0 is integrated with the bank's applications to resolve more calls at bot level without a human agent, and was undergoing a comprehensive transformation during the year.

Technology used

Machine learning and contextual analysis, integrated with robotic process automation; no external vendor or model is named in the source.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. The report gives no time or cost saving for these tools.

Enterprise AI Platform

Announced October 2026

ICICI built one secure in-house platform for its teams to develop and deploy AI and Generative AI tools. AI now scales across the whole bank, not as isolated pilots, under human oversight and model governance.

What it is

A bank-wide AI and Generative AI platform, described by Executive Director Sandeep Batra on the Q2-FY2027 results call. The bank said the areas covered include software development, cybersecurity, customer onboarding, fraud detection, portfolio monitoring, document extraction and summarisation, and customer service. The bank is also working with multiple AI start-ups to explore and develop banking solutions.

How it works

Teams build and deploy AI solutions inside the platform's secure environment. Guardrails define the boundaries for AI usage, ensure human oversight of AI-driven processes, and enforce stringent data access controls. They also set protocols for effective model governance, alongside upskilling for business, digital and technology teams.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

The source states the capabilities "help simplify processes, improve turn-around times and support decision-making across multiple banking functions." No quantified figure is given in the source.

Source (Bank announcement): "Media conference call for quarter ended September 30, 2026" — ICICI Bank, 17 October 2026

Autonomous bots for customer communications and back-office workflows

Announced July 2026

ICICI's 2025-26 annual report and Form 20-F report more than 100 autonomous bots processing inbound customer communications. Over 150 more bots automate repetitive back-office workflows, and conversational bots are being deployed across business functions.

What it is

A production fleet of autonomous bots handling customer communications and workflow automation. It was reported in the bank's annual disclosures for the fiscal year ended 31 March 2026. The annual report is dated 18 July 2026 and the Form 20-F was filed 20 July 2026. The same disclosure set also describes in-house Generative AI tools for customer communications (including drafting and summarising) and AI summaries supporting underwriting and portfolio monitoring.

How it works

The bots process inbound customer communications and run repetitive workflows autonomously, on the bank's enterprise AI platform with human-in-the-loop oversight. Staff validate AI-drafted customer communications before use.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

The source does not state a benefit. (The annual report / 20-F describe faster turnaround times and productivity gains in general terms but publish no AI value, cost-saving or revenue figure.)
