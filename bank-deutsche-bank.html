Agentic AI solution for Source of Wealth KYC processes

Announced September 2026

Deutsche Bank Private Bank rolled out agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own) to automate the research and paperwork behind Source of Wealth checks, one of the slowest parts of onboarding.

What it is

The solution supports the Source of Wealth element of Know Your Customer checks, which requires the bank to establish and document how prospective clients accumulated their wealth. It went live in the Singapore and Hong Kong booking centres at the beginning of September 2026. It was also made available to advisers in Dubai for accounts booked in Singapore, with a broader rollout across wealth management centres planned.

How it works

Integrated into the bank's digital KYC platform, the solution analyses information from client documentation and approved external sources, automating research, documentation and preparation of Source of Wealth data. It helps identify gaps or inconsistencies and prepares assessments for human review.

Technology used

Agentic AI integrated into Deutsche Bank's digital KYC platform; the underlying model is not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Based on current forecasts given in the announcement, the bank's Emerging Markets coverage region is on track to onboard approximately 30 per cent more clients in 2026 compared with 2025. No measured time saving was published.

Source (Bank announcement): Deutsche Bank Private Bank launches Agentic AI solution for Source of Wealth KYC processes" (Deutsche Bank Private Bank, own newsroom, September 2026) · Second source

DB Lumina — AI research agent for Deutsche Bank Research

Announced September 2025

DB Lumina is an AI research agent that takes manual work off Deutsche Bank Research analysts, helping them gather data, summarise documents and draft reports. It had around 5,000 users when Google Cloud documented it.

What it is

DB Lumina is a digital assistant built for Deutsche Bank Research, used in divisions including Investment Bank Origination & Advisory and Fixed Income & Currencies. It automates data analysis, streamlines workflows and helps analysts produce more accurate and timely insights.

How it works

Analysts use a generative AI chat, prompt templates for repeatable document tasks, and a knowledge feature that grounds answers in internal research, SEC filings and other documents. Inline citations support fact-checking. Documents are split into chunks, embedded and stored so the system can retrieve the most relevant passages for each question.

Technology used

Google Cloud: Gemini models (Gemini 2.0 and 2.5) via Vertex AI, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), Google Kubernetes Engine, Cloud SQL with pgvector, Discovery Engine API.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Analysts reported saving 30 to 45 minutes on preparing earnings note templates and up to two hours when writing research reports and roadshow updates, according to Google Cloud. One analyst increased the analysis in an earnings report by 50%.

Source (Independent reporting): Deutsche Bank delivers AI-powered financial research with DB Lumina" (Google Cloud Blog, September 2025)

Integrate FalconTST 2.0 into FX hedging models

Announced August 2026

Deutsche Bank integrated Ant International's FalconTST 2.0 into its FX hedging models. The forecasting model is meant to improve how the bank predicts currency exposure and times its hedges.

What it is

Deutsche Bank added the model to the FX hedging models used in its foreign exchange operations. The adoption formed part of a four-bank integration announced by Ant International in August 2026.

How it works

The time-series transformer learns patterns from large historical data sets and forecasts cash flow and currency exposure. The bank's hedging models draw on those forecasts.

Technology used

Ant International's FalconTST 2.0, a time-series transformer model.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Ant International reports forecast accuracy above 93% across FalconTST 2.0 deployments.

TPRM AI — agentic AI for third-party risk assessments

Announced December 2025

Deutsche Bank's TPRM AI checks vendor evidence documents using agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own). Reviews that took about 30 minutes per document are now drafted in minutes, with humans deciding.

What it is

TPRM AI was developed by the bank's Technology, Data & Innovation team and implemented in Global Procurement & Vendor Management in December 2025. It assesses documentary evidence that vendors must supply before they can be approved to work with the bank.

How it works

A set of specialised AI agents works in sequence. The first retrieves the right Deutsche Bank control questions, the next drafts answers based on the vendor's evidence, and the third proposes an assessment outcome with precise citations. Human assessors review the suggestions and make the final decision.

Technology used

TPRM AI, Deutsche Bank's in-house agentic AI platform (cloud-based); the underlying model is not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

A manual review typically took around 30 minutes per evidence document; TPRM AI analyses up to five documents in under two minutes, and a multi-file business continuity review that took about three hours manually can be completed in about 30 minutes with AI support and human validation. The bank reported around 90% accuracy in recommended outcomes, benchmarked against human validation.

Source (Bank announcement): Putting agentic AI to work in third party risk management" (Deutsche Bank, own newsroom, 13 May 2026)
