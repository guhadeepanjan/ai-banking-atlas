ia.i — conversational AI assistant in the banking app

Announced July 2026

Itaú launched ia.i, an AI assistant inside its app, reaching about 300,000 customers in its first phase. Customers can handle accounts, cards, credit and insurance in one conversation, without visiting a branch or call centre.

What it is

ia.i is a complete conversational layer for Itaú's super app, launched on 27 July 2026, building on the Inteligência Itaú features first introduced in 2024. The bank aims to extend it to its entire individual customer base by the end of 2026.

How it works

Customers interact by text or voice, and can also use images and clicks in the same experience. The assistant draws on "Customer DNA", a layer of financial context covering needs, habits and life stages for about 70 million customers. It also connects customers to specialist human support when needed.

Technology used

OpenAI models (a range of models matched to each task, from low-cost, low-latency work to deeper reasoning), accessed through Itaú's own IARA governance and orchestration platform.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (The source gives rollout numbers, about 300,000 customers in phase one, but no measured customer or cost benefit.)

Pix payments on WhatsApp

Announced January 2026

Itaú customers can make Pix instant payments inside WhatsApp by voice, text or image, using generative AI. Paying someone becomes as simple as sending a chat message, with no need to open the banking app.

What it is

Pix on WhatsApp was the first customer solution launched on Itaú's Inteligência Itaú platform. It lets customers make transfers, split bills and generate instant receipts inside WhatsApp. The bank expanded it from a pilot to its entire customer base during 2025.

How it works

The customer sends a message, typed, spoken or an image. The AI agent understands the payment instruction, prepares the Pix transfer and confirms it, learning from each interaction to personalise later ones.

Technology used

Inteligência Itaú, the bank's proprietary multi-agent generative AI platform, built from multiple foundation models (proprietary and open source) refined with the bank's own data. For the WhatsApp Pix task, the platform found small open-source models responded faster than large proprietary ones.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The solution made transactions up to 20 seconds faster and achieved 90% repeat usage, according to the bank as reported in the source.

Inteligência Itaú generative AI platform — 150 solutions in production

Announced January 2026

Itaú has put more than 150 generative AI solutions into production on its own multi-agent platform, with over 750 more in development. The bank is running generative AI at industrial scale, not as isolated experiments.

What it is

Inteligência Itaú is the bank's proprietary generative AI platform. Reporting on the bank's technology programme in January 2026 said it had more than 750 use cases in development and 150 already in production, covering end-customer solutions and internal work.

How it works

The platform uses a multi-agent architecture built from multiple foundation models, both proprietary and open source, refined with Itaú's own data. This keeps solutions secure and relevant to each customer interaction. In June 2025 the bank also launched Itaú Investment Intelligence, offering personalised investment recommendations from the client's profile and real-time market data.

Technology used

Inteligência Itaú platform; multiple foundation models (proprietary and open source); the bank separately runs its AI access through its IARA governance platform.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Itaú reported a 141% increase in the volume of generative AI initiatives in production in the third quarter of 2025 compared with the same period a year earlier, and about 1,800 AI models in use across the bank, as reported in the source.
