FiMI Banking — sovereign compact AI model for Indian retail banking (NPCI, to be jointly developed with HDFC Bank)

Announced September 2026 (unveiled 9 September 2026 at the Global Fintech Festival 2026, Mumbai)

NPCI unveiled FiMI Banking, India's first sovereign compact AI model for retail banking, and will strengthen it with HDFC Bank. The model lets AI agents do tasks, not just answer questions, inside a bank's rules.

What it is

FiMI Banking is a compact model trained for Indian retail banking use cases and designed to complete complex, long-context tasks within a bank's defined rules and infrastructure. NPCI will collaborate with HDFC Bank, combining NPCI's technology and AI capability with HDFC Bank's AI and banking domain expertise to jointly develop the model.

How it works

Unlike question-answering systems, the model supports agentic tasks: an AI agent can reason, interact with tools and complete actions in a banking environment. It was trained on synthetic data, with no real customer, account or institutional data in the benchmarks. NPCI also open-sourced the benchmarks, technical report and evaluation sets. The sets include IndicBank Bench, with 799 scripted multi-turn cases across six retail domains, and the Tau² Agentic Banking Benchmark, with 1,000 tasks across 50 scenario families.

Technology used

Built on Gemma, Google's family of open models, and further trained by NPCI on Indian retail banking scenarios.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Straight-through processing speeds up credit card applications

Announced July 2026

HDFC Bank runs straight-through processing for credit card applications. Processing that once took hours now finishes in near real time in some cases.

What it is

Automated, straight-through processing of credit card applications, disclosed in the bank's FY2025-26 annual report. It removes manual handling from parts of the application journey.

How it works

Applications move through processing automatically, without manual hand-offs in the covered steps. The bank reports hours of processing compressed to near real time in some cases.

Technology used

HDFC Bank's in-house Neev generative AI platform.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

Credit card application processing time fell from hours to near real time in some cases.

GenAI extracts data from import remittance documents across India

Announced July 2026

HDFC Bank uses generative AI to extract fields from import remittance documents across India. A companion tool for wider trade document handling is in early rollout.

GenAI field extraction for import remittances, rolled out pan-India and disclosed in the bank's FY2025-26 annual report. The bank is also building early agentic AI for trade document handling.

The system reads import remittance documents and extracts the required fields automatically. The bank describes extraction confidence as high. It plans to extend the approach to more trade products.

The platform analyses transactions as they occur. Suspected money-mule credits are blocked automatically, including payments on India's UPI network.

AI helps resolve card and transaction queries in customer service

Announced July 2026

HDFC Bank uses AI in customer service to help resolve customers' card and transaction queries. The bank lists it among the live AI deployments in its official annual report, not as an experiment.

What it is

The bank's Integrated Annual Report 2025-26 includes a table of AI deployments; the customer service entry is "AI-assisted resolution of card and transaction queries". The report was published in July 2026.

How it works

AI assists the handling of card and transaction questions that customers bring to the bank's service channels, so queries are resolved with less manual work. The bank's in-house "Neev" generative AI platform, which includes voice-based agents, underpins its generative AI use cases.

Technology used

HDFC Bank's in-house Neev generative AI platform (per the annual report coverage); no external vendor or model is named for this use case.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

The annual report states the benefit qualitatively ("Higher accuracy; less manual effort and faster response") and gives no figures.

'Neev' — in-house enterprise GenAI platform

Announced July 2026 (FY2025-26 annual report, reported 12 July 2026)

HDFC Bank introduced Neev, its own full-stack enterprise generative AI platform, to scale AI use across all its operations. One shared, governed platform replaces separate departmental AI builds and keeps controls consistent.

What it is

Neev is an in-house enterprise GenAI platform described in the bank's FY2026 annual report. The bank said GenAI played a key role in a transformational year in which it also completed its net banking upgrade and rolled out its in-house Mobile Banking platform.

How it works

Neev standardises AI development, so business teams can focus on solving operational problems while the platform ensures consistency and enterprise-wide controls. Governance is built in: capabilities are auditable, monitored and aligned with regulatory expectations.

Technology used

Built in-house by HDFC Bank (described as an in-house enterprise GenAI full-stack platform). No external vendor or model is named in the source.

AnnouncedEnterprise-wide

Centralised, platform-driven GenAI strategy — 15+ GenAI programmes and GenAI Academy

Announced July 2025 (FY2024-25 annual report, reported by Mint)

HDFC Bank moved from small GenAI pilots to one central, platform-driven GenAI strategy with over 15 programmes. The programmes target staff productivity and customer service across the whole bank, and a GenAI Academy trains staff.

What it is

In his FY25 annual report letter, MD & CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan told shareholders the bank had identified over 15 programmes that will use generative AI. The bank also launched a GenAI Academy, and said it had upgraded to a next-generation cybersecurity operations centre (CSOC) powered by AI and machine learning.

How it works

Instead of scattered pilots, GenAI use cases are run through a centralised platform approach to improve staff productivity and customer service. The Academy builds skills through a structured curriculum with tiered proficiency levels.

Technology used

Generative AI (programmes not individually named in the source); AI/ML in the upgraded CSOC. No vendor or model is named in the source.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (The stated aims are improved staff productivity and customer service; the CEO's expectation of substantial progress on real-time credit decisioning is an expectation, not a reported result.)

Source (Independent reporting): "HDFC Bank raises bets on GenAI for productivity gains" — Mint (Livemint), July 2025
