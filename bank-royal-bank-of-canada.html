RBC pilots meeting preparation tools for wealth advisors

Announced April 2026

RBC Wealth Management is piloting tools that prepare for and summarise client meetings. Early pilots show time savings of upwards of 30 to 40 minutes per meeting. Every Canadian wealth advisor already uses RBC Assist.

What it is

The pilots cover client meeting preparation and summarisation for advisors in RBC's Canadian wealth management and private banking businesses. They form part of a wider push to cut administrative work so advisors spend more time with clients.

How it works

The tools prepare meeting materials and summarise meetings for the advisor. They build on RBC Assist, the bank's in-house generative AI tool, which has been given to every wealth advisor in Canada.

Technology used

RBC Assist, RBC's in-house developed generative AI tool.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Pilots are demonstrating time savings for advisors of upwards of 30 to 40 minutes per meeting, according to RBC.

Source (Independent reporting): PWM Wealth Tech Awards recognize RBC's strategy of pairing top-tier advisors with intelligent technology to drive growth and client outcomes" (RBC Wealth Management via CNW, 27 April 2026)

Aiden platform — AidenResearch and AidenBanker (RBC Capital Markets)

Announced March 2026

Aiden is an agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own) platform for RBC Capital Markets, used by more than 8,000 employees. It automates earnings content, call summaries and filing access.

What it is

Aiden is RBC Capital Markets' AI platform, developed in partnership with Borealis AI. AidenResearch deploys specialised AI agents across the Global Research division, and AidenBanker is a unified system for investment bankers.

How it works

AidenResearch agents automate earnings content generation, call summarisation, data catalog analysis and real-time filing access. AidenBanker connects automated pitchbook creation, pre-meeting preparation and note-taking into a single workflow.

Technology used

NVIDIA AI Enterprise; developed with Borealis AI.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Document processing capacity has increased tenfold; research report generation is up to 60% faster; and complex client queries that previously took hours or days are now answered in near real time (as reported in the source).

Source (Independent reporting): RBC Estimates $1B in Revenue Lift by Embedding AI in Core Banking" (PYMNTS, 23 March 2026)

RBC Assist puts an in-house AI assistant on employee desktops

Announced February 2026

RBC Assist is Royal Bank of Canada's in-house generative AI assistant for employees. Almost 27,000 employees were using it by February 2026. It helps staff find information and complete everyday work faster.

What it is

RBC Assist is a proprietary AI assistant the bank built for its own workforce. RBC created a dedicated AI Group in February 2026, reporting to the chief executive, to scale tools like it. The group also advances research into generative and agentic AI use cases.

How it works

Employees use RBC Assist in their daily workflows to work faster and free up time for more meaningful work. It runs on RBC Lumina, the bank's enterprise data and AI platform. Lumina sits on one of the largest private-sector GPU clusters in Canada.

Technology used

RBC Assist, developed in-house by RBC, supported by the RBC Lumina enterprise data and AI platform.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Source (Independent reporting): RBC creates new AI Group to help bring top AI opportunities to market" (Royal Bank of Canada, 18 February 2026)

ATOM (Asynchronous Temporal Model)

Announced July 2025

ATOM is RBC's own foundation model, trained on billions of client financial transactions. It personalises products and client experience across the bank, and sits behind several live products, including credit decisions and Avion Rewards.

What it is

ATOM is a proprietary AI foundation model for financial services, created by RBC Borealis, the bank's research institute, and announced on 29 July 2025. First used in 2023, it has been deployed in several products and services to create an individualised product and client experience.

How it works

ATOM was securely trained on large-scale financial datasets, including billions of client financial transactions. It sits inside RBC's Lumina data platform, which collects and curates event data from all business lines and lets AI scientists process up to 10 billion transactions per minute. For Avion Rewards, ATOM powers personalised recommendations in a redemption newsletter viewed by 1.3 million users per month.

Technology used

ATOM, RBC Borealis's proprietary foundation model, on RBC's Lumina data platform.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

RBC stated ATOM will play a central role in its ambition to achieve $700 million to $1 billion in enterprise value generated from AI-driven benefits by 2027 (a stated target in the release, not a realised benefit for ATOM alone).

Source (Independent reporting): RBC's proprietary AI foundation model for financial services aims to enhance personalized client experience" (CNW, July 2025)

North for Banking (with Cohere)

Announced January 2025

North for Banking is a secure generative AI platform co-developed with Cohere for financial services, for use across RBC. It lets the bank build and deploy generative AI on sensitive data securely and efficiently.

What it is

North for Banking is an enterprise generative AI solution that RBC and Cohere announced on 9 January 2025 they would co-develop and securely deploy. It is a customised version of Cohere's secure AI workspace platform for the financial services industry.

How it works

The platform integrates with RBC's and Cohere's own proprietary foundation models, and with RBC's internal platforms. As an end-to-end AI workspace, it is intended to help employees automate routine tasks, search for answers and speed up their work.

Technology used

Cohere North platform; RBC's and Cohere's proprietary foundation models.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. The release states the goal of increasing workforce productivity and operational efficiency, with no figure.

Source (Bank announcement): RBC and Cohere partner to develop the next generation of highly secure generative AI solutions for financial services" (PR Newswire, January 2025)
