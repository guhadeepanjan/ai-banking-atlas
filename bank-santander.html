AI speeds up card fraud claims processing in Brazil

Announced June 2026

In Brazil, Santander uses AI to process card fraud claims. The process is around 95% faster, with up to 90% automation and an error rate below 1%.

What it is

AI applied to the handling of card fraud claims at Santander in Brazil, automating most of the claims process.

How it works

The bank's process-automation agents handle manual tasks in the claims workflow. Up to 90% of the process is automated.

Technology used

Santander's process-automation agents, part of more than 280 such agents in production across the group.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Card fraud claims processing in Brazil is around 95% faster, with up to 90% automation and an error rate below 1%.

Europe's first live end-to-end payment executed by an AI agent (with Mastercard Agent Pay)

Announced March 2026

Santander and Mastercard completed Europe's first live end-to-end payment by an AI agent. Santander calls it the first agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own) payment within a regulated banking framework.

What it is

A pilot in which an AI agent initiated and completed a real payment on behalf of a customer, processed through Santander's live payments infrastructure. Santander states the pilot "does not constitute a commercial rollout at this stage."

How it works

The transaction ran in a controlled environment using Mastercard Agent Pay. Mastercard Agent Pay makes AI agents visible, governed participants in the payment flow between issuers, acquirers and merchants; PayOS supported the end-to-end orchestration. Agents act only within predefined limits and permissions. A follow-on step came in June 2026: Getnet, Santander's merchant payments platform, launched infrastructure for merchants to accept AI agent-initiated payments. It was tested with Mastercard and Mexican startup Neivor, in what the companies call the first real-world case in Mexico and Latin America.

Technology used

Mastercard Agent Pay; PayOS (orchestration); Getnet payments infrastructure for the June 2026 merchant follow-on

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (The release frames it as a technical and operational readiness milestone, not a savings or revenue result.)

Announced March 2026

Santander and Visa completed controlled pilot purchases by AI agents in five Latin American markets. Agents bought books in four markets and chocolates in Brazil, within set consent limits.

Consumers delegate shopping tasks to AI agents, which complete purchases on their behalf. Transactions run only within defined consent parameters set by the customer.

Santander AI Lab specialised models and "Model Arena" with NVIDIA

Announced October 2026

Santander built tools to test models and agents on banking tasks and pick the cheapest one that works well. The bank is shifting from large general-purpose models to smaller, specialised models for specific banking jobs.

What it is

A set of AI Lab capabilities to evaluate, specialise and serve models and governed banking agents inside Santander's own architecture. It also includes a new three-year collaboration framework with NVIDIA. The framework covers foundation models, AI agents, fraud and anti-money-laundering, real-time decisioning and advanced simulation, including a banking digital twin.

How it works

"Model Arena" evaluates open-weight models against banking tasks and is being developed to design, simulate, evaluate and supervise banking agents, with adversarial testing of conversational AI in controlled environments. Models are specialised for banking workloads and inference is industrialised inside Santander's own systems before successful capabilities scale across the bank.

Technology used

NVIDIA NeMo Customizer (model specialisation), NVIDIA Kumo open-weight foundation models for structured data, NVIDIA Dynamo-Triton (inference serving), and Santander's Model Arena

Benefit reported (Measured result)

In selected experimental use cases, serving costs fell "from around €5 per interaction to approximately €0.03" while maintaining strong performance; in an experimental customer-service use case Santander "improved conversation quality while reducing costs by more than 50%" by deploying a more efficient AI model.

Source (Bank announcement): "Santander builds AI capabilities that improve performance and lower costs using NVIDIA AI infrastructure" — Banco Santander press release, 19 October 2026

AI access extended to all 185,000 employees, with 280+ process-automation agents in production

Announced June 2026

Santander extended AI access from nearly 40,000 active users to all 185,000 employees worldwide. At the same time it disclosed more than 280 automation agents already in production in credit, fraud, KYC and operations.

What it is

A group-wide employee AI rollout under Santander's AI-first strategy, with AI already applied to real processes in operations, risk, customer service, financial crime and software development.

How it works

Microsoft Copilot supports everyday productivity, including Microsoft 365 Copilot; for specialised work Santander uses a secure multi-provider approach. Production agents automate manual tasks and support end-to-end workflows, and solutions built in one country are designed to be reused across the group.

Technology used

Microsoft Copilot / Microsoft 365 Copilot; OpenAI's ChatGPT, Anthropic's Claude, Google's Gemini, plus other partners and G42 for specialised capabilities (all named in the source)

Benefit reported (Measured result)

In Brazil, AI in card fraud claims makes the process "around 95% faster, with up to 90% automation and an error rate below 1%"; Openbank's AI models process approximately 100,000 anti-money-laundering alerts per year, with investigations that "used to take hours" now completed in minutes; in May 2026 over 17,000 people were working with agentic AI in software development and 40% of all code in June was developed by AI. (A UK voice-channel figure in the same release, a target of around 240,000 calls resolved through self-service, is stated as a target, not a result.)

Source (Bank announcement): "Santander turns its AI-first strategy into measurable impact and extends AI access to all 185,000 employees" — Banco Santander newsroom, 22 June 2026
