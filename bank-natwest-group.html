Generative AI across the Cora digital assistant

Announced February 2026

NatWest scaled generative AI inside Cora, its digital assistant, growing its GenAI-powered journeys from four to twenty one in 2025. Customers get more natural conversations and quicker answers, with fewer queries passed to a colleague.

What it is

Cora is NatWest's customer-facing digital assistant. In March 2025 NatWest became the first UK-headquartered bank to collaborate with OpenAI, and during 2025 it embedded generative AI much more deeply into Cora's customer journeys.

How it works

Customers chat with Cora in natural language, and generative AI handles more complex journeys end to end instead of sending customers to generic web pages. NatWest's CIO reported that by the end of 2025, twenty one customer journeys were powered by GenAI, up from four.

Technology used

OpenAI models under NatWest's strategic collaboration with OpenAI (announced 20 March 2025); Cora's generative AI functionality was built with IBM (Cora+).

Benefit reported (Measured result)

NatWest said the GenAI functionality offered by Cora drove a 150% improvement in customer satisfaction while reducing the number of times a colleague needs to intervene (bank statement reported by Reuters, March 2025). The CIO's February 2026 review added that GenAI journeys brought quicker resolution times and less need for colleague intervention, without a new figure.

Brings in Cleareye.ai to automate trade document checks and TBML screening

Announced May 2026

NatWest is working with Cleareye.ai to automate checks on complex trade documents. The software checks trades against ICC rules and runs AI-driven trade-based money laundering checks.

What it is

The partnership, reported in May 2026, is part of NatWest's work to modernise trade finance and strengthen its financial crime controls. It targets the large volume of manual checks that trade transactions traditionally need.

How it works

Cleareye.ai's software automatically extracts and checks trade document data against International Chamber of Commerce rules. It also screens transactions for trade-based money laundering, a channel for moving illicit funds through trade.

Technology used

Cleareye.ai AI software for automated trade document examination and trade-based money laundering checks.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.
