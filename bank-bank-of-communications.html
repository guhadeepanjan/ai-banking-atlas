AI deployed in over 420 application scenarios

Announced August 2026 (Interim Report 2026, approved by the Board on 28 August 2026; HKEX filing 29 September 2026)

By mid-2026, BOCOM had deployed AI in over 420 application scenarios. They achieved positive results in cross-border finance, inclusive finance, risk prevention and control, and anti-telecom fraud.

What it is

The scale-up of the AI+ programme from the 2025 annual report into 420+ live application scenarios across the group in H1 2026. It is cross-cutting, spanning cross-border and inclusive finance, risk and fraud prevention, so it is classified Enterprise-wide rather than under any single business segment.

How it works

AI technologies are applied scenario-by-scenario across those business areas on the bank's enterprise AI/agent infrastructure described in the 2025 annual report. The interim report does not describe the technical mechanism for individual scenarios.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (For the AI scenarios specifically, the report says only that they "achieved positive results." The same business-review paragraph notes group business cost fell 2.08% year-on-year and the cost-to-income ratio dropped 2.47 percentage points, but the report does not attribute those figures to AI, so they are not claimed as an AI benefit here.)

LiveEnterprise-wide

"Artificial Intelligence+" Action Plan (2025-2027) and enterprise agent platform — over 2,500 intelligent agents and assistants

Announced March 2026 (Annual Report 2025, reviewed and approved by the Board on 27 March 2026)

Under its "Artificial Intelligence+" Action Plan (2025-2027), BOCOM built knowledge and agent platforms and deployed over 2,500 agents and assistants. They are used in retail inclusive finance, risk credit granting, customer service and office R&D.

What it is

A bank-wide AI build-out under the AI+ plan, intended to move business models in customer service, operation management and risk control from manual-driven to intelligent and automated. The President's message separately states "over 2,500 AI agents deployed, effectively supporting precision marketing, risk prevention and control, and reducing the burden on grassroots staff."

How it works

The bank built a thousand-card heterogeneous computing power cluster and a hundred-billion-level large model algorithm matrix. It upgraded its unified training and inference platform. It provides model capabilities "out-of-the-box", in the way of "Model as a Service", through the enterprise knowledge and agent platforms. It also jointly built a national AI application pilot base with China UnionPay, Fudan University and other institutions.

Technology used

Thousand-card heterogeneous computing cluster; hundred-billion-level large model algorithm matrix; unified training and inference platform; enterprise-level knowledge platform and agent platform; Model as a Service. No individual model name is named for this deployment in the source.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

The source states the agents effectively supported precision marketing and risk prevention and control and reduced the burden on grassroots staff. No quantified AI benefit figure is given in the source.

Source (Independent reporting): "Bank of Communications Co., Ltd. Annual Report 2025
