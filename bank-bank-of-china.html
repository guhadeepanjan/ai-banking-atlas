AI agent-initiated card payment with Visa Intelligent Commerce (BOCHK as issuer)

Announced May 2026

Visa and Bank of China (Hong Kong) completed a payment made by an AI agent for a cardholder. The test showed an agent can complete purchases while the bank controls consent and authentication.

What it is

This was a completed test of agent-initiated card payments in Hong Kong, with BOCHK taking part as the credit card issuer. It was presented as preparation for agentic commerce, a new form of AI-enabled payment.

How it works

In the demonstration, the AI agent followed a cardholder's instructions to arrange a business trip, selecting a local souvenir for a friend and organising transport to Hong Kong International Airport. It presented suitable options and completed the selected purchases using a BOC Visa credit card, with clear consent and verified authentication at every step.

Technology used

Visa Intelligent Commerce, over Visa's global payments network.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Source (Bank announcement): Visa Advances the Next Phase of Agent-Initiated Card Payments in Hong Kong" (Visa press release)

Remote banking customer service assistant

Announced August 2025

Bank of China launched a remote banking customer service assistant on its new large model platform. The assistant does more than answer questions: it checks service quality, coaches staff and writes up service tickets automatically.

What it is

The bank's 2025 interim report says it "launched a remote banking customer service assistant that achieved over 90% accuracy in typical scenarios". The assistant delivers eight core functions, including intelligent quality inspection, virtual coaching, and automated ticket summarising.

How it works

The assistant runs on the bank's large model platform, which the report says empowers over 100 scenarios across marketing, customer service, operations, compliance, office working and R&D. Its eight functions support the remote banking service operation, including checking the quality of service interactions and summarising tickets.

Technology used

The bank's large model platform, which the report says deploys a series of models such as DeepSeek-R1 and Qwen3.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Over 90% accuracy in typical scenarios, as reported in the source. No cost or time saving was disclosed.

Source (Independent reporting): Bank of China Limited 2025 Interim Results Announcement / 2025 Interim Report" (Bank of China, 29 August 2025), "Financial Technology Innovation" section

Scale BOCAI platform to more than 2,200 intelligent assistants

Announced August 2026

Bank of China has upgraded its BOCAI large model platform and built more than 2,200 intelligent assistants. They cover credit, marketing, operations, risk management, customer service and technology.

What it is

BOCAI is the bank's large model platform, upgraded in 2026 with stronger agent capabilities. Its assistants give comprehensive coverage across front-, middle- and back-office business lines.

How it works

Business teams build intelligent assistants on the platform for tasks such as question answering and report generation. The bank's intelligent research and development assistant alone has over 16,000 users.

Technology used

The BOCAI large model platform.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The bank says its intelligent R&D assistant, with over 10,000 monthly active users, contributes to improved financial technology efficiency.

Source (Independent reporting): Bank of China Limited 2026 Interim Report" (Bank of China, September 2026)

Automate document processing with BOC AI DocuAssist

Announced August 2026

BOC AI DocuAssist automatically identifies and extracts key data from documents. The bank applies AI to recognition and review across 305 document and certificate types. Automation now runs in over 3,800 scenarios.

What it is

BOC AI DocuAssist is the bank's tool for automated document recognition and data extraction. It supports document and certificate review across the bank's operations.

How it works

Multimodal large models and robotic process automation identify documents and extract all key data elements. Automated processes replace repetitive work across more than 3,800 scenarios, executing nearly 300,000 tasks a month.

Technology used

Multimodal large models and robotic process automation (RPA).

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

The bank says DocuAssist significantly improves the operating experience and document entry efficiency.

Source (Independent reporting): Bank of China Limited 2026 Interim Report" (Bank of China, September 2026)

BOCHK GenA.I. Sandbox — AI Avatar and anti-deepfake solution

Announced October 2025

Bank of China (Hong Kong), the bank's Hong Kong subsidiary, tested an AI Avatar and anti-deepfake tool in the HKMA GenA.I. Sandbox. The GenAI work informs customers and helps spot fake documents and faces.

What it is

In the first sandbox cohort, BOCHK tested AI Avatars that give information on banking services, products and promotions through interactive features. It also tested document fraud detection and anti-deepfake facial recognition to assist credit assessment.

How it works

The avatars were further deployed to produce multilingual investment commentary video clips in Cantonese, Mandarin and various Southeast Asian languages, for customers through the BOCHK mobile banking app. The fraud tools help loan approval officers identify material forgeries and AI-generated fraudulent documents.

Technology used

Generative AI, provided in a controlled sandbox environment organised by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) and Cyberport.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

The source states the fraud tools enhance the efficiency of loan application approvals; no figures are given.

Source (Bank announcement): BOCHK enters the second cohort of GenA.I. Sandbox to further explore the advanced applications of GenA.I. and drive digital transformation" (BOCHK press release, October 2025)

Large model platform empowering over 100 business scenarios

Announced August 2025

Bank of China built a large model platform and put it to work across the whole bank. AI has moved from small tests into more than 100 real business scenarios.

What it is

This is the bank's group-wide large model platform, reported in its 2025 interim report. The bank also released an AI application and promotion work plan alongside it.

How it works

Staff use the large model technology inside bank business processes. A separate enterprise robotic process automation (RPA) programme covered over 3,300 scenarios to reduce workload at primary-level institutions.

Technology used

Large models including DeepSeek-R1 and Qwen3, as reported for the 2025 interim platform.
