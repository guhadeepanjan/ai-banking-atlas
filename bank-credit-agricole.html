Builds AI drafting into the Optimtrade trade finance portal with Komgo

Announced September 2026

Crédit Agricole CIB and Komgo are adding AI to Optimtrade, the bank's digital trade finance portal. An AI agent will draft guarantee, letter of credit and standby instructions from documents clients upload.

What it is

Optimtrade launched earlier in 2026 on Komgo's technology, giving Crédit Agricole CIB's corporate clients one place to manage trade finance. The September 2026 announcement adds AI features that are still under development.

How it works

Clients will upload underlying documents, and the AI agent will extract the relevant information automatically. It will then populate a draft instruction for a bank guarantee, letter of credit or standby letter of credit. The pair will also explore natural-language questions about outstanding instruments, exposures and maturities, and access for clients' own AI agents through Model Context Protocol.

Technology used

Komgo's Optimtrade platform, with an AI agent and planned Model Context Protocol access.

The source does not state a benefit.

Payments & Remittances

AnnouncedPayments & Remittances

First agentic payment transaction in production in France (with Worldline and Mastercard)

Announced June 2026

A customer's AI agent bought a festival ticket on the customer's behalf, after the customer confirmed the purchase. It was France's first live payment carried out by an AI agent rather than the customer.

What it is

On 25 June 2026, Worldline, Crédit Agricole and Mastercard completed what they described as the first agentic payment transaction in production in France. The customer's agent searched festivals and bought a Weezevent ticket.

How it works

The payment ran through Mastercard Agent Pay on Worldline's infrastructure. Crédit Agricole, as the card issuer, kept control of customer authentication and payment authorisation.

Technology used

Mastercard Agent Pay; Worldline payment infrastructure; Crédit Agricole as card issuer.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Source (Bank announcement): Worldline, le Crédit Agricole et Mastercard réalisent la première transaction de paiement agentique en France" (Crédit Agricole press release, 25 June 2026)

Crédit Agricole Artificial Intelligence (CA AI) and partnership with Mistral

Announced October 2026 · Went live September 2026

Crédit Agricole created a dedicated group AI company and partnered with Mistral to build the group's AI platform. The group is putting its AI work, and about €500 million of investment, into one industrial-scale programme.

What it is

The group announced Crédit Agricole Artificial Intelligence (CA AI) on 10 June 2026, with €150 million of capital, and launched it in September 2026. On 20 October 2026 it announced a partnership with Mistral to build the group AI platform. The platform includes a customer conversational agent for its retail banks and agentic redesign of business processes.

How it works

CA AI develops and runs AI for the whole group, including Sécur'IA, a platform of AI agents for employees built with Prisme.AI. Sécur'IA succeeds Sécuri'Chat, the group's earlier employee assistant.

Technology used

Mistral AI models under the October 2026 partnership; Prisme.AI for the Sécur'IA employee-agent platform.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. The releases give investment figures (about €500 million under the 2026–2028 IT plan and €150 million of capital for CA AI), not a measured benefit.

Source (Bank announcement): Crédit Agricole joins forces with leading European players to accelerate the industrial deployment of artificial intelligence" (Crédit Agricole press release, 20 October 2026)
