Securities operations AI agent (SocGen AI)

Announced October 2025

An AI agent in production since October 2025 prepares securities operations work for the bank's staff. Staff review the agent's work and keep the decisions.

What it is

The agent was built by SocGen AI, the bank's dedicated AI unit of about 60 people. It works in securities operations — the back-office work behind buying and selling securities for clients.

How it works

An orchestrator routes client messages to specialised agents, which prepare the work. Staff review what the agents produce and make the decisions.

Technology used

SocGen AI's agent platform, with an orchestrator and specialised agents; the underlying models are not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The unit stated the agent's reliability at 84%, as at 14 December 2025 (as reported in the source).

Source (Independent reporting): Pourquoi la Société Générale se transforme en deeptech pour l'IA agentique" (La Revue du Digital, 14 December 2025)

Strategic agreement with Anthropic to roll out Claude

Announced September 2026

Société Générale agreed to roll out Anthropic's Claude models, coding tools and agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own) group-wide. It sits inside the bank's 2026–2029 strategic plan.

What it is

The agreement was presented at the bank's Capital Markets Day on 21 September 2026, as part of its 2026–2029 strategic roadmap. It includes co-development with Anthropic for financial services.

How it works

Claude models and tools are rolled out across the group over time, while the bank keeps responsibility for their integration, the data used and the supervision of operations. The bank's dedicated entity, SocGen AI, coordinates deployment; the group says it already has around 200 AI use cases in production.

Technology used

Anthropic Claude models, coding tools and agentic capabilities.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The bank put the savings expected from its group AI initiatives at EUR 500–600 million by 2029, of which about EUR 350 million was already identified (stated targets in the source, not realised savings).

Source (Bank announcement): Societe Generale presents an ambitious strategic roadmap supported by disciplined execution" (Société Générale press release via GlobeNewswire, 21 September 2026)

Palantir Anti Financial Crime suite deployed in international retail banking

Announced March 2025

Société Générale deployed Palantir's Anti Financial Crime suite in its international retail banking activities. The suite applies advanced analytics and machine learning to detecting money laundering and fraud across borders.

What it is

Palantir announced the successful deployment on 4 March 2025. The suite supports the bank's international retail networks in managing and mitigating financial crime risk.

How it works

Built on Palantir Foundry, the suite combines advanced analytics, machine learning algorithms and comprehensive risk assessment tools. It is designed to detect, prevent and mitigate risks such as money laundering and fraud.

Technology used

Palantir Anti Financial Crime solutions, built on Palantir Foundry.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Source (Bank announcement): Palantir Partners with Societe Generale" (Palantir press release via Business Wire, 4 March 2025)
