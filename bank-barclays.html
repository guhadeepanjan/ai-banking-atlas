Claude models triaging Global Markets client emails

Announced October 2026

Barclays' Global Markets business uses Claude models to read, classify and route about 120,000 client emails a day. Client requests reach the right operations team faster, with less manual sorting by staff.

What it is

On 1 October 2026 Barclays and Anthropic announced an expanded collaboration. Within it, Global Markets teams use Claude models on incoming client emails as part of their daily operations.

How it works

The models classify each email, enrich it with the information operations colleagues need, and determine the best processing route. Requests can then be prioritised and acted on with the required details attached.

Technology used

Anthropic Claude models.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (The announcement describes reduced manual handling in qualitative terms but gives no measured figure for the email work.)

Source (Independent reporting): Barclays Extends Anthropic's Claude Across Engineering, Markets, User Support" (Crowdfund Insider, reporting the Barclays–Anthropic announcement, October 2026)

BARXBot — AI chatbot for FX quotes

Announced February 2026

Barclays launched BARXBot, an AI trading assistant that reads client FX requests from chat and email and returns prices. Salespeople and clients get quotes far faster than through the old manual request-for-quote process.

What it is

BARXBot is an AI-powered assistant on Barclays' BARX platform, launched at the start of 2026. It is used by Barclays' own FX sales staff and is also available to clients, who can interact with BARX FX through it to receive a price directly.

How it works

The bot takes in request-for-quote messages from Bloomberg chat and emails. A large language model interprets the unstructured message, works out which instrument the client wants a price on, and enables the salesperson to initiate the trade quickly.

Technology used

A large language model on the BARX FX platform (the source does not name the model or vendor).

Benefit reported (Measured result)

BARXBot delivers FX quotes 75% faster than before, according to Barclays chief executive C.S. Venkatakrishnan, as reported in the source.

Source (Independent reporting): Next-gen FX: sales and trading enters the AI age" (Risk.net / FX Markets, November 2026)

Adopt FalconTST 2.0 for FX hedging on BARX NetFX

Announced August 2026

Barclays built Ant International's FalconTST 2.0 forecasting model into BARX NetFX, its FX hedging platform. The model predicts currency moves so the platform can set hedges with better timing.

What it is

BARX NetFX is Barclays' FX hedging platform. Adding FalconTST 2.0 gives the platform a dedicated forecasting engine for cash flow and currency exposure.

How it works

The time-series transformer learns patterns from large volumes of historical data. It forecasts cash flow and currency exposure, and the platform uses those forecasts in its hedging workflow.

Technology used

Ant International's FalconTST 2.0, a time-series transformer model, integrated into Barclays' BARX NetFX platform.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Ant International reports forecast accuracy above 93% across FalconTST 2.0 deployments.

Source (Independent reporting): Ant International's FalconTST 2.0 hits top MASE score, adopted by Barclays, Citi, Deutsche Bank and Standard Chartered" (The Asian Banker, August 2026)

Microsoft 365 Copilot rolled out to 100,000 colleagues

Announced June 2025

Barclays agreed to roll out Microsoft 365 Copilot to 100,000 colleagues worldwide, following an initial deployment to 15,000. It puts a generative AI assistant on effectively every desk in the bank.

What it is

Announced jointly by Barclays and Microsoft on 9 June 2025, the rollout extends Microsoft 365 Copilot across the bank's colleague population globally.

How it works

Copilot is integrated into Barclays' own colleague productivity tool, creating a single AI agent through which staff can reach the bank's collaboration platforms, portals and online resources in one place.

Technology used

Microsoft 365 Copilot, integrated with Barclays' in-house colleague productivity tool; Barclays also uses Microsoft Teams and Microsoft Viva Engage.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (Barclays later said, at its February 2026 results, that around 90% of colleagues use Copilot and that it saved more than one million colleague hours in 2025.)

Source (Independent reporting): Barclays rolls out Microsoft Copilot to 100,000 employees as AI adoption gathers pace" (Computer Weekly, 9 June 2025)
