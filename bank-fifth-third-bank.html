AI-powered interface in the mobile app, with the Jeanie assistant

Announced June 2026

Fifth Third's mobile app lets customers type what they need in plain words and go straight to the right place. They no longer hunt through menus to replace a card or move money.

What it is

The AI-powered interface launched on 17 June 2026 in the bank's mobile app, which has 2.4 million monthly users and more than 1 billion digital interactions a year. Depending on the request, the customer is routed to a screen, to the Jeanie chatbot, or to a live representative.

How it works

Language-understanding models interpret what the customer types, such as "replace card" or "transfer funds". The models were trained on hundreds of millions of customer interactions.

Technology used

Fifth Third's language-understanding models and its Jeanie virtual assistant.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The bank said Jeanie's natural language understanding recognises customer intent 90% of the time.

Source (Bank announcement): Fifth Third Launches AI-Powered Interface in Mobile App" (Fifth Third Bank press release via Business Wire, 17 June 2026)

Fifth Third Commercial Card powered by Brex

Announced December 2025

Fifth Third made Brex's AI-powered platform the default behind its commercial card for business clients. AI agents take over receipt capture, transaction matching and spend-policy checks for companies.

What it is

The partnership was announced on 9 December 2025 and covers about US$5.6 billion in annual commercial card volume. The Brex-powered card becomes the default commercial card for the bank's business clients, starting with a pilot in one regional market.

How it works

Brex's AI agents capture receipts, match them to transactions, enforce the company's spend policy and automate approvals. This is designed to speed up the month-end close for business customers.

Technology used

Brex platform and Brex AI agents, on Brex's embedded, API-driven infrastructure.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Source (Bank announcement): Fifth Third and Brex Partner to Bring AI-Powered Finance to Businesses" (press release via Nasdaq, 9 December 2025)

Newline MCP Server for AI agents

Announced December 2025

Fifth Third's Newline platform opened an MCP server so that AI agents can connect securely to its banking services. AI agents can work with the bank's payment and account services through a standard, controlled connection.

What it is

Newline, Fifth Third's embedded payments platform, introduced its MCP Server in December 2025. In April 2026 it added a "Skills" layer, which standardises how AI models use Newline's tools and workflows.

How it works

The server connects AI models and agents to Newline's APIs, data and documentation in a secure way. Chief executive Tim Spence described it as a first among US banks.

Technology used

Model Context Protocol (MCP) server on Fifth Third's Newline platform.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Source (Bank announcement): Fifth Third's Newline Extends MCP Server Capabilities with Skills" (Fifth Third Bank press release via Business Wire, 13 April 2026)
