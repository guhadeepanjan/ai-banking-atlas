Pulse AI — real-time support for client service agents

Announced March 2026

Pulse AI gives Capitec's support agents real-time context on the client in front of them, including declined transactions, outages and account queries. Agents start each conversation already knowing the likely problem.

What it is

Pulse AI is triggered when a client contacts Capitec's support through the app. It pulls together the client's recent activity and likely issue before and during the conversation.

How it works

The system presents the agent with real-time, contextualised client information so the agent can resolve the query faster. CIO Andrew Baker said the aim is to augment agents, not replace them.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

In the bank's three-month test, agents who were initially about 7% slower than the contact-centre average cut their call handling times by up to 18% after adopting Pulse AI, a net performance shift of 26%, as reported in the source.

AI models stop fraud in real time

Announced April 2026

Capitec reported that its AI-driven fraud models protected clients from R673 million in potential losses. The disclosure shows AI already running in live fraud prevention and compliance work, not in pilots.

What it is

The bank's annual report, as reported by Business Day, says AI is used in real-time fraud prevention and risk modelling, and that generative AI is live in its compliance operations. Almost 5,000 employees hold active AI licences.

How it works

The AI-driven models watch transactions in real time to stop fraud before money is lost. The source does not describe the models' internal workings in more detail.

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The AI-driven fraud models protected clients from R673 million in potential losses during the period, and employees with AI licences use the tools about four times a day on average, as reported in the source.
