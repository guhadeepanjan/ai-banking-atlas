IBThink — in-house large language model platform for call-centre staff (QNB Türkiye)

Announced 2025

IBThink is QNB Türkiye's own large language model platform, answering call-centre staff questions in real time during live calls. Agents get product and process answers during the call, instead of escalating to team leaders.

What it is

IBThink is described in the 2025 Integrated Annual Report of QNB Bank A.Ş. (QNB Türkiye), the group's Turkish subsidiary. The call centre it supports works with more than 1,000 agents and handles approximately 28 million inbound calls a year.

How it works

The model generates its responses from training documents that the call-centre teams continuously update and prepare specifically for call-centre employees. Staff experience a natural, human-like interaction rather than traditional chatbot responses.

Technology used

IBThink, QNB Türkiye's in-house large language model platform.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

The report said IBThink has significantly reduced the number of internal support calls escalated to team leaders; no figure is given.

Source (Bank announcement): 2025 Integrated Annual Report" (QNB Bank A.Ş. — QNB Türkiye, 2025)

AI screens sanctions lists and monitors transactions (Group Compliance)

Announced February 2026

QNB's group compliance function deployed a customised AI-driven sanctions screening system and started AI projects in transaction monitoring. Better screening means stronger detection of sanctioned parties with fewer false alarms for staff to clear.

What it is

The work is described in QNB's 2025 Corporate Governance Report, approved at the bank's general assembly on 16 February 2026. The sanctions screening system was implemented with a vendor, which the report does not name.

How it works

The AI-driven system screens against sanctions lists to improve detection accuracy and reduce false positives. Separate projects apply AI and analytics within transaction monitoring.

Technology used

Customised AI-driven sanctions screening system; the vendor is not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

The report said the system improves detection accuracy and reduces false positives; no figures are given.

Source (Bank announcement): Corporate Governance Report 2025" (Qatar National Bank, February 2026)
