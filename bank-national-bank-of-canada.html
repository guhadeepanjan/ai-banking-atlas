Public AI assistant for business clients

Announced November 2026

National Bank launched a conversational AI agent on its website for small, medium and large business clients. Clients can find information at any time, and calls to commercial call agents fell by 25%.

What it is

The public AI assistant extends the bank's agentic AI from internal adviser support to customers: a conversational agent on the bank's website serving businesses of all sizes. It is documented in the AWS case study of the bank's agentic AI programme, published in November 2026.

How it works

Business clients ask questions through the website agent and get answers without calling the bank. For commercial call agents, this translated into a 25% reduction in call volume, while branch clients get more fluid interactions with advisers.

Technology used

Agentic AI built on AWS (Amazon Bedrock with Anthropic's Claude), per the AWS case study of the bank's AI Factory programme.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

A 25% reduction in commercial call volume, as reported in the AWS case study.

Source (Independent reporting): National Bank of Canada Success Story" (Amazon Web Services case study, published November 2026)

Agentic AI assistant for branch advisers (Advice Center)

Announced September 2025

National Bank of Canada gave branch advisers an agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own) assistant instead of a support-centre line. Calls fell, and the home-finance support line was retired in 2025.

What it is

The assistant was built by the bank's AI Factory team. It grew from a 150-adviser pilot to 700 advisers within a month, expanding into a bilingual (French and English) multi-agent system across six banking segments. It now serves as the first point of contact in the bank's contact centre model for adviser support.

How it works

Advisers ask questions in natural language and the multi-agent system retrieves answers from the bank's knowledge, working in both French and English. It absorbs routine inquiries and reduces reliance on phone-based escalation, freeing the contact centre for outbound calls and complex situations.

Technology used

Anthropic's Claude models on Amazon Bedrock, with Amazon OpenSearch for retrieval, built by National Bank's AI Factory on AWS.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

AWS reports a 43% net reduction in calls to the internal support centre, a 97% user satisfaction rate, and 75% of advisers reporting daily time savings; within three months there was also a 10 to 15% net reduction in call-centre volume even as mortgage and business volumes surged about 40 to 50%.

Source (Independent reporting): National Bank of Canada Success Story" (Amazon Web Services case study, 2026) · Second source
