Wealth Intelligence

Announced September 2025

Wealth Intelligence is a generative AI platform that helps HSBC private bankers turn research and news into timely market insights for clients. Advisers can prepare personalised investment strategies faster, starting in Hong Kong and Singapore.

What it is

Wealth Intelligence is HSBC Private Bank's own generative AI-powered ecosystem for client-facing staff, investment counsellors and product specialists. It was launched on 16 September 2025, first in Hong Kong and Singapore, with more markets to follow.

How it works

The platform analyses and summarises HSBC's own research reports and external news feeds, currently covering more than 10,000 data sources. Staff use those summaries to give clients quality market insights and personalised investment strategies.

Technology used

OpenAI's large language model; built by HSBC's in-house developers.

Scale AI Markets and Autohedge for corporate FX hedging

Announced September 2026

HSBC grew its digital FX hedging tools sharply in Europe. Autohedge lets treasurers upload exposures, apply set hedge rules and execute recommended trades from one dashboard, while AI Markets supplies pricing and research.

What it is

Autohedge is HSBC's automated hedging tool for corporate clients. AI Markets began as a natural language analytics tool and has become a treasury platform with real-time pricing, liquidity depth and research.

How it works

Treasurers upload their exposures to Autohedge and apply pre-defined hedge rules. The platform then recommends trades and executes them from the dashboard, with 2025 upgrades adding treasury system connectivity and value-at-risk analytics.

Technology used

HSBC AI Markets (natural language analytics) and the Autohedge automated hedging engine, available through the Evolve platform or by API.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Euromoney reports Autohedge client numbers rose eightfold and platform volumes grew more than 200% year on year. Algorithmic execution among European corporates grew 34%.

Source (Independent reporting): Europe's best FX bank for corporates 2026: HSBC" (Euromoney, 30 September 2026)

Smart Checking reads trade documents with AI

Announced September 2026

HSBC has put Smart Checking to work on documentary trade in the UK, Hong Kong and the UAE. It reads complex trade documents and turns them into structured data for review.

What it is

Smart Checking is an AI tool that standardises how trade finance documents are processed, traced and reviewed. HSBC handles over a million documentary presentations a year, including documentary credits and collections. Each presentation can run to hundreds of pages.

How it works

AI extracts and classifies information from each document, converting inconsistent paperwork into organised, searchable data. Human specialists still judge exceptions and make the decisions that matter. Checking that once took hours can then move faster.

HSBC and Mastercard run a first live agentic B2B payment

Announced June 2026

HSBC and Mastercard have completed a first live agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own) payment in Singapore. The pilot links a corporate buyer, procurement platform SourceSage and supplier FortyTwo.

What it is

The pilot tests agentic commerce, where trusted digital agents automate purchasing and payments for businesses under set controls. It covers the journey from sourcing through to settlement. HSBC completed the first live transaction on 29 May 2026.

How it works

The buyer's agents work through SourceSage, an AI-powered procurement platform, to source and order from FortyTwo. Payment runs on Mastercard Agent Pay, using tokenised payments plus merchant discovery and referral. Juspay's technology stack supports the payment infrastructure.

Technology used

Mastercard Agent Pay, with tokenised payments, running on Juspay's payment infrastructure.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

HSBC and Google Cloud transformative AI banking partnership

Announced June 2026

A multi-year partnership will build and deploy AI across HSBC's global operations, starting with wealth support, financial crime and frontline staff tools. HSBC expects it to enable over 200 AI use cases in two years.

What it is

HSBC and Google Cloud announced the partnership on 17 June 2026 at the Google Cloud Summit in London. The first delivery areas are hyper-personalised wealth management support, stronger financial crime risk management, and AI tools for frontline and relationship manager client service.

How it works

HSBC will work with Google Cloud and Google DeepMind engineering teams on new AI tools and programmes. For financial crime, it will deploy generative and agentic AI to build an architecture that detects risk at an earlier stage.

Technology used

Google Gemini models and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, with Google Cloud and Google DeepMind forward-deployed engineers.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

HSBC estimates the benefit value will exceed $100 million, through either direct revenue gains or wider efficiency improvements, and expects the programme to enable more than 200 new AI use cases over the next two years (both are HSBC's stated expectations in the release, not realised results).

Source (Bank announcement): HSBC and Google Cloud announce transformative AI banking partnership" (HSBC press release, June 2026)

HSBC and Mistral AI strategic partnership for generative AI across the bank

Announced December 2025

HSBC signed a multi-year deal to run Mistral AI's models inside its own systems across the whole bank. The same AI will support client communications, lending analysis, marketing and procurement, not just one team.

What it is

A strategic partnership between HSBC and French AI company Mistral AI to speed up the use of generative AI across the global bank. It gives HSBC access to Mistral's commercial models, including future versions.

How it works

Mistral's models run on a self-hosted basis on HSBC's internal technology systems. HSBC and Mistral's applied AI, science and engineering teams will build solutions together, including for financial analysis of document-heavy lending processes and multilingual translation.

Technology used

Mistral AI commercial large language models (self-hosted).

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. The release states aims, improving business processes and saving employees time, but gives no figure.

Source (Bank announcement): HSBC and Mistral AI join forces to accelerate AI adoption across global bank" (HSBC press release)
