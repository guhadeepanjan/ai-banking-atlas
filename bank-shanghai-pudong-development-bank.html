Corporate Banking

LiveCorporate Banking

AI drafts due diligence reports in corporate credit

Announced March 2026

SPD Bank uses AI to draft pre-lending due diligence reports for corporate loans. Credit staff get much of the report written automatically, with financial statements read by machine.

What it is

The 2025 Annual Report describes AI support in corporate credit for large and medium-sized enterprises. It also covers pre-lending due diligence for micro and small enterprises under the bank's inclusive finance work.

How it works

During customer acquisition the system generates customer profile analyses; in pre-lending due diligence it automatically assesses the rationality of fund transactions and generates reports. For large and medium-sized enterprises, about half of the due diligence report content is generated automatically.

Technology used

The bank's large language models and enterprise knowledge systems; specific models are not named for this use in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The report said financial statement recognition accuracy exceeded 90% and efficiency improved by 80% in corporate credit, and that pre-lending due diligence report preparation for micro and small enterprises became over 60% more efficient.

Source (Independent reporting): 2025 Annual Report" (Shanghai Pudong Development Bank, March 2026)

AI tags and summarises customer calls in remote banking

Announced March 2026

SPD Bank's remote banking service uses AI to tag and summarise customer calls automatically. Agents get call records and summaries without writing them up by hand.

What it is

The bank's 2025 Annual Report describes an AI system for remote banking that combines customer-facing services with operational support. It covers the 95528 hotline and the bank's WeCom workstation used for customer consultations.

How it works

The system automatically tags calls and generates intelligent call summaries to help agents respond. The WeCom workstation answers consultation questions using the bank's knowledge systems.

Technology used

The bank's large language model platform and enterprise knowledge base; specific models are not named for this service in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The report said the 95528 hotline processed over 28 million automated call tags with an accuracy rate of 80%, and produced 10.67 million intelligent call summaries; the WeCom workstation achieved an 88% question-and-answer accuracy rate.

Source (Independent reporting): 2025 Annual Report" (Shanghai Pudong Development Bank, March 2026)

Enterprise-wide

LiveEnterprise-wide

30 large language models run on-premises, including DeepSeek-671B ("AI Plus" programme)

Announced March 2026

SPD Bank deployed 30 large language models on its own premises, including the full-size DeepSeek-671B model. Running inside the bank, the models support more than 200 AI application scenarios across the business.

What it is

In its 2025 Annual Report, the bank said it had completed the on-premises deployment of 30 large language models. It also released an "AI Plus" application planning white paper, and built a high-performance computing cluster of 1,000 GPUs. The work follows China's national "AI Plus" initiative.

How it works

The bank built a large-model application platform and an enterprise knowledge base holding 610 million words of knowledge assets, more than 40,000 knowledge documents and more than 120,000 FAQ entries. Applications run across five areas: intelligent marketing, intelligent operations, efficient operations, digital risk control and intelligent management.

Technology used

30 on-premises large language models including DeepSeek-671B; a 1,000-GPU computing cluster; the bank's large-model application platform and enterprise knowledge base.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The report said the bank had developed more than 200 AI application scenarios, with cumulative large-model calls exceeding 500 million, generating productivity gains equivalent to 300 person-years.

Source (Independent reporting): 2025 Annual Report" (Shanghai Pudong Development Bank, March 2026)
