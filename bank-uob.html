MERaLiON multilingual call intelligence pilot with A*STAR

Announced November 2026

UOB is testing a multilingual AI tool that transcribes and translates client calls in real time. It is built for ASEAN's mix of languages, including Singlish, and supports compliance checks on the same calls.

What it is

The pilot is an initiative under Project AURA. UOB signed a memorandum of understanding with A*STAR's Institute of Advanced Intelligence and Computing to test the MERaLiON model with its relationship managers.

How it works

The tool transcribes and translates speech across 11 ASEAN languages in real time. It prompts relationship managers with the right risk disclosures during a call, and automatically reviews calls afterwards against fair-dealing standards. Relationship managers keep full accountability for client outcomes.

Technology used

MERaLiON, a multilingual language model developed by A*STAR, with support from Singapore's Future of Finance Institute framework.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. The tool is in a testing and exploration phase.

Project AURA — bank-wide AI programme under Enterprise Singapore's Champions of AI

Announced October 2026

Project AURA is UOB's bank-wide AI programme, selected under Enterprise Singapore's Champions of AI scheme. UOB has set a public target of S$900 million in economic value from AI by 2030.

What it is

AURA stands for "Advancing UOB, Reinventing through Advanced AI". It covers 11 initiatives across five priority areas: AML and KYC, wholesale banking credit, retail collections, and relationship-manager support in retail and wholesale banking.

How it works

The bank builds on AI it already runs: more than 300 AI and analytics use cases in production and more than 30 domain-specific chatbots. Around 90 per cent of staff have completed generative AI upskilling, and staff used more than 10 million Copilot prompts in the first six months. An Enterprise Intelligence Hub is planned.

Technology used

Microsoft 365 Copilot is named in the source; the other models and platforms are not specified.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. The S$900 million figure is a target for 2030, not a result achieved.

Source (Independent reporting): UOB Targets S$900M in AI Value by 2030 Under Singapore Programme" (Fintech News Singapore, 27 October 2026)

GenAI and agentic AI collaboration with Accenture

Announced April 2025

UOB signed a three-year agreement with Accenture to use generative and agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own) across the bank. It was a first for Accenture with a Singapore bank.

What it is

The memorandum of understanding, signed on 30 April 2025, covers using advanced technologies including generative AI and agentic AI. The aim is to improve business processes, productivity and risk management, and to personalise customer experience.

How it works

UOB uses Accenture's AI Refinery platform to build and run the solutions, and Accenture's LearnVantage training service to upskill staff.

Technology used

Accenture AI Refinery platform and Accenture LearnVantage; the underlying AI models are not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.
