HELIOS: agentic AI for wealth customer due diligence

Announced July 2026

HELIOS carries out wealth clients' due-diligence checks before a relationship manager gets involved. It is agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own), and people still make the final approval.

What it is

HELIOS (Holistic wEalth Lifecycle Insights & Ongoing Surveillance) was announced on 29 July 2026. It front-loads wealth customer due diligence. Relationship managers at Bank of Singapore in Singapore, Hong Kong and Dubai were already using it, with rollout completing in the third quarter of 2026.

How it works

The platform does the early due-diligence work and maps a client's relationship networks before the relationship manager engages. Human staff keep the final approval on decisions.

Technology used

Agentic AI platform built by OCBC; the source does not name a model or vendor.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Account opening in 15 business days, compared with an industry median of about six weeks, according to the bank.

Source (Bank announcement): OCBC rolls out agentic AI platform for wealth customer due diligence" (OCBC press release via The Asian Banker, 29 July 2026)

Bank of Singapore Source of Wealth Assistant (SOWA)

Announced October 2025

SOWA drafts source-of-wealth reports for Bank of Singapore's private banking clients. It is agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own), and it cuts the work from days to about an hour.

What it is

SOWA is an agentic AI assistant embedded in the bank's know-your-customer process, announced on 10 October 2025. It prepares the source-of-wealth reports that explain where a client's money came from.

How it works

The assistant extracts, structures and synthesises information from client documents, flags gaps, and drafts standardised source-of-wealth reports. It also checks the story for plausibility against benchmarks such as salary and company revenue, and it runs on the bank's private cloud.

Technology used

Agentic AI running on OCBC's private cloud; the source does not name a model or vendor.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Report preparation time was cut from up to 10 days to one hour, according to the bank.

Source (Bank announcement): Bank of Singapore deploys agentic AI to automate source of wealth reporting" (Bank of Singapore press release via The Asian Banker, 10 October 2025)

AI Copilot for corporate relationship managers

Announced March 2026

OCBC gave its corporate relationship managers an AI copilot that reads long documents and produces ESG, financial and industry analysis. The tool speeds up the analysis work behind corporate credit decisions.

What it is

The copilot was disclosed in OCBC's Annual Report 2025, published in March 2026, and was in use during 2025. It supports relationship managers who must review long, unstructured documents as part of credit assessment.

How it works

The copilot generates ESG, financial and industry analysis from the documents a relationship manager is reviewing. The annual report does not describe the tool's internal workings in more detail.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Preparation time was reduced by up to 30%, according to the annual report.

Source (Bank announcement): Driving customer centricity through AI, digital and data" (OCBC Annual Report 2025, March 2026)
