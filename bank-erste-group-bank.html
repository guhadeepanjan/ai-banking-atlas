AI flags suspicious payments and warns clients in George (Česká spořitelna)

Announced March 2026

A new algorithm in George slows down suspicious payments and shows a red warning screen before the client confirms. It steps in just as a manipulated client is about to send money to a fraudster.

What it is

Česká spořitelna announced the results on 26 March 2026. All payments in George are screened by an AI-based detection system, and the new algorithm adds the warning screen. The screen explains the most common scams, such as fake investments and fake bankers or police officers; it was extended to web banking in February 2026.

How it works

The detection system flags payment behaviour that does not match the customer's profile or that matches known fraud patterns. When a payment looks like cyber fraud, George slows the sending process and shows the red warning screen before the payment completes.

Technology used

AI-based payment detection system and cybersecurity algorithm in the George app; the vendor is not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The bank said client losses from cyber fraud fell by 40%, more than CZK 21 million, from October 2025, after the algorithm was introduced.

Hey George — AI adviser in the George app (Česká spořitelna)

Announced December 2024

Hey George is an AI adviser inside the George mobile app, first piloted at Česká spořitelna in late 2024. It gives retail clients personalised financial advice based on their own account data, at any time.

What it is

Erste Group's 2024 annual report describes Hey George as an AI-powered digital advisor that delivers data-driven, highly personalised financial recommendations. After a successful pilot with 10,000 customers in 2024, the bank said the service would be made available to all Česká spořitelna clients during 2025. Euromoney later reported a wider group rollout in partnership with OpenAI, including to Romania.

How it works

Clients chat with the assistant in natural language inside the George app. It works with the client's own data to answer questions and give personalised advice and recommendations, for example on spending and savings.

Technology used

OpenAI GPT-4o, per Česká spořitelna's launch reporting.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

Generative AI checks call-centre calls for quality and compliance (Česká spořitelna)

Announced December 2024

Česká spořitelna tested generative AI to check the quality and compliance of call-centre calls automatically. The call centre handles about 2 million calls a year, far more than human reviewers can listen to.

What it is

The work was carried out with DataSentics on the Databricks platform and described in a Databricks case study published on 18 December 2024. It covers automated checking of agent calls against the bank's compliance requirements.

How it works

Calls are transcribed with speech-to-text, and large language models then act as automated checkers of the conversation. The team tested OpenAI's GPT models and open-source models such as Mixtral in the checker role.

Technology used

Databricks Data + AI Platform; OpenAI Whisper for transcription; GPT and Mixtral models tested as checkers; built with DataSentics.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Source (Independent reporting): Databricks blog case study on Česká spořitelna's generative AI call-centre work (Databricks, 18 December 2024)
