Selects Traydstream to automate trade document checking

Announced October 2026

Mizuho Bank has selected Traydstream's TraydCheck and TraydConnect for its trade finance digitisation programme. The move means all three Japanese megabanks now use the same document checking platform.

What it is

Mizuho Bank, the banking arm of Mizuho Financial Group, chose the two Traydstream products in October 2026. TraydCheck reviews trade finance documentation, while TraydConnect links the bank digitally with its corporate customers.

How it works

TraydCheck uses intelligent automation to streamline the review of trade documents. IBS Intelligence reports it reduces manual processing, improves consistency and speeds up transaction turnaround.

Technology used

Traydstream TraydCheck and TraydConnect.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Wiz Base platform launches with RM Studio and sales-support agents

Announced December 2025

Mizuho released Wiz Base, its governed platform for building AI agents, and switched on its first sales-support tools, including RM Studio, which drafts proposal slides for relationship managers.

What it is

Mizuho Financial Group released Wiz Base on 1 December 2025 and began large-scale proofs of concept in the sales support domain ahead of full-scale use. The platform is designed around Mizuho's own "AI Oriented Architecture" (AIOA) rules for reliability, auditability and authority control. The first in-house components running on it are RM Studio, the meeting record and minutes agent "Menkikun", Wiz Search, the Mizuho AI Interviewer and Mizuho DeepResearch. Together they support relationship managers from proposal preparation through delivery to follow-up.

How it works

Wiz Base runs on Amazon Web Services and connects several large language models, including Anthropic's Claude, through Amazon Bedrock. Multiple agents can therefore work together on a task inside the platform's controls. RM Studio analyses dialogue history and proposal content, suggests next steps and proposal strategies, and creates the slides. The minutes agent records and summarises face-to-face and online meetings. Wiz Search answers staff questions from internal documents in administrative and credit areas. The AI Interviewer captures experienced bankers' know-how as training data for the bank's knowledge base and its own LLM.

Technology used

Wiz Base on Amazon Web Services with Amazon Bedrock (including Anthropic Claude) and other LLMs in a multi-model setup; Mizuho's proprietary LLM under construction.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

Mizuho said it aims to at least double the productivity of sales activities with Wiz Base and its components; this is a stated aim, not a measured result. The bank also said effects such as faster proposals and better knowledge sharing had begun to appear in several departments, with no figures given.

Source (Bank announcement): Mizuho Releases Generative AI-powered Platform 'Wiz Base' for Sales Support" (Mizuho Financial Group press release, 1 December 2025, reproduced by Japan FinTech Observer)
