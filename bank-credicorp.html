Clara — BCP's generative AI assistant for telephone and WhatsApp banking

Announced December 2024

Clara, BCP's generative AI assistant, has handled telephone banking conversations since 10 December 2024. BCP was first in Peru to put generative AI into phone banking; Clara is now its main WhatsApp assistant.

What it is

Clara is run by BCP's contact centre, where the bank's Conversational Automation team sits. In its first stage it handles account statements, account opening, transfers in process and payment status.

How it works

Unlike the older menu-based bot, generative AI Clara understands free-form questions and the context of the conversation, according to BCP's contact-centre AI manager Priscila Vásquez in the source. It serves clients on the phone channel and on WhatsApp.

Technology used

Generative AI; BCP's implementation is described in Twilio's case study of the bank (see second source).

Benefit reported (Measured result)

According to Twilio's case study of BCP, 67% of WhatsApp inquiries are resolved by Clara with no human agent, the automation level doubled in one year, net promoter score rose by more than 20 points, and WhatsApp grew from about 20% to 43% of contact-centre interactions (vendor-published figures; see the research notes).
