Agentic AI mortgage assistant

Announced June 2026

ING rolled out an AI agent that helps process mortgage applications in the Netherlands. The agent does the document checking and analysis, while an ING employee still makes every final decision.

What it is

The assistant was piloted in the Netherlands in March 2026, and its scaled rollout was announced on 10 June 2026. It supports applications that need manual assessment.

How it works

The agent gathers and checks documents, analyses the application, explains possible outcomes and suggests next steps. An ING employee makes the final decision on every application.

Live end-to-end agentic payment with Worldline and Mastercard

Announced June 2026

An ING customer's AI assistant found concert tickets within budget and paid after the customer approved. The partners call it Europe's first live end-to-end AI-agent payment in a production environment.

What it is

The transaction was completed on 2 June 2026 between an ING cardholder and a merchant in the Netherlands, on infrastructure that also operates across Belgium. It was announced at Money 20/20 by Worldline, ING and Mastercard.

How it works

The merchant's AI agent identified concert tickets within the customer's defined budget and presented a selection; the payment completed only after the customer gave explicit approval. ING, as the issuing bank, authenticated and authorised the payment, and Worldline processed it end to end.

Technology used

Mastercard Agent Pay; Worldline issuing and acquiring platforms.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Generative AI customer chatbot live in seven countries

Announced January 2026

ING's generative AI chatbot now handles customer contact in seven countries. Most customer chats are resolved without a person, while customers can still ask for a human at any time.

What it is

As reported in January 2026, ING's generative AI chatbot is live in customer contact across seven countries. They are the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, Spain, Italy, Romania and Australia, according to ING's 2025 reporting. It runs on ING's central AI platform.

How it works

The chatbot answers customer chats and resolves most of them on its own. Customers can request a person at any point in the conversation.

Technology used

Google technology on ING's central AI platform.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

About 75% of customer chats were resolved without a human in 2025, and more than 80% in four countries, as reported in the source.

Source (Independent reporting): Interview: How ING reaps benefits of centralising AI" (Computer Weekly, 13 January 2026)
