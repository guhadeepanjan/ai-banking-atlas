Starts inheritance proof of concept under AGI alliance with Matsuo Institute and Third Intelligence

Announced November 2026

MUFG Bank has signed a comprehensive AI partnership with Matsuo Institute and Third Intelligence. A proof of concept in inheritance services has already started, testing an AI that remembers context across conversations.

What it is

The alliance, signed on 17 November 2026, covers research and development of Third Intelligence's Ubiquitous AGI for financial services. The partners also plan finance-specific AI models and AGI-equipped products for digital front-office banking.

How it works

The inheritance proof of concept tests whether the system can understand each family's circumstances through ongoing dialogue. The aim is an AI concierge that organises inheritance preparations and links families to MUFG's services. Matsuo Institute separately provides AI advisory services and talent development programmes to MUFG.

Technology used

Third Intelligence's Ubiquitous AGI, a next-generation model using an algorithm distinct from mainstream Transformer architectures, designed for long-term memory, continuous learning and inference speed.

Links customer accounts to ChatGPT through Moneytree

Announced May 2026

MUFG Bank and Moneytree have rolled out a service that links customer accounts to ChatGPT. Users can check balances and review categorised spending by asking questions in plain language.

What it is

The service embeds Moneytree LINK, Moneytree's financial data infrastructure, into OpenAI's Apps in ChatGPT. It is a concrete customer-facing step under the retail AI strategy MUFG unveiled in November 2025.

How it works

Existing Moneytree users link their accounts inside ChatGPT using their current credentials. They then check balances and view categorised spending logs through natural-language prompts, instead of switching between banking apps. Moneytree says it limits the data accessed to essential financial records only.

Technology used

OpenAI's Apps in ChatGPT, connected through Moneytree LINK.

Sakana AI partnership — AI automation of complex banking documents (credit approval work)

Announced May 2025

MUFG signed a partnership of more than three years with Japanese AI research firm Sakana AI to automate complex banking documents. First use: credit documents needing expert knowledge, core corporate-lending work.

What it is

A long-term strategic technology partnership between MUFG Bank and Sakana AI. Sakana AI co-founder and COO Ren Ito was also retained as MUFG's AI Advisor. Japan Times reporting describes the work as automating banking documents including credit approval memos, with a pilot phase starting in July 2025.

How it works

MUFG will first apply the expertise behind Sakana AI's "The AI Scientist" to create internal and external documents that require complex thinking and advanced specialised knowledge. "The AI Scientist" is a system originally built to automate scientific research and writing. Human bankers remain the decision-makers; the AI drafts the documents.

Technology used

Sakana AI: "The AI Scientist" expertise / technology

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (Press reporting put a value on the deal itself, ¥5 billion / ~US$34 million per Japan Times, but that is contract value, not a stated benefit.)

Strategic alliance with Google — agentic commerce / agentic payments for retail customers

Announced May 2026

MUFG and Google agreed a retail alliance to build AI-agent services covering shopping, payments and household budgeting. The goal is infrastructure on Google Cloud that lets an agent support a purchase from selection through payment.

What it is

A strategic partnership in the retail sector, described by MUFG under three pillars: advanced customer experience with AI agents, data-driven marketing, and new value from finance × non-finance integration. Proof-of-concept trials were planned within fiscal 2026, starting with limited functions.

How it works

Based on conditions the user sets in advance, the AI agent searches shopping sites for matching products. It checks the user's spending position and available payment methods. It then proposes the best payment method (e.g. card, debit or QR payment), and aims eventually to execute it. Google Cloud supplies the cloud and AI technology, technical advice and development support.

Technology used

Google / Google Cloud AI technology (specific model not named in MUFG's release)

Speech-based call routing with a generative AI agent (NTT Docomo Business)

Announced December 2025

MUFG Bank introduced "speech-based routing": a generative AI agent listens to callers and connects them to the right operator. Callers say what they need in their own words instead of working through a keypad menu.

What it is

NTT Docomo Business announced on 16 December 2025 that it had begun providing MUFG Bank with a call centre solution. In the solution, a generative AI agent analyses the caller's speech in real time and connects the caller to the most suitable operator. It applies to part of the bank's toll-free (free-dial) menu.

How it works

The system analyses what the caller says as the call comes in and routes it by subject. MUFG Bank and NTT Docomo Business ran a proof of concept from March to July 2024. In it, the AI learned the bank's own terminology and accumulated data, improving routing accuracy. For inquiries that are hard to pin down, a jointly developed generative AI learning and routing technique (patent pending) keeps improving the routing flow.

Technology used

NTT TechnoCross "CTBASE/Intelligent Router" (a routing platform using generative AI) and "VoiceMall" (an automated voice response platform using speech recognition and synthesis), provided by NTT Docomo Business.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

The release states the expected effects qualitatively: improved customer satisfaction, more efficient call centre operations, and preventing work from concentrating on a few operators. No figures were given.

Source (Bank announcement): Generative AI agent call centre solution launched for a financial institution — speech-based routing" (NTT Docomo Business press release, 16 December 2025, in Japanese)

Cuts procedure flowchart work by 90 percent with AI Flowchart

Announced October 2026

MUFG Bank's global operations team has put AI Flowchart into production across its overseas branches. The tool drafts and compares branch procedure flowcharts, work that underpins standardising operations in about 30 countries.

What it is

AI Flowchart is a custom application built by MUFG Bank's Global Operations Planning Division. It accelerates the system-development work behind standardising branch procedures, which previously took about 11 person-days per branch procedure.

How it works

An AI agent first queries a knowledge graph of each branch's procedures, stored against a shared ontology. The graph constrains what the language model can output, and each generated step cites the graph nodes behind it. Experts then review and revise the flowcharts instead of authoring them from scratch.

Technology used

Amazon Bedrock foundation models, Amazon Neptune knowledge graphs, an RDF/OWL ontology with HermiT reasoning, and a Strands Agents based agent, in a serverless AWS application.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

AWS reports total effort per branch procedure fell from 11 person-days to 1 person-day, a cut of up to 90 percent. Flowchart creation alone fell 95 percent, and comparisons dropped from half a person-day to minutes.

Source (Independent reporting): How MUFG Bank aims to cut flowchart work by up to 90% with ontology-grounded AI on Amazon Neptune and Amazon Bedrock" (AWS Database Blog, October 2026)

ChatGPT Enterprise rollout under strategic collaboration with OpenAI

Announced November 2025 (rollout from January 2026) · Went live January 2026

MUFG moved from a trial begun in October 2024 to a full strategic collaboration with OpenAI. From January 2026, about 35,000 MUFG Bank employees get ChatGPT Enterprise, with "AI Champions" trained in each area.

What it is

A bank-wide generative AI deployment aimed at making MUFG an "AI-native company." It also covers new retail customer experiences under MUFG's "Emut" brand. These include an AI Concierge in MUFG group apps and an account-opening AI chat ("Emut Quick Start"). They also include a possible link to Apps in ChatGPT, and support for agentic commerce payments.

How it works

Employees use ChatGPT Enterprise in daily operations, supported by a joint project team, training programmes and quarterly strategic reviews across strategy, products and customer services. MUFG gets early access to OpenAI's latest models and features, and co-creates customer-facing services with OpenAI specialists.

Technology used

OpenAI: ChatGPT Enterprise; latest GPT models (specific model version not named in the release)
