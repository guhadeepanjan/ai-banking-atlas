BOCHK GenAI Assistant for corporate banking staff

Announced March 2026

BOCHK put a generative AI assistant into full production for its corporate banking staff, its first ever GenAI project. Corporate bankers get instant, contextual answers from complex financial documents instead of reading everything by hand.

What it is

The BOCHK GenAI Assistant is a GenAI-powered tool that gives corporate banking staff instant insights through a conversational interface. It was the bank's first GenAI project and moved from a successful proof of concept into full production.

How it works

Staff ask questions in a chat-style interface and the tool returns contextual insights drawn from complex financial documents, helping them assess corporate client relationships and risks. The bank's AI Committee oversees more than 100 AI use cases across the bank.

Technology used

Generative AI (BOCHK GenAI Assistant); the underlying model is not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. No usage or time-saving figures were published.

Agentic AI for BoC Pay+ in the GenA.I. Sandbox++

Announced August 2026

BOCHK and BOC Life joined the GenA.I. Sandbox++ to test agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own) in the BoC Pay+ app. The tests cover agents that recommend spending and complete payments.

What it is

BOCHK's intelligent consumption use case will explore integrating its mobile payment app BoC Pay+ with partner platforms including Visa Intelligent Commerce, China Mobile Hong Kong and dining platform OpenRice. The six-month proof of concept covers personalised credit card management, spending recommendations and intelligent payment services.

How it works

Agentic AI is tested inside the Sandbox++, a joint initiative of the HKMA, the Securities and Futures Commission, the Insurance Authority, the MPFA and Cyberport. Inside it, the bank can validate the feasibility and security of agent-based payments before any wider launch.

Technology used

Agentic AI; Visa Intelligent Commerce is named as a partner platform in the source. No model vendor is named.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. This is a proof-of-concept selection, with no results published yet.

AI Voice Customer Service tested in the HKMA GenA.I. Sandbox

Announced October 2026

BOCHK validated an AI voice customer service system that speaks Hong Kong's major languages. It understands the mixed Chinese-English speech customers use, and testing showed AI can also spot synthetic (fake) voices used in fraud.

What it is

The AI Voice Customer Service was one of two applications BOCHK validated in the second cohort of the Hong Kong Monetary Authority's GenA.I. Sandbox. The Sandbox is a controlled testing programme run with Cyberport. It is designed to handle spoken customer inquiries fluently.

How it works

The system handles Hong Kong's major spoken languages with a high degree of fluency and accurately recognises mixed Chinese-English speech patterns common in daily conversation. During testing, the bank also validated using AI to identify synthetic voices in voice-based interactions, strengthening anti-fraud defences.

Technology used

Generative AI (GenA.I.), tested in the HKMA and Cyberport GenA.I. Sandbox; no model vendor is named in the source.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. The release reports successful technical validation, with no customer or cost figures.
