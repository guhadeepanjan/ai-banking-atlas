Kotak AI — in-house generative AI platform

Announced September 2025

Kotak Mahindra Bank built its generative AI platform, Kotak AI, used for credit analysis, staff queries and coding. About 12 large language models run inside its own firewall, rather than coming from outside vendors.

What it is

Kotak AI is a proprietary generative AI platform described by the bank as the cognitive core of its digital ecosystem. Its architecture has a model layer of 12 large language models from multiple providers, and a router layer that picks the most suitable model for each task. It also has a library of specialised AI "skills" that agents can orchestrate to complete end-to-end tasks.

How it works

The models are curated, fine-tuned and anchored on the bank's own data so queries do not cross the bank's firewall. Credit analysts use it to process large amounts of data faster while staying in the loop. Frontline staff can query internal processes in natural language, and developers use it for coding assistance.

Technology used

Kotak AI platform: 12 large language models from multiple providers, with a model router and an AI skills library, built in-house by Kotak Mahindra Bank.

Kompanion — conversational AI assistant for frontline and sales staff

Announced September 2025

Kompanion is Kotak's conversational AI assistant for frontline and sales staff, built on the bank's Kotak AI platform. Staff can ask about internal processes in natural language instead of searching manuals or calling support teams.

What it is

Kompanion is a conversational AI assistant for the bank's frontline and sales staff, named in Kotak's 2024-25 annual report alongside "Koder", an AI-driven development tool for software engineers. Both sit on the bank's proprietary AI platform.

How it works

Staff query internal processes in natural language and get answers grounded in the bank's own data and documents. The bank's technology leadership has said the AI recommends and the human acts, keeping people in control of decisions.

Technology used

Kotak AI platform (in-house; 12 large language models with a routing layer).
