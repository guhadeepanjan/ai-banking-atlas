AI agents handle customer inquiries on Platform V Flow

Announced September 2026

Sber has put a team of AI agents to work on written customer inquiries. Its GigaChat language model reads free-text requests, so inquiries staff once processed by hand are now handled automatically, end to end.

What it is

SberTech, the bank's technology arm, announced on 23 September 2026 that Sber had automated the handling of customer inquiries using a multi-agent system built on Platform V Flow and GigaChat. More than 90 processes for handling different kinds of customer inquiries have been automated on the platform.

How it works

Platform V Flow organises the work and connects the agents. Earlier versions followed fixed, pre-set rules. Since 2025 the GigaChat model reads the customer's free-text message, understands what is being asked, and agents with different roles then carry out the steps needed to resolve it.

Technology used

SberTech Platform V Flow (low-code process platform) and Sber's GigaChat large language model.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (The source says the agents were built to speed up inquiry processing and reduce labour costs, but gives no measured figure.)

Sber open-sources the GigaChat Ultra Preview and Lightning models

Announced November 2025

Sber publicly released the weights of two new flagship GigaChat models, the largest open-source AI release in Europe. Any company can now run them in-house and fine-tune them on private data, free of charge.

What it is

In November 2025 Sber opened the weights of GigaChat Ultra Preview and GigaChat Lightning, two Mixture-of-Experts language models trained from scratch for Russian-language tasks. The release also included the GigaAM-v3 speech recognition models, the Kandinsky 5.0 image and video generation family, and the K-VAE 1.0 visual encoders, all under the MIT licence.

How it works

The models are published with their weights and inference acceleration techniques. Outside developers and companies can run and adapt them on their own infrastructure, including fully offline inside closed systems.

Technology used

GigaChat Ultra Preview and GigaChat Lightning (MoE large language models), GigaAM-v3 speech recognition, Kandinsky 5.0, K-VAE 1.0.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.
