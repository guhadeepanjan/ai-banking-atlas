"Apo" large model completes internal testing; nine AI agent projects under way

Announced July 2026

Bank of Ningbo's own large model, "Apo", completed internal testing with more than 5,000 employees in July 2026. It is positioned as an AI financial assistant for both staff and customers.

What it is

Mobile Payment Network reported on 5 August 2026, citing China Fund News, that the bank's head-office large model project "Apo" had finished employee testing. The testing covered front, middle and back office departments, and the model was ready for customer-facing use. For staff it supports office work and marketing; for customers it offers knowledge education, account services, product consultation, function guidance and market information.

How it works

Since September 2025, the bank and its subsidiaries have issued nine tender announcements for AI agents covering the corporate banking workflow. They include early-warning agents, due diligence agents, a financial large-model AI audit agent and a corporate mobile banking agent.

Technology used

The bank's self-developed "Apo" large model and related AI agents; the underlying base model is not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Agentar KBase knowledge-engineering platform with Ant Digital

Announced October 2025

Bank of Ningbo and Ant Digital built an intelligent decision-making system on the Agentar Knowledge Engineering Platform (KBase). Staff get accurate, explainable answers from the bank's own knowledge instead of searching documents by hand.

What it is

The joint case was selected as a "2025 International Standard Financial Application Excellence Case" at the 2025 Financial Street Forum on 29 October 2025. The system combines a knowledge processing and management platform, a logical reasoning engine and intelligent application scenarios. It is used in scenarios such as market analysis, product interpretation, conversation training and report writing.

How it works

The platform manages multi-source data from inside and outside the bank across its full lifecycle. Its knowledge-enhanced generation engine uses a "planning – retrieval – reasoning" mechanism and links knowledge graphs with original texts. It deepens reasoning from one hop to 3–5 hops, and can show the reasoning path and data sources behind an answer.

Technology used

Ant Digital's Agentar Knowledge Engineering Platform (KBase), with knowledge graphs and a knowledge-enhanced generation engine.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Evaluation results showed the accuracy of complex Q&A rose from 68% to 91%, content recommendation accuracy increased by 35%, and the recall rate increased by 40%, with response speeds at the hundred-millisecond level, according to the case description.

Source (Independent reporting): Ant Financial Agentar Builds a Financial AI Brain and Is Selected as an Excellence Case in International Standards" (AIbase News, October 2025)
