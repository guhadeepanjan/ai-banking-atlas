AI agent supporting customer enquiries, on a new bot platform

Announced September 2026

In the third quarter of 2026, PKO Bank Polski put its first AI agent process to work on customer enquiries. Its bots already handle millions of conversations, and agents go beyond scripted replies.

What it is

The bank's report for the third quarter of 2026 says the first AI-agent process supporting customer enquiries was implemented in that quarter. The bank also implemented SentiOne Automate, a new bot-building platform offering greater scope for advanced bots, particularly those using generative AI. In the same quarter the bank launched eight bots and chatbots.

How it works

The bots serve customers across the bank's channels, and the AI agent supports the handling of incoming customer enquiries. The new platform is used to build more advanced, generative-AI-based bots.

Technology used

SentiOne Automate bot platform; generative AI.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

In the third quarter of 2026, all the bank's bots handled nearly 6.9 million conversations, and by 30 September 2026 they had handled over 104 million conversations in total, up 25% year on year, according to the bank's quarterly report.

Source (Independent reporting): Report of the PKO Bank Polski S.A. Group for the Third Quarter of 2026" (PKO Bank Polski, 2026)

AI agent classifies incoming customer emails

Announced August 2026

PKO Bank Polski deployed an AI agent that reads incoming customer emails, classifies their topics and proposes how each should be handled. Sorting mail by hand is slow; faster triage means customers get answers sooner.

What it is

The agent was built by the bank's hyperautomation team with support from its AI Competence Centre, and was reported by the bank on 5 August 2026. Employees retain responsibility for key decisions.

How it works

The agent reads each incoming email, classifies its topic and proposes the further handling, so staff can act on pre-sorted correspondence instead of reading and routing every message manually.

Technology used

The bank's internally built AI agent; the underlying model is not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The bank claimed up to 70% faster customer handling following deployment of the email agent; it did not disclose the measurement method behind the claim.

Source (Independent reporting): PKO Bank Polski: AI agent for incoming customer-email classification" (AI Field Notes, cataloguing PKO Bank Polski's own report of 5 August 2026)

Employees get AI assistants (szukAI, Copilot Chat, Document Analyser) and Polish language models built in-house

Announced March 2026

In 2025 PKO Bank Polski rolled out AI assistants for employees, including the "szukAI" knowledge assistant, and built its own Polish-language models. Staff can search bank documents and rules in plain language.

What it is

The bank's Directors' Report for 2025 (published March 2026) describes the tools implemented under its AI Competence Centre. They include the general-purpose Copilot Chat assistant and "szukAI", which searches the bank's documents and internal regulations in natural language. SzukAI supports areas including AML and treasury products. The tools also include a Programmer Assistant for code and a Document Analyser using OCR and generative AI. Twenty thousand employees completed basic AI training.

How it works

Employees ask questions in natural language and the assistants search information, create content and organise work. In research and development, the bank created the RoBERTa 8K model specialised in Polish and financial data, released openly on Hugging Face. It also created two generative models with 1 billion and 24 billion parameters optimised for Polish and finance.

Technology used

Copilot Chat; the bank's own szukAI assistant; OCR with generative AI; in-house Polish language models (RoBERTa 8K; generative models of 1B and 24B parameters); MLOps/LLMOps platforms and an AI Gateway.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Source (Bank announcement): The PKO Bank Polski S.A. Group Directors' Report for 2025" (PKO Bank Polski, March 2026), section 1.3.6 "Artificial Intelligence at the Bank
