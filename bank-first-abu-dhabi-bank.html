Agentic AI workflows for trade document processing

Announced October 2026

FAB uses AI-enabled workflows to check and process trade documents, now handling most of its trade volumes. Paperwork that once took days of manual checking is turned around far faster for importers and exporters.

What it is

FAB disclosed the deployment with its nine-month 2026 results on 29 October 2026. The trade work is one of more than 30 agentic AI use cases the bank is progressing across trade, payments, client operations, compliance and technology engineering. These are coordinated by its AI Innovation Hub.

How it works

Autonomous AI workflows validate and process trade documents end to end. They sit on the bank's modern enterprise data platform, into which more than 90% of its structured data has been integrated under an agentic AI layer.

Technology used

FAB's agentic AI layer on its enterprise data platform, developed through the bank's AI Innovation Hub (the disclosure does not name the specific models used for trade documents).

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Trade-document processing turnaround times were reduced by over 70%, as disclosed by FAB and reported in the source.

Source (Bank announcement): FAB cuts trade-document processing times by over 70%, BBVA reports 28% developer productivity gain" (The Asian Banker, November 2026, reporting FAB's disclosure of 29 October 2026)

IBM watsonx platform at FABMISR (Egypt)

Announced October 2026

FABMISR, FAB's Egyptian subsidiary, has put an AI platform into production on its own premises, including a knowledge agent and a financial analyst agent. Data stays inside the bank's own walls, under its direct control.

What it is

FABMISR announced on 18 October 2026 that it had implemented an enterprise-grade AI platform using IBM's watsonx portfolio within its on-premises infrastructure. It was delivered with IBM Client Engineering and IBM Expert Labs. Production-ready solutions include a retrieval-augmented generation agent for enterprise knowledge and a Financial Analyst AI agent.

How it works

watsonx.ai supports the development and deployment of AI models and agents for the bank's use cases, while watsonx Orchestrate provides secure knowledge access, agent integration and workflow automation. All workloads and data stay inside the bank's controlled environment.

Technology used

IBM watsonx portfolio (watsonx.ai and watsonx Orchestrate), run on-premises.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Source (Bank announcement): FABMISR advances enterprise AI deployment with IBM watsonx" (IBM Newsroom, Middle East & Africa, 18 October 2026)

LiveEnterprise-wide

Enterprise AI scale-up through the AI Innovation Hub

Announced February 2026

FAB has moved AI from experiments into production across the whole group, with Microsoft 365 Copilot for all employees and agents embedded in departments. One coordinated programme with shared governance replaces scattered pilots.

What it is

In a press release on 17 February 2026, FAB said its AI Innovation Hub provides governance, reusable capabilities and a structured pipeline to scale high-impact use cases across the bank. AI agents are embedded across departments to automate routine activities, surface insights and support decision-making.

How it works

The programme runs on a modern enterprise data platform with more than 90% of the bank's structured data integrated and supported by an agentic AI layer. More than 30 agentic AI use cases are progressing across trade and payments, client operations, compliance and technology engineering.

Technology used

Microsoft 365 Copilot (rolled out to all employees in 2025, with a library of more than 1,000 Copilot agents by the end of that year) and FAB's agentic AI layer on its enterprise data platform.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (FAB later reported, in its H1 2026 disclosures, over 20% productivity uplift and 70 to 80% less manual effort in key workflows, and by October 2026 more than 6,000 employee-developed agents in use.)

Source (Bank announcement): FAB Accelerates Enterprise AI Adoption Through Group-Wide Innovation and Talent Enablement" (First Abu Dhabi Bank press release, 17 February 2026)
