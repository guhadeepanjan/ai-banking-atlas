Coach AI for private client advisers

Announced May 2025

Coach AI helps JPMorgan's private client advisers find research and client information far faster. During April 2025's sharp market swings, advisers could answer worried wealthy clients quickly.

What it is

Coach AI is an AI tool used by JPMorgan's private client advisers in its asset and wealth management business. It is part of a wider set of generative AI tools the bank was rolling out to staff.

How it works

The tool pulls together a client's data, trading patterns and old investment preferences, and pre-populates information in real time. Advisers use it to locate the right content and research to drive conversations with clients.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Advisers were "finding the right information up to 95 per cent faster," according to Mike Urciuoli, chief information officer at JPMorgan asset and wealth management, quoted in the source. The source also reported a 20% increase in gross sales in Asset & Wealth Management between 2023 and 2024, with GenAI-driven tools helping teams focus on high-impact client work.

Source (Independent reporting): JPMorgan says AI helped boost sales, add clients in market turmoil" (The Business Times, Reuters report, 6 May 2025)

Deploy Ask DAVID, a multi-agent research assistant for the Private Bank

Announced May 2025

Ask DAVID is an internal research assistant for JPMorgan's Private Bank investment teams. A supervisor agent delegates work to specialist sub-agents. Answer accuracy has risen to about 90%.

What it is

Ask DAVID is a multi-agent AI assistant used by Private Bank investment teams for research. Its name stands for Data, Analytics, Visualization, Insights, Decision-making.

How it works

A supervisor agent with memory breaks each request into tasks. It delegates them to sub-agents for structured data queries, document retrieval and proprietary analytics. Version 2.0 rebuilds the supervisor as a Deep Agent for harder research tasks.

Technology used

LangGraph and LangChain's open-source deepagents package, with JPMorgan's proprietary private-banking Skills.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Accuracy rose from below 50% with a general model to about 90% with orchestration. The bank says the final 10% still needs human review because billions of dollars are in scope.

Source (Independent reporting): Agents in Production: Lessons building agents from J.P. Morgan, Chime, and Bridgewater" (LangChain, 2026)

Partner with Mirakl on agentic commerce payments

Announced March 2026

J.P. Morgan Payments has agreed to support agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own) shopping through Mirakl Nexus. The service is in closed beta with select retailers.

What it is

Mirakl Nexus is an agentic commerce layer that helps products be found by AI shopping agents. It covers the journey from discovery through checkout to aftersales.

How it works

J.P. Morgan Payments provides payment processing for agent-led purchases. It also provides tokenisation so agents can transact safely, plus fraud protection.

Technology used

Mirakl Nexus, with J.P. Morgan Payments processing and tokenisation.

EVEE Intelligent Q&A for call centre specialists

Announced May 2025

EVEE is a generative AI question-and-answer tool that Chase call centre specialists use while helping customers. Specialists find the right policy answer quickly instead of searching long documents during a live call.

What it is

EVEE Intelligent Q&A is a generative AI tool built for Chase's call centre teams, which handle customer service, fraud and claims, home lending, wealth management and collections. Specialists ask a question and receive a concise answer drawn from Chase policies and documentation.

How it works

The tool integrates with the systems call centre employees already use. The specialist chats with the interface, asks a question, and gets a short answer based on the bank's own policies and documents.

Technology used

JPMorgan's generative AI (EVEE Intelligent Q&A). The source does not name the underlying model or any outside vendor.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

Katie Hainsey, JPMorgan's head of AI/ML for digital, marketing and operations, told Tearsheet that EVEE improved efficiency, call resolution times, and employee and customer satisfaction. No figures were given.

Source (Independent reporting): JPMorgan Chase's Gen AI implementation: 450 use cases and lessons learned" (Tearsheet, 27 May 2025)

LLM Suite — firm-wide generative AI toolkit

Announced May 2025

LLM Suite is JPMorgan's own generative AI platform, installed on the desktops of more than 200,000 employees. It gives staff across the whole bank a shared, controlled way to use generative AI.

What it is

JPMorgan describes LLM Suite as its flagship model-agnostic generative AI platform, launched in 2024 and reported on at its 2025 Investor Day. By May 2025 the bank's GenAI toolkit was deployed on the desktops of more than 200,000 employees.

How it works

Staff access generative AI through the bank's own platform rather than through public tools. The Reuters report said more than half of the employees with the toolkit used it several times a day.

Technology used

JPMorgan's in-house LLM Suite, described by the bank as a model-agnostic generative AI platform; the underlying model vendors are not named in these sources.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

JPMorgan said its AI initiatives had already saved the bank nearly US$1.5 billion through fraud prevention, personalisation, trading, operational efficiencies and credit decisions (figure for its AI initiatives overall, as reported in the source).
