First agentic negotiation of a U.S. Treasury trade, using the Agentic Negotiation Protocol

Announced November 2026

BNY and Microsoft completed a pilot in which AI agents from the two firms negotiated a U.S. Treasury bill trade with each other. BNY called it an industry first.

What it is

The pilot was announced on 17 November 2026. At its centre is BNY's Agentic Negotiation Protocol (ANP), a patent-pending framework that lets authorised agents from different organisations negotiate securely and transparently.

How it works

Microsoft Treasury gave its agent specific trading instructions, and the agent connected to BNY through a secure agent gateway. The agents exchanged offers and counteroffers within preset limits and confirmed matching terms. Human traders at both firms then reviewed and approved the terms. The trade was executed and booked through the firms' existing systems. The agents could not execute the trade themselves.

Technology used

BNY's Agentic Negotiation Protocol (ANP); AI agents developed by BNY and Microsoft Frontier Company.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. This was a pilot trade, and BNY said it plans further testing, including other negotiated workflows such as repo.

Source (Bank announcement): BNY and Microsoft Complete First Agentic Negotiation of a U.S. Treasury Trade" (BNY press release, 17 November 2026)

LivePayments & Remittances

Digital employees repairing incomplete payment instructions

Announced November 2025

BNY has put more than 100 "digital employees" to work. These supervised AI agents include a cohort that works out what is wrong with payment instructions missing key details, and repairs them.

What it is

Chairman and CEO Robin Vince described the digital employees in November 2025: they operate as autonomous agents whose work is reviewed by human employees. Payment remediation and code remediation are the two examples in production.

How it works

When a payment instruction lacks key details, the digital employee investigates what is wrong and completes the remediation. Human employees review the agents' work.

Technology used

Multi-agent AI systems running on BNY's Eliza platform, each with its own persona and credentials and a human supervisor.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Source (Independent reporting): From process efficiency to 'digital employees'" (ABA Banking Journal, November 2025)

Enterprise-wide

LiveEnterprise-wide

Eliza enterprise AI platform: 99% of employees trained and onboarded

Announced October 2025

BNY's enterprise AI platform Eliza reached 99% of employees trained and onboarded by October 2025, up from just over a third in January 2025. BNY chose one shared platform over separate tools per team.

What it is

Eliza is BNY's single enterprise AI platform, named after Eliza Hamilton, wife of the bank's founder. In its October 2025 paper "AI in the Next Phase: Driving Adoption", BNY described the shift from giving employees access to getting them building their own agents.

How it works

Eliza gives employees secure chat, search, coding help and agent-building in one environment, with role-based AI learning paths and a responsible AI curriculum. Employees, increasingly from outside engineering, create their own Eliza agents inside the platform's governance.

Technology used

Eliza, built in-house by BNY on Microsoft Azure, integrating models and tools from providers including OpenAI, Google and Anthropic.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

BNY reported Eliza adoption up 175% in 2025 and 99% of employees fully trained and onboarded by October 2025; the source gives no financial benefit figure for the platform.

Source (Bank announcement): AI in the Next Phase: Driving Adoption" (BNY, 20 October 2025)
