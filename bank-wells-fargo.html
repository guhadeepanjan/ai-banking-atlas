Fargo passes one billion customer interactions

Announced March 2026

Wells Fargo's assistant Fargo has passed one billion customer interactions in under three years. Over three million Spanish-speaking customers have used it, across 160 million interactions. The mobile app has passed 33 million active users.

What it is

Fargo is the bank's AI-powered virtual assistant, launched in 2023 inside the Wells Fargo Mobile app. It helps customers complete everyday tasks and understand their spending and balances. The bank announced the one billion milestone on 26 March 2026.

How it works

Customers interact with Fargo conversationally to send money with Zelle, pay bills and find routing numbers. It also gives insights into spending and account balances. The bank says personalisation and convenience are driving adoption.

Technology used

Fargo, Wells Fargo's AI-powered virtual assistant in its mobile app. The source does not name the underlying models.

AI Teammate for financial advisors

Announced July 2026

Wells Fargo launched AI Teammate, a chat tool built into Advisor Gateway for financial advisors, client associates and support teams. It puts generative AI inside the daily wealth workflow, not in a separate tool.

What it is

An AI-powered capability embedded in Advisor Gateway, Wells Fargo's central advisor platform. It is available to advisors across all Wealth & Investment Management channels, including independent advisors in Wells Fargo Advisors Financial Network ("FiNet"), which serves affluent, high-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth clients.

How it works

Users ask questions in plain language and get answers quickly, and can take action from the same place. In its first phase it searches and summarises information from Wells Fargo Advisors' core resource centre (product, process and workflow information) and provides guided workflows with clear next steps.

AI agents for customer service across digital, branch and call centre channels (Google Agentspace)

Announced August 2025

Wells Fargo expanded its Google Cloud partnership to deploy AI agents in customer service across all channels, including its call centers. They automate routine tasks, such as balance inquiries and debit card replacements.

What it is

In August 2025, Wells Fargo and Google Cloud announced an expanded strategic relationship to deploy agentic AI at scale. Customer service was a named use: AI agents set to serve customers across digital, branch and call centre channels, including 24/7 personalised experiences.

How it works

Wells Fargo is an early adopter of Google Agentspace, Google Cloud's platform to build, manage and adopt AI agents (Agentspace has since become part of Gemini Enterprise). More than 2,000 Wells Fargo employees were using Agentspace at the announcement, with access expanding across teams in the following months.

Technology used

Google Cloud Agentspace (now part of Gemini Enterprise) and Google Cloud AI agents. The rollout also includes Google's NotebookLM for employee research and summaries.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. The announcement said the agents are expected to reduce wait times significantly on routine requests, but published no measured results.

AI agents close previously unknown cyber vulnerabilities

Announced October 2026

Wells Fargo uses AI, and in some cases autonomous agents, to close previously unknown cyber vulnerabilities. Chief executive Charlie Scharf described the work on the bank's October 2026 earnings call.

What it is

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, the bank said AI is applied across coding, marketing, risk management and customer tasks. Within cybersecurity, AI and autonomous agents close vulnerabilities that were not previously known. Scharf said the bank takes a disciplined approach to managing the risk.

How it works

The bank applies AI and, where appropriate, autonomous agents directly to the vulnerability problem, finding and closing gaps. Scharf did not describe the tooling in detail on the call. The work sits inside a wider programme that also accelerates underwriting decisions and complaint responses.

Agentic AI rollout via Google Agentspace

Announced August 2025

Wells Fargo widened its Google Cloud partnership to give staff AI agents through Google Agentspace. It was one of the first bank-wide rollouts of agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own).

What it is

An enterprise platform for building, managing and using AI agents at scale. It was offered to branch bankers, investment bankers, marketers, customer-relations and corporate teams, starting with more than 2,000 employees, with expansion planned across all teams.

How it works

Employees use agentic search and custom agents to find and summarise information, automate tasks and navigate policies. Named examples include answering, triaging and summarising complex foreign-exchange post-trade inquiries. They also include querying roughly 250,000 vendor-contract documents by clause, payment terms or contract type, and automating routine customer-service tasks such as balance inquiries and debit card replacements.

Technology used

Google Cloud Agentspace (now part of Gemini Enterprise), Google NotebookLM, Google multimodal / Google-quality enterprise search

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (The source states qualitatively that agents can significantly reduce wait times and free bankers for complex tasks, but gives no measured figure.)
