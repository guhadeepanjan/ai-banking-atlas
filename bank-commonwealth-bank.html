Tests CommBank Companion, a conversational cash flow assistant for businesses

Announced May 2026

CommBank has begun testing CommBank Companion, a conversational AI assistant inside its app, with 10,000 business banking customers. It explains cash flow trends in plain language and suggests practical next steps.

What it is

Companion gives business customers real-time visibility of their cash flow, including trends and projections. It works alongside existing service channels inside the CommBank app, delivering insight into a business's financial position around the clock.

How it works

Customers ask questions in a simple conversational experience and receive plain-language summaries of their business finances. When pressure or opportunity emerges, Companion highlights it and connects customers to relevant support. Testing began with business customers and employees, with personal banking customers to follow.

CommBiz GenAI-powered messaging service

Announced February 2025 (service launched late 2024, per the source)

A GenAI messaging service for tens of thousands of Business Banking customers using CommBiz. It answers payment and account questions in natural language without customers hunting through guides.

What it is

A messaging service designed to reimagine banking for Business Banking customers. It extracts information from over 80 different CommBiz user guides, FAQs and other supporting pages to provide relevant information quickly using natural language.

How it works

The service was built using CommBank's DevOps Hosting platform, its Group Generative AI Platform and Guardrails Service, plus retrieval from the guide/FAQ knowledge base. AWS states the product went from idea to production in just six weeks.

Technology used

Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases, Claude 3 and Cohere LLMs, and Amazon OpenSearch as the vector database (named in the AWS press release)

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

The source states the product went from idea to production in just six weeks, and that it will enable Business Banking customers to make payments faster and transact with confidence. No quantified customer time or cost saving is given.

Source (Bank announcement): "CommBank and AWS expand collaboration to deliver global best cloud and AI capabilities, enabling idea to production in six weeks" — AWS press release, February 2025

Launches AI agent that hunts scams and writes new fraud rules

Announced April 2026

CBA has launched an AI agent that watches payment and transaction data around the clock for scams and fraud. When it spots a suspicious pattern, it proposes new detection rules for human approval.

What it is

The agent monitors the more than 80 million signals CBA sees daily across cards, online payments, transactions and digital channels. It builds on the bank's existing fraud systems, within its $1 billion annual commitment to fraud, scam, financial crime and cyber prevention.

How it works

The system assesses the severity of suspicious patterns and analyses their context. It then proposes new detection rules to help intercept the threat. CBA's fraud analytics team reviews and approves the rules before they are implemented.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

CBA said the agent has played a role in creating or updating three-quarters of its card fraud rules. The bank also reported that its wider fraud work reduced fraud losses by over 20 percent in the first half of fiscal 2026.

Source (Independent reporting): CBA launches new scam-finding AI agent" (Cyber Daily, 24 April 2026)

AI voice bot on the inbound customer enquiries line

Announced June 2025

Commonwealth Bank put an AI voice bot on the inbound enquiries line of its Customer Service Direct contact centre. The bank first credited it with cutting call volumes, then reversed the job cuts and apologised.

What it is

The voice bot answers and handles routine inbound calls as part of the bank's "Hey CommBank" programme. It went live in June 2025 in the contact centre operation, which employs about 2,400 staff.

How it works

The bot speaks with callers on the enquiries line and deals with straightforward requests, diverting calls away from human agents. Calls it cannot handle pass to staff.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The bank initially said the bot diverted about 2,000 calls a week, and in July 2025 it announced 45 contact-centre roles would go. The Finance Sector Union disputed the figures, and on 21 August 2025 the bank reversed the cuts, apologised, and admitted its assessment "did not adequately consider all relevant business considerations". The bot itself stayed live.

Source (Independent reporting): CommBank reverses plan to replace call centre staff with AI" (Finextra, August 2025) · Second source

Releases CodeCheck, a free AI code security tool for businesses

Announced November 2026

CommBank has released CodeCheck, a free open-source tool that guides AI coding agents through security reviews of business software. It is based on a capability the bank's own security engineers built and use.

What it is

CodeCheck packages an internal CBA capability for finding software vulnerabilities earlier in development. CBA released it free on GitHub on 18 November 2026, as a one-time release, so other businesses can apply the same approach.

How it works

The tool guides AI coding agents through a consistent process for examining software. It analyses code, investigates potential vulnerabilities and presents evidence to help engineers assess each finding. Engineers remain responsible for reviewing findings and deciding what action to take.

Technology used

OpenAI Codex and Anthropic Claude Code, directed by CBA's CodeCheck process for security review.

AnnouncedEnterprise-wide

OpenAI partnership — ChatGPT Enterprise rollout to employees

Announced August 2025

CommBank became OpenAI's strategic banking partner in Australia under a multi-year partnership. Employees would progressively get ChatGPT Enterprise, while joint teams work on fraud detection and personalised services.

What it is

A partnership to bring advanced AI to customers and employees. CommBank and OpenAI engineers will work together to explore advanced generative AI solutions that aim to strengthen scam and fraud detection and deliver more personalised services for CommBank customers.

How it works

As part of the arrangement, CommBank employees progressively get access to OpenAI's advanced AI tools, including its enterprise-grade solution, ChatGPT Enterprise. FinTech Futures reported the rollout as covering CommBank's 52,000-strong workforce, with investment in training and upskilling programmes.

Expanded strategic partnership and investment in Anthropic (Claude)

Announced March 2025

CommBank expanded its partnership with, and investment in, Anthropic to accelerate AI adoption across the bank. Its engineers would work with Anthropic experts, with fraud and scam protection named as a priority use case.

What it is

An expanded strategic partnership intended to position CBA to leverage Anthropic's AI capabilities and expertise in safety and AI practices to support its people and transform customer experiences. Note: this is a partnership/investment announcement enabling deployment work, rather than a named live product launch.

How it works

CBA technologists get access to Anthropic thought leaders. Its engineers have the opportunity to work with Anthropic's AI experts to explore how to build products to serve and protect customers. That includes further protection from scams and fraud. Anthropic's CFO said the teams would work on applications particularly in fraud prevention and customer service enhancement.
