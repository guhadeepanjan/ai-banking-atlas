"One Foundation, Eight AI Agents" corporate banking assistants

Announced October 2026

CITIC Bank built a set of eight AI agents for corporate banking work, running on one shared foundation. The agents speed up everyday corporate tasks such as product screening and credit due-diligence reports.

What it is

The framework was reported in the bank's third-quarter 2026 report, published on 29 October 2026. Named examples include a wealth-management assistant and a credit due-diligence report assistant.

How it works

The wealth-management assistant helps with product screening and preparing marketing materials. The credit due-diligence assistant helps generate due-diligence reports, including financial statement and industry analysis.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The wealth-management assistant made product screening and marketing material preparation five times more efficient, and the credit due-diligence assistant cut financial and industry analysis time by 80%, according to the bank.

Source (Independent reporting): China CITIC Bank Report for the Third Quarter of 2026" (HKEX filing, 29 October 2026)

Large models generate quantitative strategies in financial markets

Announced August 2026

CITIC Bank used large AI models to generate quantitative trading strategies for its financial-markets business. That is a rare case of a bank using large models directly in markets work, not only in customer service.

What it is

CITIC Limited's interim results announcement for the first half of 2026 reported that the bank used large language models to generate quantitative strategies for financial markets. The bank's own first-quarter 2026 report also lists "intelligent quantitative strategies for financial markets" among its AI+ scenarios.

How it works

The large models are used to produce quantitative trading strategies. The sources do not describe the models or the workflow in more detail.

Technology used

Large language models; the sources do not name a specific model or vendor.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

A threefold increase in strategy research and development efficiency, according to the parent company's interim results announcement.

Source (Independent reporting): CITIC Limited Interim Results Announcement 2026" (CITIC Limited, August 2026) · Second source
