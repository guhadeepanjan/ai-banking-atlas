UPWARD AI agent for corporate field sales

Announced September 2026

Japan Post Bank has deployed UPWARD, an AI agent for field sales, across its corporate banking division. Sales staff no longer lose client time to typing up visit reports by hand.

What it is

UPWARD Inc. announced on 29 September 2026 that Japan Post Bank had adopted its field-sales AI agent. The bank's corporate sales teams previously entered post-meeting documentation manually, which cut into client-facing time and produced reports of uneven quality.

How it works

The agent detects a client visit using the smartphone's location and records face-to-face negotiations. It uses AI to summarise the conversation and file a standardised activity report into the CRM system automatically.

Technology used

UPWARD, the field-sales AI agent from UPWARD Inc. (AI meeting recording and summarisation, geofencing-based visit detection, CRM integration).

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (The stated aims are lighter administration and better-quality client proposals; no figures are given.)
