AI-powered financial assistant in the mobile app

Announced November 2025

Lloyds launched the UK's first large-scale, multi-feature AI financial assistant, built into its mobile app for over 21 million customers. Customers get personalised financial coaching at any hour, inside the app they already use.

What it is

Announced on 6 November 2025, the assistant is described by Lloyds as the first UK deployment of agentic AI to help customers manage their money in this way. It was tested with staff before the public launch and rolled out to Bank of Scotland customers first.

How it works

Customers chat with the assistant in natural language. Its first features give personalised spending insights and a savings and investment tool. The assistant retains memory of the customer, can break requests into steps and act on them, and can refer the customer to expert human support. Lloyds planned to extend it across mortgages, car finance and protection during 2026.

Technology used

Lloyds Banking Group's Generative AI and Agentic framework, combining curated bank data with generative AI models (the specific models are not named in the source).

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Launches Export Assist to check exporters' LC documents before submission

Announced November 2026

Lloyds has launched Export Assist, an AI tool that checks UK exporters' letter of credit documents for errors before submission. It is open to any UK exporter for £50 per use.

What it is

Export Assist is aimed mainly at smaller exporters, whose documents are often refused over minor errors. Users manage their letters of credit in one dashboard and fix problems before the bank sees the documents.

How it works

The tool runs on Complidata's TradeSpeed platform and uses AI to automate compliance checks on LC documents. It flags potential discrepancies, so exporters can correct them before presenting documents for payment.

Technology used

Complidata's TradeSpeed platform.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Lloyds estimates UK exporters lose around 500,000 trading days a year to document errors and resubmissions. The tool is designed to cut discrepancy fees and payment delays.

Source (Independent reporting): Lloyds unveils AI tool to spot document errors for UK exporters" (Global Trade Review, 11 November 2026)

Athena — AI knowledge hub for customer service colleagues

Announced January 2026

Athena is a generative AI knowledge hub that helps Lloyds' customer service colleagues find answers from 13,000 internal articles while they serve customers. Colleagues answer in seconds instead of searching documents while the customer waits.

What it is

Athena is Lloyds Banking Group's first large-scale generative AI product, used by customer-facing colleagues in telephone and online banking teams, who help around two million customers a month. Lloyds described its progress in its results announcement of 29 January 2026.

How it works

Instead of searching document titles, the colleague asks a natural-language question. Athena scans the Group's 13,000 authorised internal knowledge articles and returns a synthesised answer, using retrieval-augmented generation on the Group's Google Cloud Vertex AI platform.

Technology used

Generative AI with retrieval-augmented generation over internal content, on Lloyds' Vertex AI-based platform (the specific foundation model is not named in the source).

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Average search time fell from 59 seconds to about 20 seconds, a 66% reduction; around 20,000 colleagues were using Athena, and Lloyds said generative AI overall delivered around £50 million of value in 2025, as stated in the source.
