AI analyses customer calls to guide "365" contact centre staff

Announced October 2026

SEB uses AI to analyse customer calls in its "365" contact centre operation and guide staff on the next step. About 70 per cent of customer calls are now analysed within hours, speeding up follow-up.

What it is

The capability sits in SEB's Business & Retail Banking division and was reported on the bank's third-quarter 2026 earnings call.

How it works

Calls are analysed by AI shortly after they happen. The analysis helps contact centre staff determine the appropriate next step for each customer.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Approximately 70 per cent of customer calls are now analysed within hours, as stated by the bank on the call.

Source (Independent reporting): SEB Q3 2026 earnings call transcript (22 October 2026)

AI voicebot answers night calls in Baltic contact centres

Announced July 2026

Since July 2026, an AI voicebot handles night-shift calls in SEB's Baltic contact centres. Customers can block cards and internet banking access at night without waiting for human support.

What it is

The voicebot was reported in SEB's interim report for the third quarter of 2026 and discussed on the bank's earnings call of 22 October 2026.

How it works

The voicebot answers calls during night shifts and lets customers block their cards and internet banking access. The bank said expansion to daytime operations is planned.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

Management reported significantly reduced customer waiting times and improved efficiency (qualitative, as stated by the bank; no figures given).

Microsoft 365 Copilot rolled out to staff

Announced April 2026

SEB rolled out Microsoft 365 Copilot broadly to staff, starting in the fourth quarter of 2025. Adoption was very high: more than 95 per cent of licence holders were active users by early 2026.

What it is

The rollout gives employees an AI assistant inside their everyday Microsoft 365 tools. GitHub Copilot was also rolled out to developers.

How it works

Staff use Copilot within their normal office applications. By the first quarter of 2026, around 80 per cent of licence holders used it daily.

Technology used

Microsoft 365 Copilot; GitHub Copilot for developers.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.
