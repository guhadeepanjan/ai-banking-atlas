AdvisorAssist: AI meeting notes for advisers

Announced July 2026

AdvisorAssist is a generative AI tool that captures and summarises client meeting notes for CIBC advisers. Advisers spend less time on paperwork after meetings and more time with clients.

What it is

AdvisorAssist was launched nationally on 31 July 2026 and was developed internally by CIBC. It supports advisers working with personal banking, Imperial Service and wealth management clients.

How it works

The tool captures and summarises meeting notes and streamlines the follow-up documentation advisers must complete. Compliance requirements are built into the process.

Technology used

CIBC's in-house generative AI; the source does not name the underlying model.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Adviser administration time was reduced by up to 50%, according to the bank.

Source (Independent reporting): CIBC launches new AI-powered platform to help advisors spend more time with clients" (CIBC news release via CNW, 31 July 2026, reproduced at CanadianSME)

CIBC AI (CAI): in-house generative AI platform launched bank-wide

Announced May 2025

CIBC launched its own generative AI platform, CAI, to staff across the bank after a successful pilot. The whole bank now has one governed, in-house AI tool instead of staff turning to outside tools.

What it is

CAI is a custom-built generative AI platform that CIBC launched enterprise-wide on 27 May 2025. A pilot that began in July 2024 grew from 500 users to 7,500 users before the full launch.

How it works

Staff use CAI to summarise documents, draft emails and compile research. Use is backed by mandatory training and responsible-AI governance, and CIBC was the first major Canadian bank to sign Canada's voluntary code of conduct for generative AI, in March 2025.

Technology used

CIBC's in-house CAI platform; the source does not name the underlying model.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

CIBC estimated that the pilot saved more than 200,000 hours of work.

Source (Bank announcement): CIBC launches in-house AI platform enterprise-wide after successful pilot" (CIBC news release, 27 May 2025)
