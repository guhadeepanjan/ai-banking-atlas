Truist Insights — AI-driven personalised financial insights

Announced August 2025

Truist's AI-driven Insights platform delivers more than 550 million personalised insights a year to clients in mobile and online banking. Clients get help with cash flow, balances and subscriptions instead of working it out themselves.

What it is

Truist Insights is an AI-driven platform available in the bank's mobile and online banking, described in Truist's August 2025 strategic growth investment announcement.

How it works

The platform analyses client accounts to produce personalised, real-time insights, including cash-flow summaries, income and expense analysis, proactive balance monitoring and help managing subscriptions.

Technology used

AI-driven analytics (Truist Insights platform); the underlying models are not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Truist states the platform delivers more than 550 million personalised, real-time financial insights per year. No savings or satisfaction figure was given.

Source (Bank announcement): Truist announces significant multi-year investment in high-growth markets" (Truist Financial, own newsroom, 20 August 2025)

AI Call Summaries across Truist care centers

Announced September 2026

Truist deployed AI Call Summaries across its care centers, using generative AI to turn client conversations into structured summaries. Note-taking is handled automatically, so teammates can focus on the client and resolve needs faster.

What it is

AI Call Summaries is a generative AI capability in Truist's care centers (its contact centres). Truist began implementing it in the third quarter of 2025 and announced its full deployment on 9 September 2026. The bank describes it as one of its earliest enterprise-scale generative AI applications approved for broad use.

How it works

After client conversations, the system automatically converts them into structured summaries within seconds. Teammates use the summaries to access key information from prior interactions and prepare for follow-up conversations.

Technology used

Generative AI; the underlying model or vendor is not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The capability generated more than 4.5 million summaries in the second quarter of 2026 alone and supported more than 70% of care center inbound client interactions, according to Truist. Based on average time savings of approximately 30 seconds per call, it saved teammates more than 36,000 hours in that quarter.

Source (Bank announcement): Truist advances AI to create more meaningful client interactions" (Truist Financial, own newsroom via PR Newswire, 9 September 2026)
