Runs Conpend Trade AI for document checks on its regional trade platform

Announced April 2026

Maybank's single regional trade platform, built on Finastra technology, includes Conpend Trade AI. It automates document recognition, compliance validation and anti-money laundering checks across Malaysia, Singapore and Indonesia.

What it is

Maybank replaced three legacy trade systems with one Finastra Trade Innovation platform for its three core markets. Conpend Trade AI sits inside that platform alongside limits management and watchlist screening.

How it works

Conpend Trade AI reads trade documents and automates their recognition. It then runs compliance validation and anti-money laundering checks as transactions move through the platform.

Technology used

Conpend Trade AI, integrated with Finastra Trade Innovation.

Mastercard Agent Pay pilot — first authenticated agentic transaction in Malaysia

Announced March 2026

In a Mastercard pilot, Maybank completed Malaysia's first authenticated payment by agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own). The agent booked and paid for an airport ride, with consent and strong authentication.

What it is

Mastercard announced the completed pilot with CIMB, Maybank and RHB using Mastercard Agent Pay. In the pilot, an AI agent booked a ride from Kuala Lumpur International Airport to KL Sentral through mobility provider hoppa, in a controlled environment.

How it works

CardInfoLink's AI agent connected to hoppa's taxi and airport limousine service. The transaction used tokenised credentials authenticated with Mastercard Payment Passkeys, with consumer consent and security safeguards; commercial deployment is to be introduced in phases.

Technology used

Mastercard Agent Pay, tokenised credentials and Mastercard Payment Passkeys.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. The pilot was designed to confirm the feasibility of agentic transactions in Malaysia.

Source (Independent reporting): Finovate Global Malaysia: Agentic Commerce, Embedded Finance, and Shariah-Compliance" (Finovate, March 2026)

ServiceNow AI brings operational intelligence to the regional Resilience Operations Centre

Announced October 2026

Maybank signed a five-year agreement with ServiceNow to bring AI-driven operational intelligence to its Resilience Operations Centre in three markets. AI spots early signs of system problems so staff can act before customers are affected.

What it is

The agreement, announced on 20 October 2026, gives Maybank a consolidated view of its technology systems, letting teams monitor applications, infrastructure and service performance across the three markets. Maybank will also set up an internal Centre of Excellence, supported by ServiceNow University, and the rollout begins in Malaysia and Singapore before expanding to Indonesia.

How it works

Using ServiceNow's AI platform, the centre aims to detect potential problems earlier, automate operational processes and address risks before they affect customers. The bank's chief technology officer likened it to spotting a lightbulb flickering and acting before systems go down.

Technology used

ServiceNow AI Platform.

Microsoft partnership — Copilot for all 44,000 employees and Azure cloud adoption

Announced August 2025

Maybank entered a five-year strategic partnership with Microsoft worth about RM1.0 billion, including rolling out Microsoft 365 Copilot to all 44,000 employees. Every employee gets an AI assistant in daily work.

What it is

Maybank's press release of 5 August 2025 announced the partnership to accelerate digital transformation and AI-driven innovation. Maybank will adopt Microsoft Azure as one of the main cloud platforms for some of its most strategic systems, functions and data workloads. The two companies will also establish a Centre of Excellence for cloud, data and AI.

How it works

Microsoft 365 Copilot is rolled out across all employees to amplify productivity and intelligence in daily workflows. Meanwhile, strategic workloads move to Azure and Microsoft security tools strengthen the group's cyber posture.

Technology used

Microsoft 365 Copilot, Microsoft Azure and Microsoft security solutions.
