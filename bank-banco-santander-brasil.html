AI hyper-personalisation — over 1,400 personalised campaigns in 2025

Announced February 2026

Santander Brasil ran more than 1,400 hyper-personalised campaigns in 2025, and 60% of all client interactions are now personalised. Each offer is matched to the customer's moment and profile, instead of one campaign for everyone.

What it is

On its fourth-quarter 2025 earnings call (4 February 2026), the bank's CEO described hyper-personalisation and AI as the two core levers of its customer strategy. A new CRM-based customer interaction platform integrates client information and market information to personalise campaigns across all channels.

How it works

AI-driven contextual offers connect the customer, the moment and the product. The bank also uses AI for efficiency in areas such as fraud and its ombudsman process, and generative AI strengthens its credit and recovery models, according to the same call.

Technology used

The bank's new customer interaction platform (CRM) with AI-driven personalisation; specific models are not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The bank said 60% of all interactions with clients across all channels were already hyper-personalised, and more than 1,400 hyper-personalised campaigns were deployed in 2025, according to the earnings call.

Source (Independent reporting): Banco Santander (Brasil) Q4 2025 Earnings Call Transcript" (4 February 2026)

Pitch Maker — AI pitch generator for investment advisers

Announced October 2025

Pitch Maker, a generative AI tool, prepares a personalised investment pitch for each client in about 30 seconds. Advisers get that time back for talking to clients.

What it is

Santander Brasil launched Pitch Maker in mid-2025 for its AAA investment-advisory business, which serves clients with over R$250 thousand invested, and later extended it to Santander Select specialists. The bank highlighted it in its third-quarter 2025 earnings release as a concrete example of AI in investment advisory.

How it works

The tool analyses the client's investment profile, current portfolio, interaction history and market trends, then generates a personalised pitch inside the adviser's normal work environment. It was built with technology partner BRQ on Amazon Bedrock.

Technology used

Generative AI on Amazon Bedrock (with Amazon Aurora and Amazon EKS), built with BRQ.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Pitch preparation time fell from about 35 minutes to under 30 seconds, effective weekly contacts rose 24%, and more than 500 pitches were generated per day, with 91% user satisfaction, according to BRQ's case study of the deployment.

Source (Independent reporting): Earnings Release, 3rd quarter of 2025" (Banco Santander Brasil, October 2025) · Second source

Pega GenAI automates legal document screening

Announced December 2025

Santander Brazil uses Pega's generative AI in its legal operations to automate document screening and interpret legal terms. Legal teams handle large document volumes, and automating the first screening frees lawyers for higher-value work.

What it is

The bank pointed Pega GenAI at legal documents, working within the bank's own procedures, policies and escalation paths. Steve Morgan, Pegasystems' global banking industry lead, described the deployment on FinAi News' "The Buzz" podcast in December 2025. The bank had presented the case at Pega's conference in June 2025.

How it works

The AI interprets legal terms and screens documents against the bank's strict guidelines, helping to automate screening, reduce risk and free up human capital in legal operations.

Technology used

Pega GenAI (Pegasystems).

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Pega GenAI allows the bank to interpret legal terms with 99% accuracy, according to Steve Morgan of Pegasystems as reported by FinAi News.

Source (Independent reporting): Podcast: Rabobank, Santander Brazil see returns from Pega GenAI" (FinAi News, December 2025)
