Banking through customers' own AI agents via an MCP server

Announced October 2026

Danske Bank lets business customers connect their own AI agents to the bank through an MCP server. Danske was the first bank in the Nordic countries to let customers bank through their own AI solutions.

What it is

Announced on 21 October 2026, the service lets business customers use AI tools such as Claude or ChatGPT to interact with the bank through its Premium APIs.

How it works

The customer's own AI solution connects to Danske Bank's MCP server, which sits in front of the bank's Premium APIs. Customers can ask questions, build tailored financial views and ultimately take action through the agent.

Technology used

Model Context Protocol (MCP) server and Danske Bank Premium APIs; the bank names Claude and ChatGPT as examples of AI solutions customers may connect.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Source (Bank announcement): Danske Bank becomes the first bank in the Nordic countries to enable business customers to bank through AI agents" (Danske Bank press release, 21 October 2026)

First AI agent card payment in Denmark with Mastercard Agent Pay

Announced September 2026

An AI agent paid for a booking with a Danske Bank Mastercard, in Denmark's first AI agent card payment. The agent completed a real purchase on live card rails, not in a test environment.

What it is

On 21 September 2026 an AI agent booked a coffee tasting on Mastercard's Priceless.com platform and paid autonomously using a Danske Bank-issued Mastercard.

How it works

Mastercard Agent Pay handled the payment using Agentic Tokens, so the real card number was never exposed. PayOS orchestrated the process from the agent's instruction through to settlement.

Technology used

Mastercard Agent Pay with Agentic Tokens; PayOS orchestration.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. This was a first-of-its-kind pilot transaction.

Source (Independent reporting): Mastercard Helps Danske Bank With Denmark-First Agentic Transaction" (PYMNTS, 21 September 2026)

AI Assistant answers customers in the Danish mobile banking app

Announced February 2026

Danske Bank's AI Assistant in its mobile banking app gives Danish customers 24/7 automated support. It had already handled about 20 per cent of enquiries since launch, easing pressure on human advisers.

What it is

The assistant was a key milestone of the bank's Forward '28 strategy in 2025 and was reported in Danske Bank's Annual Report 2025, published on 5 February 2026. It helps customers handle everyday banking tasks on their own, or connects them with experts for complex needs.

How it works

Customers chat with the assistant inside the Danske Mobile Banking app at any time. It combines instant automated help with a hand-over to human experts when needed.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Since its launch, the AI Assistant had handled approximately 20 per cent of enquiries and contributed to cutting waits for human chat to under two minutes, as stated in the annual report.

Source (Independent reporting): Annual Report 2025" (Danske Bank, 5 February 2026)
