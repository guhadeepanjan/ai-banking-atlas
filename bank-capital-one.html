Chat Concierge: multi-agent assistant for car buying

Announced January 2025

Capital One launched Chat Concierge on car dealers' websites to help shoppers research cars and book test drives. It was the bank's first customer-facing agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own).

What it is

Chat Concierge works through Capital One's Auto Navigator platform on participating dealers' websites. Fast Company reported its launch on 31 January 2025, and Capital One published its engineering account of the system in March 2025.

How it works

Several AI agents work together, understanding what the shopper wants, reasoning about options, validating answers and explaining them. They answer questions, compare vehicles, and complete tasks such as scheduling a test drive, around the clock.

Technology used

Capital One's proprietary multi-agent framework (MACAW) on fine-tuned open-weight (Llama-based) models customised in-house.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Dealers using Chat Concierge reported a 55% improvement in engagement and serious-lead metrics, as reported by Fast Company.

Source (Independent reporting): Capital One's new AI agent will help you buy your next car" (Fast Company, 31 January 2025)

Generative AI gives customer service agents real-time policy answers

Announced March 2025

Capital One built its generative AI servicing tool that lets customer service agents find the right policy answer in real time, while they are helping a customer. Agents no longer search documents by hand mid-call.

What it is

Capital One described the proprietary tool in its own technology publication in March 2025, and presented the platform at NVIDIA's GTC 2025 conference. The bank said the tool had been rolled out across multiple business units.

How it works

The tool uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): it searches Capital One's internal knowledge with hybrid keyword and vector search, then generates an answer grounded in that content. For example, an agent can instantly check whether a declined card transaction counts against a customer's daily card limit.

Technology used

Capital One's in-house RAG platform: retrieval on AWS OpenSearch and LLM inference on Amazon SageMaker with NVIDIA GPUs, using open-weight models customised by the bank.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. Capital One said the tool helps agents resolve customer questions "more quickly and efficiently", with no figure given.

Source (Bank announcement): How AI Is Transforming Financial Services" (Capital One technology publication, 26 March 2025)
