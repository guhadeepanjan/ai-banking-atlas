About Banking on AI

Banking on AI tracks how the world’s 100 biggest pure banks are using — and announcing — artificial intelligence — deployment by deployment, in plain language, with the source behind every claim.

What this is

A curated record of AI, machine learning and agentic AI deployments at the world’s top 100 pure banks by market capitalisation (ranking: CompaniesMarketCap, 25 November 2026; investment banks, brokerages and diversified financial groups that are not primarily banks are excluded). Each deployment is classified into one of nine business segments, from Retail Banking and Trade Finance to Contact Centre and Enterprise-wide programmes.

How deployments are checked and sourced

  • Every deployment is traced to a source that was opened and read — never to a search snippet or another aggregator.
  • Each entry is labelled by source type: Bank announcement (the bank’s own release or newsroom) or Independent reporting (trade press and news media).
  • Each entry carries a status: Live (in production use), Rolling out (being deployed in phases) or Announced (a plan, pilot or partnership, not yet in general use).
  • Benefits are stated only where the source states them, and marked as a Measured result, a Bank-stated aim, or Not stated. Nothing is estimated or filled in.
  • The window is the last 24 months (December 2024 to November 2026). The record is refreshed monthly: new deployments are added, announced ones are re-checked, and entries that age out of the window are rolled off.

What the coverage means

Coverage depth reflects public disclosure, not total AI activity. A bank with few entries may simply disclose less; a bank with no tracked deployment is shown as such rather than padded with rumours. As of November 2026 the record holds 318 deployments across 97 of the 100 banks.

Who curates it

Banking on AI is researched and curated by Deepanjan Guha, a banking operations leader with two decades in banking operations and payments, currently Head of Operations — Banking Operations & Payments at ICICI Bank in Singapore. Banking on AI is an independent personal project; it is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any bank named on this site, and the views expressed are the curator’s own. His own employer, ICICI Bank, is tracked on the same evidence standard as every other bank.
