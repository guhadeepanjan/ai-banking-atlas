AI in Treasury

As of November 2026, 12 of the world’s top 100 banks have 14 verified AI deployments in treasury tracked by Banking on AI. AI in treasury and liquidity: cash forecasting, payments within the bank and balance-sheet tools. Every deployment below is explained in plain language, with its status and the source behind it.

Common questions

Which banks use AI in treasury?

The banks with verified deployments tracked here are: Bank of America, HSBC, Citigroup, DBS Group, Postal Savings Bank of China, BNY Mellon, U.S. Bancorp, CITIC Bank, Barclays, Deutsche Bank, Standard Chartered, Huntington Bancshares. The most active are Bank of America, BNY Mellon, HSBC.

How many AI deployments in treasury have been verified?

14 deployments across 12 banks, announced between December 2024 and November 2026. 8 of them report a measured result; the rest state an aim or no benefit, and this page says which.

What is the latest AI development in treasury?

First agentic negotiation of a U.S. Treasury trade, using the Agentic Negotiation Protocol at BNY Mellon (November 2026, status: Announced). BNY and Microsoft completed a pilot in which AI agents from the two firms negotiated a U.S. Treasury bill trade with each other. BNY called it an industry first.

How does Banking on AI verify these deployments?

Every deployment is traced to an opened source, labelled as a bank announcement or independent reporting, and given a status: Live, Rolling out or Announced. Benefits are reported only where the source states them.
