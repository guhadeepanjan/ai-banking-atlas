Blue — generative AI virtual assistant (rebuilt version)

Announced February 2025 (BBVA announcement published 14 February 2025)

BBVA rebuilt its app assistant, Blue, on generative AI so customers can manage accounts and cards by chatting in plain language. It was a first for a Spanish bank's virtual assistant.

What it is

Blue sits in BBVA's digital channels and handles up to 150 queries and transactions, which BBVA says translates into answering over 3,000 customer questions. It gives personalised information about a customer's own finances and can carry out common account and card transactions. BBVA also launched a separate AI assistant for its own agents, based on more than 30,000 product and service references, initially for more than 100 agents.

How it works

Large language models interpret what the customer means and generate the reply, keeping track of the conversation context, including if the customer changes topic mid-conversation. Blue works only on banking topics, with validations and guardrails to keep answers within BBVA's commercial, reputational and legal requirements. A customer's private information is used only within the active conversation.

Technology used

Large language models (LLMs); the specific model is not specified in the source.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (BBVA says Blue aims to greatly enhance efficiency in answering questions, but gives no measured figure in this announcement.)

Source (Bank announcement): "For the first time in Spain, BBVA will offer AI-powered account and card management with its virtual assistant" — BBVA newsroom, 14 February 2025 · Second source

Generative AI assistants for contact centre agents in Italy and Germany

Announced August 2026

Agents at BBVA's digital banks in Italy and Germany use generative AI assistants for customer phone, email and in-app messages. The assistants handle about 100,000 enquiries a month alongside more than 260 agents.

What it is

BBVA's own article (4 August 2026) describes two assistants built by the contact centre teams of its fully digital banks in Italy and Germany. They were built on BBVA Assistants, the group's corporate generative AI platform.

How it works

While dealing with a customer, the agent asks the assistant questions in natural language; it drafts answers from the bank's product and procedure documents. The assistants have no access to customer personal data, and the agent checks the draft before replying.

Technology used

BBVA Assistants platform, using OpenAI models.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

BBVA states the assistants cut the average handling time for the most common enquiries by more than 15%. About 40,000 agent–copilot messages were exchanged in June 2026 alone.

Source (Bank announcement): BBVA cuts customer inquiry response times by more than 15% in Italy and Germany thanks to AI" (BBVA, 4 August 2026)

Strategic alliance with OpenAI — ChatGPT Enterprise extended to more than 120,000 employees

Announced December 2025 (announced 12 December 2025)

BBVA signed a multi-year alliance with OpenAI and extended ChatGPT Enterprise to its whole workforce of over 120,000 people. BBVA called it one of the largest corporate deployments of the technology globally.

What it is

Joint teams from BBVA and OpenAI will co-create solutions, with BBVA getting preferential access to OpenAI's most advanced models and expert talent. Planned workstreams include an intelligent conversational assistant for customers' day-to-day finances, personalised tools for relationship managers, streamlined risk-analysis systems, and software-development and employee-productivity tools.

How it works

The ChatGPT Enterprise rollout follows an initial adoption phase with 11,000 employees across all business areas. The alliance also includes work on a digital "alter ego" for employees: a proactive second self that learns how an employee works and remembers their projects. It executes tasks only with the employee's authorisation and oversight. The alliance also covers integrating BBVA's products and services directly into ChatGPT.

Technology used

OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise and OpenAI models (specific model names not specified in the source).

Benefit reported (Measured result)

In the initial 11,000-employee phase, 80% of users accessed the assistant daily and reported saving an average of three hours per week on routine tasks (as stated by BBVA in the announcement).

Source (Bank announcement): "BBVA and OpenAI Seal a Strategic Alliance to Redefine Banking with Artificial Intelligence" — BBVA newsroom, 12 December 2025

Google Workspace with Gemini deployment to over 100,000 employees

Announced July 2025 (announced 2 July 2025)

BBVA deployed Google's Gemini AI inside the everyday tools its staff already use — Gmail, Docs, Sheets and more. The rollout covers over 100,000 employees worldwide, backed by a mandatory 'AI Express' training programme.

What it is

A firm-wide generative AI deployment, extending BBVA's Google Cloud partnership (running since 2011) into daily work tools. Staff also get the standalone Gemini app and NotebookLM, the AI research and writing assistant.

How it works

Gemini is embedded in Google Workspace to summarise, draft and find information across emails, chats and files, and to help create documents, presentations, spreadsheets and videos and take meeting notes. Access is given only to employees who have completed BBVA's internal training, which is aligned with the EU AI Act and BBVA's data-protection policies.

Technology used

Google Cloud: Google Workspace with Gemini, Gemini app, and NotebookLM.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

BBVA employees report that automating repetitive tasks with AI saves them nearly three hours per week on average, freeing time for more strategic, customer-focused work (as stated in the announcement).

Source (Bank announcement): "BBVA deepens partnership with Google Cloud to innovate with AI" — BBVA newsroom, 2 July 2025
