Document Intelligence platform for onboarding and due diligence

Announced October 2026

Emirates NBD's Document Intelligence platform reads customer documents and turns them into validated data for onboarding and compliance systems. Business onboarding due diligence that took about 19 days now takes about 4.7 days.

What it is

Document Intelligence is an enterprise platform that combines optical character recognition, machine learning and generative AI to convert unstructured customer documents into validated, reusable data. It supports onboarding, servicing and compliance processes, and won The Asian Banker's award for Best AI-Powered Customer Experience and Engagement Initiative in the Middle East.

How it works

OCR captures document content, AI classifies documents and extracts attributes with context, and validation services decide whether the information can be reused. During onboarding the platform pre-populates around 100 of 250 application fields, with human review and data lineage kept in the process.

Technology used

Optical character recognition, machine learning and generative AI (Emirates NBD Document Intelligence platform); no model vendor is named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Standard due diligence turnaround fell from approximately 19 days to 4.7 days (about 75% faster), and enhanced due diligence from 41.5 days to 11.4 days, according to figures Emirates NBD gave The Asian Banker. The platform achieved approximately 98% precision in document classification and attribute extraction, and high-risk annual review turnaround fell from about 66 days to 21 days.

OpenAI GPT models deployed on in-country infrastructure in the UAE

Announced October 2026

Emirates NBD is the first MENA bank to deploy OpenAI's GPT models on in-country infrastructure in the UAE. Running models inside the UAE lets it use AI while meeting local data residency and privacy rules.

What it is

Under the collaboration announced on 19 October 2026, Emirates NBD will deploy OpenAI's models across several areas. These cover customer engagement on digital channels, risk and compliance analytics, automation of knowledge-intensive workflows and employee productivity.

How it works

The bank will connect OpenAI's models directly to its internal systems, APIs and enterprise data. Processing stays on infrastructure inside the UAE, to address the security, governance and compliance requirements of banking.

Technology used

OpenAI GPT models, run on in-country (UAE) inference infrastructure.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. The announcement describes planned uses, with no measured results published.
