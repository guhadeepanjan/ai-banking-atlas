AI agent handles the middle of KB Kookmin Bank's complaint process

Announced September 2026

An AI agent now handles the middle of KB Kookmin Bank's customer complaint process, from searching material to drafting the reply. The bank reported average complaint handling time falling from 19.7 days to 8.4 days.

What it is

The bank presented the agent at the Microsoft Industry Summit 2026 in Seoul on 30 September 2026. Staff keep the two ends of the process: confirming the facts of the complaint and signing off the final reply.

How it works

The agent reads and summarises the complaint, classifies its type, searches for relevant material and drafts the reply letter. An employee then checks the facts, reviews the draft and answers the customer — cutting the process from five steps to three.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

As presented by the bank, average complaint handling time fell from 19.7 days to 8.4 days, and open complaints fell from 1,204 to 278. The bank also said it runs more than 60 AI agents internally, with over 8,000 users.

Source (Independent reporting): KB Kookmin Bank Cuts Complaint Handling to 8.4 Days" (case report of the bank's presentation, reported by Byline Network, October 2026)

KB DAVIS — Gemini-based data analysis agent

Announced July 2026

KB DAVIS is an AI agent that lets employees search and analyse group data by asking questions in everyday language. Staff without data skills can turn manuals, product data and market information into visual reports.

What it is

KB DAVIS was announced on 28 July 2026 under a strategic collaboration between KB Financial Group and Google Cloud. A related customer-facing agent, KB Star Byeoli, had finished internal testing and was in staged rollout.

How it works

Employees ask questions in natural language. The agent searches work manuals, product data and market information, analyses it and presents visualised reports.

Technology used

Google Gemini, on Google Cloud.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

KB GenAI Portal — group-wide agentic AI platform

Announced May 2025 · Went live April 2025

KB opened a generative AI portal for its holding company and eight subsidiaries, where any employee can build AI agents. KB called it the finance sector's first such portal for building AI agents.

What it is

The portal was established in April 2025 and announced in May 2025. It serves KB Kookmin Bank, KB Securities, KB Insurance, KB Kookmin Card, KB Life Insurance, KB Asset Management, KB Capital and KB Savings Bank.

How it works

The portal offers tiered development environments (no-code for beginners, simple coding, and a professional developer environment), so staff at any skill level can build agents. KB's roadmap targets about 300 AI agents across 58 core work areas by 2026, and its 2025 annual report counts more than 80 agents developed across five subsidiaries.

Technology used

KB GenAI Portal (the group's own platform); underlying models are not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.
