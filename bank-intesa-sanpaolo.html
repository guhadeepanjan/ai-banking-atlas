AI automatically resolves client requests in the Digital Branch

Announced February 2026

In Intesa Sanpaolo's Digital Branch, AI already resolved about 30% of client requests automatically in 2025. The bank plans to raise that to over 80% by 2029.

What it is

The figures were published in the bank's 2026-2029 Business Plan on 2 February 2026. The plan presentation reported about 18 million Digital Branch contacts in 2025. Of those, 4 to 5 million were handled by a virtual agent with accuracy above 90%. Every human-handled contact is supported by a generative AI copilot.

How it works

A virtual agent answers and resolves client requests directly; where a person handles the contact, a generative AI copilot supports them. The plan extends the Digital Branch's capabilities and insources more of its operation.

Technology used

Generative AI on the bank's isytech cloud-native platform; the plan documents do not name the underlying models.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Automated resolutions of client requests were around 30% in 2025, per the bank's Business Plan press release; the bank targets over 80% by 2029 and an increase in productivity of over 20% in middle and back-office activities.

Source (Bank announcement): INTESA SANPAOLO: 2026-2029 Business Plan" (Intesa Sanpaolo press release, 2 February 2026)

Intesa redesigns processes "Agent-first" under its 2026-2029 Business Plan

Announced February 2026

Intesa Sanpaolo made AI a structural lever in its 2026-2029 cost plan, with an "Agent-first" redesign of its processes. The plan covers generative and agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own).

What it is

The Business Plan, approved and presented on 2 February 2026, lists "Artificial Intelligence (AI/GenAI and Agentic AI) evolution" as initiative 1.2 of its structural cost reduction programme. It also extends the isytech cloud platform to around 100% of applications by 2029.

How it works

The bank will evolve service models, redesign operational processes on an "Agent-first" basis, and strengthen risk and control oversight with AI. That includes the Digital Branch and AI-enabled, fully automated credit processes for SME clients.

Technology used

isytech, the bank's cloud-native platform (built with Google Cloud and TIM); specific AI models are not named in the press release.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The bank targets cost savings of €1.6 billion in 2026-2029 from its overall structural cost programme and an increase in productivity of over 20% in middle/back-office activities from the AI initiative; these are plan targets, not reported results.

Source (Bank announcement): INTESA SANPAOLO: 2026-2029 Business Plan" (Intesa Sanpaolo press release, 2 February 2026)
