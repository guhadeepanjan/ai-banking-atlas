AI in Contact Centre

As of November 2026, 50 of the world’s top 100 banks have 56 AI deployments in contact centre tracked by Banking on AI. AI in customer service: voice and chat agents, call transcription and agent assistance. Every deployment below is explained in plain language, with its status and the source behind it.

56 deployments50 banks31 with a measured result

Common questions

Which banks use AI in contact centre?

The banks with deployments tracked here are: JPMorgan Chase, China Construction Bank, Agricultural Bank of China, Bank of China, ICBC, Bank of America, HSBC, Morgan Stanley, Royal Bank of Canada, Wells Fargo, Mitsubishi UFJ Financial, Toronto-Dominion Bank, and 38 more. The most active are Santander, SEB (Skandinaviska Enskilda Banken), PKO Bank Polski.

How many AI deployments in contact centre have been tracked?

56 deployments across 50 banks, announced between December 2024 and November 2026. 31 of them report a measured result; the rest state an aim or no benefit, and this page says which.

What is the latest AI development in contact centre?

AI Agent Assist for credit card calls at Bank of Montreal (November 2026, status: Rolling out). BMO introduced AI Agent Assist in its Virtual Connect contact centre to help staff resolve credit card calls faster. The AI handles transaction matching, policy retrieval and case preparation.

How does Banking on AI check these deployments?

Every deployment is traced to an opened source, labelled as a bank announcement or independent reporting, and given a status: Live, Rolling out or Announced. Benefits are reported only where the source states them.
