Corporate Banking

LiveCorporate BankingNew

Apply AI across the entire credit approval process

Announced March 2026

CCB applies AI across its whole credit approval process under an "AI-assisted plus expert decision-making" model. In 2025 it served over 113,000 corporate customers. Average net approval time fell by over 30%.

What it is

The programme embeds AI in every stage of corporate credit approval at CCB. The bank calls it a flagship project in its first batch of digital risk control initiatives.

How it works

Multimodal financial large models draw on a credit approval knowledge base of public and proprietary data. They distil expert know-how from historical cases into chains of thought. The models draft rating review opinions and review documents for staff to polish.

Technology used

Multimodal financial large models, with a credit approval knowledge base built from public and proprietary data.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The project generates over 50,000 documents a month, with a generation rate of up to 90%. It cut the average net approval period by over 30% in 2025.

Source (Independent reporting): China Construction Bank Corporation Annual Report 2025" (China Construction Bank, March 2026)

CCB applied large models, video-enabled customer service and digital avatars across its Remote Intelligent Banking Centre, its remote customer service operation. The centre served over 400 million customer inquiries across all channels in 2025.

How it works

The report describes remote, multimedia financial services delivered anywhere and anytime, with large models and digital avatars among the technologies applied, alongside the "CCB Customer Service" service account. It does not describe the systems in more technical detail.

Technology used

Large models, video-enabled customer service and digital avatars, as described in the source. No model or vendor is named.

Benefit reported (Measured result)
