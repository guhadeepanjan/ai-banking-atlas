AI in Trade Finance

As of November 2026, 12 of the world’s top 100 banks have 12 AI deployments in trade finance tracked by Banking on AI. AI in trade finance: document checking, compliance screening and trade processing. Every deployment below is explained in plain language, with its status and the source behind it.

Common questions

Which banks use AI in trade finance?

The banks with deployments tracked here are: HSBC, DBS Group, HDFC Bank, State Bank of India, Mizuho Financial Group, Lloyds Banking Group, First Abu Dhabi Bank, Deutsche Bank, NatWest Group, Standard Chartered, Crédit Agricole, Maybank. The most active are DBS Group, Lloyds Banking Group, First Abu Dhabi Bank.

How many AI deployments in trade finance have been tracked?

12 deployments across 12 banks, announced between December 2024 and November 2026. 2 of them report a measured result; the rest state an aim or no benefit, and this page says which.

What is the latest AI development in trade finance?

Checks trade documents with generative AI at scale at DBS Group (November 2026, status: Live). DBS uses generative AI at scale to check trade documents, alongside compliance checks and credit memos. More than 70% of its trade finance business now starts digitally.

How does Banking on AI check these deployments?

Every deployment is traced to an opened source, labelled as a bank announcement or independent reporting, and given a status: Live, Rolling out or Announced. Benefits are reported only where the source states them.
