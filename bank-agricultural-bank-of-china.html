ABCClaw — the bank's own AI agent for lending due diligence

Announced March 2026

ABC launched its own AI agent, ABCClaw, to help relationship managers with lending work. It automates data handling and report writing in the loan process, starting with green projects.

What it is

In March 2026, ABC chairman Gu Shu revealed that the bank had launched its own AI agent, ABCClaw. China Daily Hong Kong reported it as one of two large state-owned Chinese banks rolling out their own versions of OpenClaw-style agents.

How it works

The system supports relationship managers by automatically processing data on green projects and cross-validating multidimensional information. It then intelligently generates due diligence reports.

Technology used

ABC's own AI agent, ABCClaw; the underlying model is not specified in the source.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

Large language models in customer service — intent detection, intelligent Q&A and voice routing

Announced March 2026

ABC used large language models to help its customer service robot understand complex questions and route hotline callers by voice. Callers reach the service they need with one voice command instead of keypad menus.

What it is

In its 2025 annual report, under "accelerating the development of digital and intelligent customer services," ABC said it established an enterprise-level knowledge base and developed intelligent Q&A capabilities. It also applied large language models to assist in intent detection for its customer service robot, and promoted an intelligent voice routing strategy for the customer service hotline.

How it works

The large model works out what the customer means (intent detection), which improves the robot's ability to analyse and understand complex queries. On the hotline, voice routing lets customers receive the desired service through a single voice command instead of keypad input.

Technology used

Large language models, an enterprise-level knowledge base and intelligent voice routing, as described in the source. No model or vendor is named.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. The same report section records service scale, 383 million customer contacts across all media in 2025, but does not attribute a measured benefit to the AI itself.

Source (Bank announcement): Agricultural Bank of China Limited Annual Report 2025" (Agricultural Bank of China, 30 March 2026), "Service Channels" section
