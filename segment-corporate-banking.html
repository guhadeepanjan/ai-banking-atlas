AI in Corporate Banking

As of November 2026, 30 of the world’s top 100 banks have 35 AI deployments in corporate banking tracked by Banking on AI. AI for business and corporate clients: lending, onboarding and relationship tools. Every deployment below is explained in plain language, with its status and the source behind it.

35 deployments30 banks15 with a measured result

Common questions

Which banks use AI in corporate banking?

The banks with deployments tracked here are: China Construction Bank, Agricultural Bank of China, ICBC, Morgan Stanley, Mitsubishi UFJ Financial, Toronto-Dominion Bank, Commonwealth Bank, China Merchants Bank, DBS Group, Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group, UniCredit, State Bank of India, and 18 more. The most active are China Merchants Bank, Agricultural Bank of China, Commonwealth Bank.

How many AI deployments in corporate banking have been tracked?

35 deployments across 30 banks, announced between December 2024 and November 2026. 15 of them report a measured result; the rest state an aim or no benefit, and this page says which.

What is the latest AI development in corporate banking?

Agentic AI in business lending at National Australia Bank (November 2026, status: Live). NAB uses agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own) in business lending, including tools that help bankers write credit memos. Business loan decisions can be prepared and approved faster.

How does Banking on AI check these deployments?

Every deployment is traced to an opened source, labelled as a bank announcement or independent reporting, and given a status: Live, Rolling out or Announced. Benefits are reported only where the source states them.
