AI in Private Banking

As of November 2026, 12 of the world’s top 100 banks have 15 verified AI deployments in private banking tracked by Banking on AI. AI for wealth clients and their advisers: research, portfolio insight and client service. Every deployment below is explained in plain language, with its status and the source behind it.

Common questions

Which banks use AI in private banking?

The banks with verified deployments tracked here are: JPMorgan Chase, HSBC, Morgan Stanley, Royal Bank of Canada, Wells Fargo, Citigroup, CIBC, OCBC Bank, Deutsche Bank, NatWest Group, Standard Chartered, Banco Santander Brasil. The most active are OCBC Bank, Citigroup, JPMorgan Chase.

How many AI deployments in private banking have been verified?

15 deployments across 12 banks, announced between December 2024 and November 2026. 9 of them report a measured result; the rest state an aim or no benefit, and this page says which.

What is the latest AI development in private banking?

Agentic AI solution for Source of Wealth KYC processes at Deutsche Bank (September 2026, status: Rolling out). Deutsche Bank Private Bank rolled out agentic AI (software that carries out tasks on its own) to automate the research and paperwork behind Source of Wealth checks, one of the slowest parts of onboarding.

How does Banking on AI verify these deployments?

Every deployment is traced to an opened source, labelled as a bank announcement or independent reporting, and given a status: Live, Rolling out or Announced. Benefits are reported only where the source states them.
