Corporate Banking

LiveCorporate Banking

Agentforce AI agents inside the business banking CRM

Announced February 2026

ANZ rolled out AI agents inside a new CRM for business and private bankers, the first Asia-Pacific bank to deploy Salesforce Agentforce at scale. Bankers now get one customer view, not 20 separate systems.

What it is

ANZ's new customer relationship management platform brings together data from 20 disparate systems into a single interface for business bankers and frontline teams, after a national roll-out. AI agents powered by Salesforce's Agentforce platform work inside the CRM.

How it works

The agents help bankers find leads, visualise customer insights and prioritise daily activities. Bankers can also call up real-time customer account summaries and use a chat interface to search customer data.

Technology used

Salesforce Agentforce, inside ANZ's new CRM platform.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

Real-time account summaries are expected to save bankers the equivalent of about one working month per year, according to ANZ as reported in the source. (This is the bank's expectation, not a measured result.)

Source (Independent reporting): ANZ rolls out AI agents for business bankers" (Computer Weekly, 6 February 2026)

Contact Centre

LiveContact Centre

AI transcribes and routes contact centre calls

Announced November 2026

ANZ uses AI in its contact centres to transcribe calls, write conversation summaries and route enquiries to the right team. Staff spend less time on after-call paperwork and more time on customers with complex problems.

What it is

ANZ's own November 2026 article "AI in action at ANZ" describes AI capabilities already in use in its Enterprise Contact Centre, one of the bank's three priority AI use cases. At its FY26 results the bank said it is re-platforming the contact centre experience.

How it works

The tools provide voice transcription, automated post-call summaries, note-taking, conversational insights and intelligent routing of enquiries, reducing manual administration and after-call work for contact centre colleagues.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (ANZ's FY26 results presentation said more than 90% of contact centre interactions are expected to be AI-assisted when the re-platforming is complete; that is a target, not a result.)

Source (Bank announcement): Behind the numbers: AI in action at ANZ" (ANZ Bluenotes, November 2026)
