BMO Assist chatbot upgraded with large language models

Announced November 2026

BMO upgraded its client chatbot, BMO Assist, with large language model technology. Conversations now feel more natural, and clients can do more themselves, including checking balances.

What it is

BMO Assist is the bank's client-facing chatbot. In November 2026 BMO said its LLM integration, its AI-enabled balance inquiry and its proactive guidance to update contact information had won 2026 OMNI Awards for AI innovation.

How it works

Clients chat with BMO Assist, and the LLM technology helps it understand requests and respond in a more natural way. It also handles conversational balance inquiries and prompts clients to keep their contact information up to date.

Technology used

Large language model technology integrated by BMO; the model provider is not disclosed in the source.

The source does not state a benefit.

AI Agent Assist for credit card calls

Announced November 2026

BMO introduced AI Agent Assist in its Virtual Connect contact centre to help staff resolve credit card calls faster. The AI handles transaction matching, policy retrieval and case preparation.

What it is

AI Agent Assist is an agentic AI capability for internal use by BMO's client service team, announced on 6 November 2026. It supports clients who contact the Virtual Connect contact centre about credit card transactions.

How it works

The tool brings relevant transaction information and guidance to the employee during the call and prepares the case for review. Employees stay accountable: they review the AI-generated information and make the servicing decisions, under a human-in-the-loop design.

Technology used

Agentic AI built by BMO; the underlying model provider is not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

BMO said it aims to cut the time to resolve eligible inquiries by 20 to 30 per cent and to process about 40 per cent of eligible credit refund requests automatically, so qualifying clients could receive a credit the same day; these are targets stated at launch, not measured results.

Source (Bank announcement): BMO Using Agentic AI to Help Credit Card Clients Get Faster Answers and Resolutions" (BMO newsroom, Canada NewsWire, 6 November 2026)

Lumi Assistant for frontline employees

Announced May 2025

BMO launched Lumi Assistant, a generative AI tool that gives frontline staff instant answers from more than 8,000 banking policies and procedures in English and French. Staff answer client questions on the spot.

What it is

Lumi Assistant was launched in May 2025 for BMO employees, starting in Canadian Personal and Business Banking. BMO announced in June 2025 that it had won two digital innovation awards. They included "Best Use of Gen AI for Customer Experience – Canada" at the 2025 Digital Banker CX Awards.

How it works

Employees ask questions in natural language and Lumi searches policies, procedures, job aids, operations guides and standard operating procedures, summarises long documents and gives actionable guidance in real time. The tool has no access to client information.

Technology used

Generative AI built by BMO; the bank has not disclosed the underlying model provider.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.
