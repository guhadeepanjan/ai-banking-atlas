OTP Bank Ukraine call-centre AI — voice robot, chatbot and full call analytics

Announced January 2025

OTP Bank Ukraine runs a voice robot and the Lesia chatbot in its call centre, and analyses every customer conversation with AI. Every call is transcribed and checked, replacing small manual quality samples.

What it is

OTP Bank Ukraine, the group's Ukrainian subsidiary, uses an Omilia voice robot and the Lesia chatbot with speech recognition for customer calls and chats. An LLM-based language analytics system from Ender Turing reviews the conversations.

How it works

According to call-centre head Dmytro Gramatik, the analytics system transcribes and analyses all customer conversations. It covers quality control, automatic feedback to agents, real-time anomaly detection and sales analytics.

Technology used

Omilia voice robot; Lesia chatbot; Ender Turing LLM-based language analytics.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Source (Bank announcement): OTP Bank Ukraine call centre AI" (OTP Bank Ukraine, own website, 21 January 2025)

AI runs across lending, service channels and fraud prevention

Announced May 2026

OTP's CEO reported that machine learning models make 80% to 90% of the bank's consumer loan decisions automatically. AI also runs at scale in customer service and fraud prevention across the group.

What it is

In a Global Finance interview published in May 2026, CEO Péter Csányi described the group's live AI use. It covers automated credit assessment, AI-based intent recognition across chat and voice channels, a recommendation engine, and a proprietary deep-learning fraud prevention model. Micro and SME pre-approvals are also supported by machine learning.

How it works

Machine learning models assess consumer credit applications automatically in most cases, while intent recognition routes and supports customer conversations. The fraud model works in real time to protect transactions.

Technology used

Machine learning models and a proprietary deep-learning fraud model; no external vendor is named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Automated credit assessment covers 80% to 90% of consumer loan decisions, as stated by the CEO in the source; no money or time saving is given.

Source (Independent reporting): OTP Bank: Becoming AI and Data Literate" (Global Finance, interview with CEO Péter Csányi, published 8 May 2026)
