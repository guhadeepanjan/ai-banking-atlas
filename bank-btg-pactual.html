AI-led lending platform for SMEs (BTG Pactual Empresas)

Announced September 2026

BTG Pactual Empresas, the bank's SME platform, uses an AI-led lending platform that can approve loans instantly. Small businesses get credit decisions and money in minutes, instead of waiting days for manual reviews.

What it is

The Digital Banker profiled BTG Pactual Empresas' digital ecosystem for small and medium-sized companies, covering about 260,000 SME clients at the end of the third quarter of 2025. Its credit infrastructure digitises the whole SME lending journey, from application to disbursement.

How it works

The AI-led platform assesses applications and auto-approves loans instantly. The surrounding process, including digital onboarding, payments and collections, is also automated through the platform and its APIs.

Technology used

The bank's AI-led lending platform within the BTG Pactual Empresas digital ecosystem; specific models are not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

96% of SME loan disbursements were completed within 10 minutes, roughly 16 times faster than traditional banks, according to The Digital Banker.

Source (Independent reporting): BTG Pactual is at the forefront of South America's SME banking evolution" (The Digital Banker, 1 September 2026)

AI Virtual Assistant on WhatsApp for Pix payments

Announced February 2025

BTG Pactual's AI assistant inside WhatsApp lets customers make Pix transfers and pay bills by text, voice or photo. Everyday payments happen inside a chat app customers already use.

What it is

The bank announced the Virtual Assistant on 17 February 2025. Customers can make Pix transfers, schedule and make payments, and check balances and open bills through WhatsApp messages.

How it works

The customer sends a text, audio or image message. The assistant can split a bill between people, interpret text, and read amounts from photos of documents or even handwritten notes. At launch, the daily transaction limit was R$500, adjustable by the customer in the BTG Banking app.

Technology used

Artificial intelligence on WhatsApp; the underlying model or vendor is not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Source (Independent reporting): BTG Pactual lança assistente virtual baseado em IA para o WhatsApp" (IT Forum, 19 February 2025)

Knowledge Assistant and AI tools support customer service teams

Announced September 2026

BTG Pactual uses AI tools in customer service, including a Knowledge Assistant that helps agents answer customer queries accurately. Agents resolve more questions on first contact, and automated summaries cut the paperwork after each case.

What it is

The Digital Banker's profile of BTG Pactual's SME banking operation describes the AI tools supporting its customer service. They include a Knowledge Assistant for query handling, automated case summaries and data entry for agents, and virtual assistants for faster responses.

How it works

The Knowledge Assistant improves how agents find and use information when handling queries. Automated case summaries and data entry simplify agent workflows, reducing errors and improving accuracy.

Technology used

The bank's internal AI tools, including the Knowledge Assistant; specific models are not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

The source states the tools increased first-contact resolution and customer satisfaction and reduced average handling time, but gives no figures.

Source (Independent reporting): BTG Pactual is at the forefront of South America's SME banking evolution" (The Digital Banker, 1 September 2026)
