AI in Payments & Remittances

As of November 2026, 21 of the world’s top 100 banks have 23 AI deployments in payments & remittances tracked by Banking on AI. AI in payments: fraud checks, routing, reconciliation and cross-border transfers. Every deployment below is explained in plain language, with its status and the source behind it.

23 deployments21 banks5 with a measured result

Common questions

Which banks use AI in payments & remittances?

The banks with deployments tracked here are: JPMorgan Chase, Bank of China, Bank of America, HSBC, Mitsubishi UFJ Financial, Commonwealth Bank, Santander, DBS Group, HDFC Bank, State Bank of India, Scotiabank, BNY Mellon, and 9 more. The most active are Santander, Danske Bank, Bank Hapoalim.

How many AI deployments in payments & remittances have been tracked?

23 deployments across 21 banks, announced between December 2024 and November 2026. 5 of them report a measured result; the rest state an aim or no benefit, and this page says which.

What is the latest AI development in payments & remittances?

First AI agent card payment in Denmark with Mastercard Agent Pay at Danske Bank (September 2026, status: Announced). An AI agent paid for a booking with a Danske Bank Mastercard, in Denmark's first AI agent card payment. The agent completed a real purchase on live card rails, not in a test environment.

How does Banking on AI check these deployments?

Every deployment is traced to an opened source, labelled as a bank announcement or independent reporting, and given a status: Live, Rolling out or Announced. Benefits are reported only where the source states them.
