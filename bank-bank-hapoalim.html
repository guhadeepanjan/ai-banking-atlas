Project Sentinel — AI agent for incoming foreign transfers

Announced September 2026

Project Sentinel, an AI agent, checks incoming foreign transfers every 30 minutes and recommends approving, rejecting or escalating each one. Over 90% of transfers are handled automatically, each with an audit trail.

What it is

The agent reviews each incoming foreign transfer against operational and risk rules, using a 360-degree view of the customer. It also applies the labels needed for regulatory reporting.

How it works

Every 30 minutes the agent evaluates new transfers and issues a recommendation with a confidence score, the evidence and its reasoning. Approved instructions are passed to robotic process automation for execution, and every recommendation keeps an audit trail.

Technology used

Built on Dataiku, with robotic process automation (RPA) for execution, as described in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The agent handles more than 90% of incoming foreign transfers automatically, and handling time fell from about 6 minutes to about 2 minutes per transfer, three times faster, as reported in the source.

Source (Independent reporting): Bank Hapoalim — Project Sentinel" (Dataiku blog, 21 September 2026)
