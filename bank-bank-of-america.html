Erica passes three billion client interactions

Announced August 2025

Bank of America's virtual assistant Erica has handled more than three billion client interactions since 2018. Nearly 50 million people have used it. It now averages over 58 million interactions a month.

What it is

Erica is the bank's AI-driven virtual financial assistant, launched in 2018 and built into its digital banking services. Clients use it to manage spending, budgeting and savings, and to receive proactive, personalised insights. It has delivered more than 1.7 billion of those insights so far.

How it works

Clients ask questions in everyday language, and Erica recognises and responds to millions of possible questions. Its answers draw on a library of more than 700 responses, refined through over 75,000 updates since launch. More than 98% of users find the information they need, which reduces call centre volume.

Technology used

Erica, Bank of America's in-house AI virtual assistant, trained by its data scientists on a library of more than 700 responses.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

More than 98% of users find the information they need, and clients have spent over 18.7 million hours conversing with Erica. The bank says this significantly decreases call centre volume.

Source (Bank announcement): A Decade of AI Innovation: BofA's Virtual Assistant Erica Surpasses 3 Billion Client Interactions" (Bank of America via PR Newswire, 20 August 2025)

CashPro launches Payments Insights for treasury teams

Announced September 2026

Bank of America has added Payments Insights to CashPro, its platform for corporate clients. The tool gives treasury teams intelligence on payment efficiency, cross-border flows and working capital on their US accounts.

What it is

Payments Insights is a new capability in CashPro Data Intelligence, the bank's AI-powered suite of treasury analytics. It is aimed at corporate and commercial customers operating US accounts.

How it works

The tool organises a client's payment and foreign exchange data and presents it in a form finance teams can act on. It highlights how payments are processed and settled, how money moves across borders, and how working capital performs.

Technology used

CashPro Data Intelligence, Bank of America's AI-powered analytics suite within the CashPro platform.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

AI-driven CashPro Chat and CashPro Search

Announced October 2025

Bank of America's AI-driven CashPro Chat use rose 21% year over year, with nearly 70% of corporate clients using it. Treasurers get account and transaction answers in under 30 seconds, without phoning or emailing.

What it is

CashPro is the platform companies use to manage and monitor payments, deposits, loans and trade transactions. CashPro Chat gives real-time account information, transaction tracking and service resolution, and CashPro Search validates payment status across wires, ACH, RTP, FX and loan proceeds.

How it works

Chat provides 24/7 support with intelligent advisor routing to live experts available 24/5, with average response times under 30 seconds. Search routes inquiries to service teams with real-time tracking and visibility.

Technology used

AI-driven CashPro Chat and transaction search inside the CashPro platform; the source does not name the specific model.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

CashPro Chat use increased 21% year-over-year with nearly 70% of corporate clients using it, a Q3 2025 containment rate of 43%, and CashPro Search surpassed 18 million searches including nearly 2.4 million in Q3 2025, a quarterly record; Search reduced phone and email inquiries by 20% among early adopters.

Source (Bank announcement): BofA's AI-Driven CashPro Chat and Transaction Search Capabilities Reach Record Usage by Corporate Clients" (PR Newswire, 27 October 2025)

Upgrade the AI model behind CashPro Forecasting

Announced April 2025

Bank of America upgraded the AI model inside CashPro Forecasting, its cash forecasting tool for corporate clients. The new model reads account data five times faster, helping treasurers react sooner to market moves.

What it is

CashPro Forecasting sits inside CashPro, the platform clients use for treasury, trade and cross-border payments. It pulls account data in automatically and forecasts global cash positions from one day to one year ahead.

How it works

Machine learning models learn from each client's historical cash flows and generate forecasts within minutes. The April 2025 upgrade lets the model process and interpret data five times faster than before.

Technology used

Bank of America's proprietary machine learning models within CashPro Forecasting, part of the CashPro platform.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

In 2025, more than 3,000 companies saved over 250,000 hours using the tool, according to the bank.

Source (Bank announcement): BofA's AI Solution CashPro Forecasting Helps Clients Navigate Year of Volatility" (Bank of America via PR Newswire, 11 December 2025)

Ask Global Payments Solutions (AskGPS) — GenAI assistant for Global Payments Solutions

Announced September 2025

Bank of America launched AskGPS, a generative AI assistant for staff serving over 40,000 business clients worldwide. Complex client questions that once took an hour of calls across time zones can be answered in seconds.

What it is

AskGPS is a newly launched, in-house GenAI assistant for the Global Payments Solutions (GPS) team. Employees can pose simple to complex client questions and receive answers within seconds.

How it works

The assistant was trained on more than 3,200 internal documents and presentations, including product guides, term sheets and frequently asked questions. It turns that institutional knowledge into real-time answers for staff, who then serve the client.

Technology used

Generative AI / large language model built in-house by Bank of America; external model not named in the source.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

The bank expected it to save tens of thousands of employee hours annually; a sophisticated inquiry that previously took an employee an hour is now almost instant, with answers in seconds.

Source (Bank announcement): BofA's New GenAI Assistant Transforms Global Payments Solutions" (PR Newswire, September 2025)

EricaAssist enhanced with generative AI for customer service representatives

Announced July 2026

Bank of America added generative AI to EricaAssist, which works alongside its customer service representatives during client calls. Over 18,000 representatives use it, getting guidance in under three seconds without interrupting the conversation.

What it is

EricaAssist is a human-assisted AI agent for the bank's customer service employees. The July 2026 enhancement added generative AI capabilities that summarise why a client is calling and pull together relevant information. The capabilities also recommend next steps based on the employee's role and the client's relationship with the bank.

How it works

EricaAssist works alongside the representative during a live call, summarising and surfacing relevant guidance in real time. The employee stays at the centre of the experience and makes the decisions.

Technology used

Bank of America's EricaAssist AI agent with new generative AI capabilities. The source does not name an outside vendor or model.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The bank said EricaAssist reduces average call times by nearly one minute per interaction, and the new generative AI capabilities deliver contextual guidance in under three seconds. More than 18,000 customer service representatives use EricaAssist.

Source (Bank announcement): Bank of America Enhances EricaAssist with Generative AI to Help Employees Resolve Client Needs Faster" (Bank of America press release via PR Newswire, 21 July 2026)

Erica for Employees — AI adoption by the global workforce

Announced April 2025

Over 90% of Bank of America's employees use its internal AI assistant, Erica for Employees. A tool first built for customers now handles everyday staff tasks across a workforce of 213,000.

What it is

Erica for Employees is an AI-driven internal virtual assistant, launched in 2020 and expanded in 2023. It helps staff with tasks such as mobile device password reset, device activation, health benefits, and locating payroll and tax forms.

How it works

Employees ask the assistant for technology and workplace support instead of calling the IT service desk. The bank said its capabilities would expand in 2025 to broader search and assistance, with plans to leverage both AI and generative AI.

Technology used

The Erica platform; the source does not name the specific model.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Over 90% of employees use Erica for Employees, and it reduced calls into the IT service desk by more than 50%; in the same release, developers using a GenAI coding tool reported efficiency gains of over 20%, and Erica for clients had passed 2.5 billion interactions with 20 million clients actively using it.

Source (Bank announcement): AI Adoption by BofA's Global Workforce Improves Productivity, Client Service" (Bank of America press release, April 2025)
