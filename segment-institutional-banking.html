AI in Institutional Banking

As of November 2026, 11 of the world’s top 100 banks have 13 AI deployments in institutional banking tracked by Banking on AI. AI in markets and institutional services: trading support, research and post-trade work. Every deployment below is explained in plain language, with its status and the source behind it.

Common questions

Which banks use AI in institutional banking?

The banks with deployments tracked here are: Morgan Stanley, Royal Bank of Canada, Goldman Sachs, Toronto-Dominion Bank, DBS Group, UBS, BNP Paribas, Barclays, Deutsche Bank, Société Générale, State Street. The most active are Barclays, Goldman Sachs, BNP Paribas.

How many AI deployments in institutional banking have been tracked?

13 deployments across 11 banks, announced between December 2024 and November 2026. 6 of them report a measured result; the rest state an aim or no benefit, and this page says which.

What is the latest AI development in institutional banking?

Claude models triaging Global Markets client emails at Barclays (October 2026, status: Live). Barclays' Global Markets business uses Claude models to read, classify and route about 120,000 client emails a day. Client requests reach the right operations team faster, with less manual sorting by staff.

How does Banking on AI check these deployments?

Every deployment is traced to an opened source, labelled as a bank announcement or independent reporting, and given a status: Live, Rolling out or Announced. Benefits are reported only where the source states them.
