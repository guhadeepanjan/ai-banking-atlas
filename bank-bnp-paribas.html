HelloïZ assistant with Genius bar, rolled out to over one million Hello bank! clients

Announced January 2026

Hello bank!'s assistant HelloïZ reached its whole client base of more than one million in January 2026. Customers get personalised answers in seconds and can complete many everyday transactions without waiting for a human adviser.

What it is

Genius bar is BNP Paribas' generative AI assistant platform, embedded in HelloïZ for Hello bank! and being rolled out through the Telmi assistant for BNP Paribas clients. It began with a 10,000-client Hello bank! pilot in June 2025 and covered all Hello bank! clients by January 2026. The bank confirmed the rollout in its May 2026 Mistral AI partnership release.

How it works

The platform combines large language models, retrieval-augmented generation and conversational AI, hosted on the bank's own infrastructure. It answers questions on daily banking, products, offers and pricing, and recognises transaction intents customers can complete on their own. It routes the conversation to Client Service when a human is needed.

Technology used

Mistral AI large language models on BNP Paribas' own infrastructure, with Giskard supporting AI security and testing.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The Digital Banker reported an average response time of under seven seconds, 84,600 conversations a month by May 2026, and the share of conversations escalated to Client Service falling from 66% in May 2025 to 38% in May 2026.

Agentic AI in Corporate & Institutional Banking with Google Cloud, and Gemini in LLM@CIB

Announced September 2026

BNP Paribas signed a five-year Google Cloud partnership to deploy AI agents across the bank, starting in Corporate & Institutional Banking. Gemini models will also power LLM@CIB, already used by more than 65,000 CIB staff.

What it is

The partnership was announced on 24 September 2026. It expands the bank's access to Google Cloud's AI-optimised infrastructure, Gemini models and Gemini Enterprise as the bank moves towards broader deployment of agentic AI.

How it works

With Gemini Enterprise, the bank can build, evaluate and deploy purpose-built agents for specific business needs, starting in CIB. Gemini models will be integrated into LLM@CIB, its internal generative AI assistant. Google Cloud technology had already been deployed at Nickel, the bank's payment account business.

Technology used

Google Cloud; Gemini models; Gemini Enterprise; BNP Paribas' LLM@CIB assistant.

LLM as a Service platform

Announced June 2025

BNP Paribas deployed its internal "LLM as a Service" platform, giving every group entity secure access to large language models from the bank's own data centres. Dozens of businesses build on one shared foundation.

What it is

The platform was announced on 5 June 2025 and is operated by the Group's IT teams. It was first deployed in a test phase at entities including Hello bank! and the Group's general inspection, with progressive rollout across the Group.

How it works

Each entity connects through a standardised, secure interface to a selection of models suited to its needs. The platform runs on shared GPU-equipped infrastructure hosted in the Group's own data centres.

Technology used

Open-source models and models from Mistral AI, with models trained on internal datasets to follow; GPU infrastructure in BNP Paribas data centres.
