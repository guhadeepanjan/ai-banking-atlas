AI in Enterprise-wide

As of November 2026, 74 of the world’s top 100 banks have 111 AI deployments in enterprise-wide tracked by Banking on AI. AI platforms and programmes that span the whole bank rather than one business line. Every deployment below is explained in plain language, with its status and the source behind it.

111 deployments74 banks32 with a measured result

Common questions

Which banks use AI in enterprise-wide?

The banks with deployments tracked here are: JPMorgan Chase, China Construction Bank, Agricultural Bank of China, Bank of China, ICBC, Bank of America, HSBC, Morgan Stanley, Royal Bank of Canada, Wells Fargo, Goldman Sachs, Mitsubishi UFJ Financial, and 62 more. The most active are Bank of China, UOB, Commonwealth Bank.

How many AI deployments in enterprise-wide have been tracked?

111 deployments across 74 banks, announced between December 2024 and November 2026. 32 of them report a measured result; the rest state an aim or no benefit, and this page says which.

What is the latest AI development in enterprise-wide?

Ruby — staff AI assistant built on Anthropic at National Australia Bank (November 2026, status: Live). NAB has deployed an AI assistant called Ruby to more than 40,000 staff for everyday productivity. It gives every colleague the same AI tool at once, instead of scattered pilots.

How does Banking on AI check these deployments?

Every deployment is traced to an opened source, labelled as a bank announcement or independent reporting, and given a status: Live, Rolling out or Announced. Benefits are reported only where the source states them.
