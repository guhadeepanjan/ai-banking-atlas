"Fahad" — conversational AI avatar in branches (with NCR Atleos)

Announced November 2025

Fahad is a life-like AI video avatar on kiosks in KFH branches that answers customers' questions. KFH describes it as Kuwait's first AI-powered virtual employee, handling routine branch questions without staff.

What it is

KFH announced Fahad with NCR Atleos on 4 November 2025. The hyper-realistic avatar sits on account-service kiosks in branches and answers frequently asked questions; a second phase is planned to guide customers through transactions, with escalation to live agents. The same programme includes AI speech analytics and automated quality management.

How it works

The avatar converses with customers using conversational AI on the kiosk. The vendor release states plainly that the solution is a field test and is not yet available as a commercial product.

Technology used

NCR Atleos conversational AI avatar technology.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

RiskGPT — in-house AI engine for credit risk ratings

Announced December 2024

KFH's in-house RiskGPT engine produces a dynamic risk rating for a credit case in under an hour. Ratings previously took three to five days on average. Managers can also advise customers on improving their scores.

What it is

RiskGPT was built by KFH with its research and development institute and described in a Microsoft customer story published on 3 December 2024. It is used by the bank's risk team, which previously assessed customers only on historical data. The team can now also forecast a customer's future risk profile, including financials and cash flow.

How it works

The engine runs on Microsoft Azure AI services and Microsoft Fabric, with Microsoft 365 Copilot supporting staff work. Data uploaded through Fabric appears instantly in Power BI, replacing reporting that used to take two weeks to reconcile.

Technology used

Microsoft Azure AI services, Microsoft Fabric, Microsoft 365 Copilot and Power BI.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The story said KFH cut its risk processing time by 96%, producing a dynamic risk rating in under an hour instead of an average of three to five days.

Source (Independent reporting): Kuwait Finance House cuts risk processing time by 96% with Microsoft AI solutions" (Microsoft Customer Stories, 3 December 2024)
