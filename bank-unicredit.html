Buddy digital branch powered by GenAI to support advisors (Italy)

Announced July 2026 (reported in UniCredit's 2Q26 results presentation, Milan, 23 July 2026)

UniCredit's Italian digital branch, Buddy, is powered by generative AI that supports its human advisors. It puts GenAI directly into day-to-day retail client service in the bank's largest market.

What it is

In its 2Q26 results presentation, UniCredit highlights Italy under the heading "Buddy boosted by AI & new tech." Buddy is described as an "Unlimited Digital branch, powered by Gen-AI to support advisors." The same presentation notes that Italy's credit process has been automated, improving business efficiency and reducing time-to-yes.

How it works

Generative AI sits inside the Buddy digital branch and assists the advisors who serve clients through it. It runs alongside wider AI use in Italy's credit lifecycle, where the credit process is automated to cut the time to a lending decision. UniCredit's 1Q26 presentation described the Buddy GenAI use case as supporting advisors by enhancing the speed, consistency and quality of service.

DealSync — AI-powered M&A matching platform for SMEs

Announced October 2026 (Serbia launch reported then; the platform was already operating in Italy, Germany and Austria, and had launched in Croatia earlier in 2026)

DealSync uses AI to match small and mid-sized companies with international investors, buyers and strategic partners. It opens M&A advice to SMEs, which have traditionally had little access to broad buyer and investor networks.

What it is

DealSync is an AI-powered mergers and acquisitions platform launched by UniCredit Bank Serbia in October 2026, described by the bank as the first of its kind in Serbia. It connects domestic companies with international investors, buyers and strategic partners. The same platform was already available in Italy, Germany and Austria, and in Croatia through Zagrebačka Banka.

How it works

The platform combines advanced AI tools with the expertise of UniCredit's M&A specialists. Together they identify the most suitable potential partners for each transaction.

AI voice assistant "Mia" in the Croatian contact centre (Zagrebačka banka)

Announced May 2026

UniCredit's Croatian bank, Zagrebačka banka, runs an AI voice assistant in its contact centre that answers common calls and routes the rest. UniCredit's own reporting says 30% of its Croatian contact-centre calls are now AI-managed.

What it is

The assistant, called Mia, is the first enterprise voice assistant deployed in Croatian. UniCredit disclosed the 30% figure in its first-quarter 2026 reporting on 5 May 2026; the deployment itself is documented in the vendor's customer case study.

How it works

Mia answers frequently asked questions (card activation, card replacement, adding a card to a digital wallet, online banking support). For call types it does not automate, it asks clarifying questions and routes the caller straight to the right department.

Technology used

PolyAI voice assistant platform.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

UniCredit reports 30% of Croatian contact-centre calls are AI-managed. The vendor case study adds that Mia automates 27% of all calls, routes callers 83% faster than the previous phone menu, cut the call abandonment rate by 10%, and lifted the bank's NPS by 14 points in the first six months.

Google Cloud 10-year AI platform partnership (Vertex AI & Gemini)

Announced May 2025 (press release 12 May 2025)

UniCredit signed a 10-year deal making Google Cloud its platform for all AI workloads across its 13 markets. Every later AI tool at the bank is meant to run on this one shared platform.

What it is

UniCredit and Google Cloud signed a Memorandum of Understanding for a 10-year agreement to speed up the bank's digital transformation. Large parts of the bank's application landscape, including legacy systems, will gradually move to Google Cloud, and UniCredit's 13 banks will progressively transition onto this single foundation.

How it works

UniCredit will use Google Cloud as its new platform for AI workloads, using its infrastructure, AI and data analytics tools. Planned uses named in the release include investment banking insights, better customer interactions, operational process optimisation, financial crime prevention and new AI-powered financial products. Google Cloud will also provide digital skills training to UniCredit employees across its 13 European markets.

Technology used

Google Cloud, Vertex AI platform and Gemini models.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (The release describes planned uses and expected improvements, with no measured figures.)

Source (Bank announcement): "UniCredit Partners with Google Cloud to Accelerate Digital Transformation Across 13 Markets" — UniCredit press release, 12 May 2025 · Second source
