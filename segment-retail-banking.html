AI in Retail Banking

As of November 2026, 32 of the world’s top 100 banks have 39 verified AI deployments in retail banking tracked by Banking on AI. AI aimed at everyday customers: apps, onboarding, lending and personal finance tools. Every deployment below is explained in plain language, with its status and the source behind it.

39 deployments32 banks15 with a measured result

Common questions

Which banks use AI in retail banking?

The banks with verified deployments tracked here are: China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Mitsubishi UFJ Financial, Toronto-Dominion Bank, DBS Group, Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group, HDFC Bank, BBVA, UniCredit, Bank of Montreal, and 20 more. The most active are Mitsubishi UFJ Financial, Swedbank, HDFC Bank.

How many AI deployments in retail banking have been verified?

39 deployments across 32 banks, announced between December 2024 and November 2026. 15 of them report a measured result; the rest state an aim or no benefit, and this page says which.

What is the latest AI development in retail banking?

BMO Assist chatbot upgraded with large language models at Bank of Montreal (November 2026, status: Live). BMO upgraded its client chatbot, BMO Assist, with large language model technology. Conversations now feel more natural, and clients can do more themselves, including checking balances.

How does Banking on AI verify these deployments?

Every deployment is traced to an opened source, labelled as a bank announcement or independent reporting, and given a status: Live, Rolling out or Announced. Benefits are reported only where the source states them.
