Unveils Citi Sky, an AI avatar for wealth clients

Announced April 2026

Citi has unveiled Citi Sky, an AI avatar that holds voice and video conversations with wealth clients about their money. It is in testing with selected clients ahead of a phased U.S. rollout.

What it is

Citi Sky gives Citigold clients an always-on conversational adviser sitting alongside their human wealth team. It surfaces market insights from Citi Wealth's Chief Investment Office and flags items such as certificates of deposit nearing maturity.

How it works

Clients speak to a real-time avatar in English or Spanish and ask questions about their finances. The avatar answers using live audio and video models, drawing on the bank's investment views and client data. Human advisers remain in place, and Citi says the tool extends their reach rather than replacing them.

Technology used

Google's Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, with Google DeepMind real-time avatar and live audio and video models.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Advisor Insights pilot and AskWealth — Citi Wealth

Announced August 2025

Citi Wealth launched two AI tools to improve how bankers and advisors communicate with clients. AskWealth went global after a launch in Asia; Advisor Insights started as a North America pilot.

What it is

Advisor Insights is a tailored dashboard for bankers and advisors with timely messages about market turns, portfolios and current events, including insights from Citi Wealth's Chief Investment Office. AskWealth is a GenAI-powered conversational assistant giving service teams, advisors and managers instant answers across the Wealth business.

How it works

Advisors use the dashboard alerts to have faster, deeper client conversations around useful content. With AskWealth, service teams can resolve client inquiries and advisors can access timely market insights and research to answer questions about clients and portfolios. Advisor Insights was in pilot with Citigold and Citi Private Client North America advisors. The release planned expansion to North America Citi Private Bankers in Q4 2025 and international colleagues in Q1 2026.

Technology used

AskWealth powered by Meta's Llama large language model suite, shifting to Google's Gemini (per CIO Dive, from Citi's Head of Data, Analytics & Innovation); generative AI also embedded in Advisor Insights. The Citi press release itself does not name the models.

Benefit reported (Bank-stated aim)

Citi states in its press release that the platforms "will save hours of time for our advisors, bankers and service teams", but no specific number of hours is given.

Combine FalconTST 2.0 with the Fixed FX Rates solution

Announced August 2026

Citi combined Ant International's FalconTST 2.0 forecasting model with its Fixed FX Rates solution. The pairing aims to give clients more predictable currency costs when they convert funds.

What it is

Fixed FX Rates is Citi's solution for clients who need certainty over conversion rates. FalconTST 2.0 adds AI forecasting of cash flow and currency exposure to that offer.

How it works

The model studies historical data to predict future cash flows and FX exposure. Those forecasts feed the rates Citi sets for clients under the Fixed FX Rates solution.

Technology used

Ant International's FalconTST 2.0 time-series transformer model, combined with Citi's Fixed FX Rates solution.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

Ant International reports forecast accuracy above 93% across FalconTST 2.0 deployments.

Source (Independent reporting): Ant International's FalconTST 2.0 hits top MASE score, adopted by Barclays, Citi, Deutsche Bank and Standard Chartered" (The Asian Banker, August 2026)

AI assists agents, runs the phone menu and flags complaints in US consumer cards call centres

Announced May 2026

Citi's US consumer cards call centres use a generative AI agent-assist tool and a generative AI-enabled phone menu (IVR). Citi reported the assist tool cutting average call handle time by about 60 seconds.

What it is

Citi set out the tools in its 2026 Investor Day US Consumer Cards presentation (7 May 2026). They are a "Gen AI Agent Assist tool for call center agents", a "Gen AI-enabled IVR", and generative AI complaint detection.

How it works

The agent-assist tool supports the agent during the call, and the AI-enabled IVR handles the opening phone interaction with the customer. Complaint detection flags customer complaints automatically.

Technology used

Generative AI; no vendor or model named in the source.

Benefit reported (Measured result)

The deck states the agent-assist tool gives "60 seconds lower call handle time" and complaint detection gives a ">95% reduction in customer impact detection time". It also states "up to 40% targeted productivity gains in time reduction" — that figure is a target set by the bank, not a measured result.

Source (Bank announcement): Citi 2026 Investor Day — US Consumer Cards presentation" (Citigroup, 7 May 2026)

CitiService Agent Assist helps Services business agents answer client queries

Announced June 2025

CitiService Agent Assist is a generative AI tool that helps Citi's customer service agents answer client questions faster and more accurately. It is live for Citi service staff in 47 countries, not a small pilot.

What it is

The tool supports agents in Citi's Services business while they handle client queries. Citi announced it on 3 June 2025 when the solution won Pega's Innovation Award for financial institutions.

How it works

The generative AI helps the agent find and shape the right response while dealing with the client, so answers are quicker and more consistent. The agent stays in control of the reply the client receives.

Technology used

Generative AI, built with Pega (the award is Pega's; Citi's release does not name an underlying AI model).

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. The release says the tool gives faster, more accurate responses but publishes no figures.

Source (Bank announcement): Citi AI Solution Wins Innovation Award, Elevating Client Service" (Citigroup press release, 3 June 2025)

Launches Arc, its platform for building AI agents firmwide

Announced April 2026

Citi has launched Arc, a platform for building and scaling AI agents across every business line and geography. Developers get access first, with wider employee access to follow over time.

What it is

Arc is Citi's central platform for creating embedded AI agents for internal use cases. Citi says every agent built on Arc is monitored, auditable and governed under the same rigour applied to any critical system.

How it works

Developers build agents for tasks such as research, synthesis, client preparation and execution. The platform applies monitoring and controls centrally, so individual teams do not rebuild governance for each agent. Over time, agents will be added to everyday employee workflows across the firm.

Technology used

Citi's proprietary Arc agent platform; the coverage does not name the underlying models.

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit.

Citi Stylus Workspaces with Agentic AI

Announced September 2025

Citi upgraded its proprietary employee AI platform so one prompt can complete a multi-step task. The rollout began in September with thousands of employees, followed by staged expansion across the firm.

What it is

An enhanced version of Citi Stylus Workspaces that enables employees to tackle longer, more complex objectives with greater speed, efficiency and insight. It is designed to support research, extracting insights from vast datasets, and streamlining multi-stage workflows into a single automated process.

How it works

The platform integrates directly with Citi systems, linking to internal data and project management tools, the global employee directory, commonly used enterprise platforms, web search and analysis capabilities. Citi's example: it can identify the top five branded cards businesses in the U.S., distil their strategic goals, and translate the insights into Spanish, via only one prompt. Banking Dive reported the initial agentic rollout started with a pilot group of 5,000 workers.

Technology used

Proprietary Citi platform using Google's Gemini and Anthropic's Claude models (per a Citi spokesperson to Banking Dive / CIO Dive; the Citi press release itself does not name the models)

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (The press release describes greater speed and efficiency and less manual work, with no quantified figure.)

Source (Bank announcement): "Citi Unveils Citi Stylus Workspaces with Agentic AI, Turbocharging Productivity at Citi" — Citigroup press release, September 2025 · Second source

Citi Assist and Citi Stylus — initial firm-wide rollout

Announced December 2024

Citi gave two GenAI tools to about 140,000 employees in eight countries. It was the starting rollout that the later Stylus Workspaces platform was built on.

What it is

Citi Assist helps staff find and navigate internal bank policies and procedures across HR, risk, compliance and finance. Citi Stylus is a document-intelligence tool for working with documents; Tim Ryan described Citi Assist as "like having a super-smart coworker at your fingertips."

How it works

Employees ask Citi Assist about commonly used policies and procedures instead of searching manuals themselves. Citi Stylus can summarise, compare or search multiple documents at the same time.

Technology used

The source does not name the technology. (The tools are Citi's own.)

Benefit reported (Not stated)

The source does not state a benefit. (The tools were said to be meant to simplify work and increase productivity; no quantified saving is given.)

Source (Independent reporting): "Citigroup rolls out artificial intelligence tools for employees in eight countries" — Reuters, December 2024, via ET HRWorld · Second source
